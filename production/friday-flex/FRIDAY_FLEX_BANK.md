# Friday Flex Bank — 8.5-Week Track

> Friday = RECOVER / PRACTICE / EXTEND only. **Never essential first exposure.**
> Legend: **RECOVER** = remediation/catch-up · **PRACTICE** = reinforce taught content · **EXTEND** = enrichment.
> Timing: **QUICK** 8–12 min · **STANDARD** 15–20 min · **FULL FLEX** 25–30 min.
> All options are absent-student-appropriate unless marked otherwise.

## Notes
- **Cookie mining** is a FLEX-only simplified model (chips extracted, before/after sketch, qualitative disturbance, "What does this model help us understand?" / "What does this model NOT show?"). No Mining Impact Ratio, no precision, no CER requirement, no notebook branding.
- **Fast & Curious** uses ramp height, displacement, averages, measured vs inferred. No invented m/s; "Relative Motion Energy" renamed "Relative Energy of Motion".
- No Friday option introduces new essential content.
