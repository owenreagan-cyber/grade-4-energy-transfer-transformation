# Lab Teacher Spec — Energy Detectives (Scavenger Hunt)

- **Lab #:** Chapter 1 optional scavenger-hunt activity (formerly Lab 1 of the 8-lab backbone; RECLASSIFIED Phase 5)
- **Title:** Energy Detectives
- **Chapter/week:** Chapter 1 · Week 1 — use after the Friday quiz, on a review day, or on a later FLEX day (never inside Sessions 1–3)
- **Tag:** OPTIONAL · ungraded · no points (look-fors only: `production/answer-keys/01-ch1-energy-detectives-key.md`)
- **Big Question:** Where can we observe evidence of energy causing change?
- **Concept:** energy is the ability to cause change; a change is evidence of energy
- **Time:** ~25–30 min (investigation + debrief)
- **Prep:** low
- **Cleanup:** return devices; collect student pages
- **Group size:** individual or pairs (works either way)

## Materials
- Classroom devices for observation (light switch, fan, clock, bell/drum)
- `01-ch1-energy-detectives-student.docx` (ADAPTED from AP 1.1)
- Index cards (vocabulary deck)

## Consumables
- printed student pages (1/student)

## Safety
- Students observe, not manipulate, electrical devices.
- No climbing; observe only classroom-safe items.

## Prediction
- "Which object in this room do you think will show the biggest change?"

## Fair test
- **Changed variable:** none (this is an observation hunt, not a controlled experiment)
- **Controlled factors:** n/a
- **Evidence collected:** before/after changes + inferred energy form

## Procedure
1. Model one example as a class (e.g., light bulb: off→on).
2. Students find **three** classroom examples of change.
3. For each: record Object/System, Before, After, Observable Change, Energy Form.
4. Write one question they still have.
5. Optional Part C: flashlight/battery demo (battery shown inside the device) — prediction, observation, explanation.

## Expected pattern
- Students record real, observable changes (dark→light, still→moving, quiet→noisy) and connect each to an energy form.

## Possible unexpected results
- Students may claim to "see" energy — redirect to "you saw the change; energy is what caused it."

## Troubleshooting
- If a student can't find examples, prompt: light, fan, clock, pencil sharpener, bell, radiator.

## Debrief
- Share 2–3 examples; ask "How is that change evidence of energy?" Reinforce ENERGY → CHANGE → EVIDENCE.
