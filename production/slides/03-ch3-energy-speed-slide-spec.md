# Slide Spec — Chapter 3: Energy and Speed Are Related

> Editable teaching deck. One core idea per slide. Notebook cues drive the ISN. No "Speed vs. Energy" graph; no measured-speed claims.

## Deck
- **File:** `production/slides/03-ch3-energy-speed-slides.pptx` (16:9, editable)
- **Target:** ~28 slides (do not pad)
- **Style:** navy/amber/light/green palette (matches Ch.1–2); Arial; one idea per slide

## Notebook cue legend
- **NOTEBOOK** — open to the insert being completed
- **WRITE** — copy/fill the exact answer
- **DRAW** — student draws in the box
- **LABEL** — add labels/arrows
- **REVEAL** — teacher reveals the answer after student attempt

## Visual strategy
- Same ball shown twice (slow vs fast) with different-length arrows; arrows labeled "model, not a measurement."
- Ramp diagram with cup + ruler for the lab slides.
- Sand tray + ball for Drop It slides.
- No graph labeled "Speed vs. Energy." Graph title is always "Ramp Height vs. Average Cup Displacement."

## Rules honored
- one idea per slide · prediction before reveal · no advanced vocabulary · no mass variation · no answer leakage on student-facing slides (REVEAL used).
