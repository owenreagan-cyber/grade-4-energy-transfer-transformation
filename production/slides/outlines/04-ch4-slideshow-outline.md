## Review

### Slide 17 — Review Q1
- **Key text:** "Energy transfer means —"
  - A. energy changes form · B. energy moves from place to place · C. energy disappears · D. energy is stored
- **Reveal:** answer next.

### Slide 18 — Q1 Answer
- **Answer:** B. Transfer = energy MOVES. (A is transformation.)

### Slide 19 — Review Q2
- **Key text:** "Look at the diagram: battery → wires → bulb → light. The LIGHT is evidence that —"
  - A. energy was transformed AND transferred · B. energy disappeared · C. the battery made light from nothing · D. no energy was involved
- **Visual:** DIAGRAM — the flashlight cutaway.
- **Reveal:** answer next.

### Slide 20 — Q2 Answer
- **Answer:** A. Electrical energy moved (transfer) and changed to light (transformation) — BOTH.

### Slide 21 — Review Q3
- **Key text:** "Tapping a cup made a paper target move across a gap. The evidence of transfer is —"
  - A. the cup being tapped · B. the paper moving without contact · C. the sound getting louder · D. the cup staying still
- **Reveal:** answer next.

### Slide 22 — Q3 Answer
- **Answer:** B. The target changed (moved) with no contact — that change is the evidence of transfer.

### Slide 23 — Review Q4
- **Key text:** "A warm cup cools down on a table. Which best describes this?"
  - A. transformation only · B. transfer of heat to the table and air · C. energy disappearing · D. electric current
- **Reveal:** answer next.

### Slide 24 — Q4 Answer
- **Answer:** B. Heat (thermal energy) transferred from the warm cup to cooler surroundings.

### Slide 25 — Review Q5
- **Key text:** "Which is a source, path, and receiver?"
  - A. flashlight → light beam → lit card · B. light → card → flashlight · C. card → beam → flashlight · D. beam → card → flashlight
- **Reveal:** answer next.

### Slide 26 — Q5 Answer
- **Answer:** A. The flashlight is the source, the beam is the path, the card is the receiver.

### Slide 27 — Review Q6
- **Key text:** "After energy transfers to a target, the energy —"
  - A. is gone forever · B. has moved and/or changed form · C. turned into matter · D. stopped existing
- **Reveal:** answer next.

### Slide 28 — Q6 Answer
- **Answer:** B. Energy is never destroyed; it transfers and/or transforms.
