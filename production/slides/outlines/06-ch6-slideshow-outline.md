## Review

### Slide 16 — Review Q1
- **Key text:** "A battery powers a buzzer that makes sound. The battery energy changing to sound is —"
  - A. transfer only · B. transformation · C. energy disappearing · D. stored energy
- **Visual:** DIAGRAM — battery → buzzer → sound waves.
- **Reveal:** answer next.

### Slide 17 — Q1 Answer
- **Answer:** B. The form changed (electrical → sound): a transformation.

### Slide 18 — Review Q2
- **Key text:** "Which shows energy transformation?"
  - A. warm water warming a cooler spoon · B. a rolling ball knocking a cup · C. a flashlight changing stored energy to light · D. a car coasting on flat ground
- **Reveal:** answer next.

### Slide 19 — Q2 Answer
- **Answer:** C. Stored (chemical) energy changes form into light — a transformation.

### Slide 20 — Review Q3
- **Key text:** "Look at the model: INPUT → TRANSFORMATION → OUTPUT. The OUTPUT is —"
  - A. the energy that goes in · B. the change in form · C. the useful change or signal the device makes · D. the problem to solve
- **Visual:** SYSTEM MODEL.
- **Reveal:** answer next.

### Slide 21 — Q3 Answer
- **Answer:** C. The output is the useful change/signal the device produces.

### Slide 22 — Review Q4
- **Key text:** "A student's device fails on Test 1. The BEST next step is —"
  - A. give up · B. change one thing and test again · C. change everything at once · D. pretend it worked
- **Reveal:** answer next.

### Slide 23 — Q4 Answer
- **Answer:** B. Engineers change ONE thing, retest, and use the evidence to improve.

### Slide 24 — Review Q5
- **Key text:** "Criteria are the things a good design must do. Constraints are the things that —"
  - A. make it better · B. limit what you can use or do · C. are not important · D. are always the same
- **Reveal:** answer next.

### Slide 25 — Q5 Answer
- **Answer:** B. Constraints limit the design (materials, time, space, safety).

### Slide 26 — Review Q6
- **Key text:** "Which is BOTH a transfer AND a transformation?"
  - A. a falling domino pushing the next one · B. a battery sending energy through wires to a bulb that glows · C. a warm mug sitting on a table · D. a ball at rest
- **Visual:** DIAGRAM — flashlight.
- **Reveal:** answer next.

### Slide 27 — Q6 Answer
- **Answer:** B. Energy moves through the wires (transfer) AND changes to light (transformation).
