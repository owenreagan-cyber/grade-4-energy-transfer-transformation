## Review

### Slide 12 — Review Q1
- **Key text:** "A moving car crashes into a resting car. The resting car starts to move. This shows —"
  - A. energy transferred from the moving car to the resting car · B. the resting car made its own energy · C. energy disappeared · D. no energy was involved
- **Visual:** DIAGRAM — before/after collision.
- **Reveal:** answer next.

### Slide 13 — Q1 Answer
- **Answer:** A. Energy of motion transferred from the moving car to the resting car.

### Slide 14 — Review Q2
- **Key text:** "To fairly compare how SPEED affects a collision, you should —"
  - A. change the car's mass and speed at once · B. keep the SAME car and change only the ramp height · C. use two different cars at the same speed · D. change the surface each time
- **Reveal:** answer next.

### Slide 15 — Q2 Answer
- **Answer:** B. Keep the car the SAME and change only speed (ramp height) — a fair test.

### Slide 16 — Review Q3
- **Key text:** "Look at the data: SAME car, low ramp → cup moves 3 cm; high ramp → cup moves 9 cm. The best claim is —"
  - A. the faster car had more energy of motion · B. the cup got heavier · C. speed made no difference · D. the low ramp had more energy
- **Visual:** DATA table.
- **Reveal:** answer next.

### Slide 17 — Q3 Answer
- **Answer:** A. Greater target displacement is evidence the faster (same-object) car had more energy of motion.

### Slide 18 — Review Q4
- **Key text:** "After a collision, some energy can become —"
  - A. sound · B. nothing · C. a new car · D. stored matter
- **Reveal:** answer next.

### Slide 19 — Q4 Answer
- **Answer:** A. Collisions often produce sound — energy changes form, it does not disappear.

### Slide 20 — Review Q5
- **Key text:** "Which best completes the chain? COLLISION → MOTION CAN CHANGE → ENERGY CAN ___ → ENERGY DOES NOT DISAPPEAR"
  - A. GROW · B. TRANSFER · C. STOP · D. FLOAT
- **Reveal:** answer next.

### Slide 21 — Q5 Answer
- **Answer:** B. Energy can TRANSFER to the other object during a collision.
