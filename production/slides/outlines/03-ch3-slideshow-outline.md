## Review

### Slide 14 — Review Q1
- **Key text:** "The SAME ball is rolled from a low ramp, then a high ramp. Which is true?"
  - A. the high-ramp roll has more energy of motion · B. the low-ramp roll has more energy of motion · C. both have the same energy · D. the ball gains stored energy on the ramp
- **Visual:** DIAGRAM — low vs. high ramp with the same ball.
- **Reveal:** answer next.

### Slide 15 — Q1 Answer
- **Answer:** A. For the SAME object, faster motion = more energy of motion; the high ramp makes it faster.

### Slide 16 — Review Q2
- **Key text:** "In Ramp It Up!, the distance the cup moved is —"
  - A. measured · B. inferred · C. a guess · D. the speed of the ball
- **Reveal:** answer next.

### Slide 17 — Q2 Answer
- **Answer:** A. Cup displacement is MEASURED with a ruler. Speed and energy are INFERRED.

### Slide 18 — Review Q3
- **Key text:** "Which pair is a fair comparison of speed and energy?"
  - A. a heavy ball vs. a light ball at the same speed · B. the SAME ball at two different speeds · C. two different balls at different speeds · D. a ball vs. a car
- **Reveal:** answer next.

### Slide 19 — Q3 Answer
- **Answer:** B. A fair comparison keeps the object the SAME and changes only speed.

### Slide 20 — Review Q4
- **Key text:** "Look at the data: 1 book → cup moves 4 cm; 3 books → cup moves 11 cm. The BEST claim is —"
  - A. more books make the cup heavier · B. the faster roll caused a greater change · C. the cup moved because of stored energy · D. speed was directly measured
- **Visual:** DATA table — the two data points.
- **Reveal:** answer next.

### Slide 21 — Q4 Answer
- **Answer:** B. Greater change (11 cm) is evidence of more energy of motion from the faster (same-object) roll.

### Slide 22 — Review Q5
- **Key text:** "Which did students NOT directly measure?"
  - A. ramp height · B. cup displacement · C. the ball's speed · D. the number of books
- **Reveal:** answer next.

### Slide 23 — Q5 Answer
- **Answer:** C. Speed was INFERRED from the change; height and displacement were measured.
