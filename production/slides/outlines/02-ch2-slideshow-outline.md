## Review

### Slide 12 — Review Q1
- **Key text:** "A rolling ball knocks over a cup. The cup falling over is —"
  - A. the energy of motion · B. evidence the ball had energy · C. a form of stored energy · D. the ball's motion
- **Visual:** PHOTOGRAPH — ball knocking cup over.
- **Reveal:** answer next.

### Slide 13 — Q1 Answer
- **Answer:** B. The change (cup falls) is evidence that the moving ball had energy.

### Slide 14 — Review Q2
- **Key text:** "Which object has energy of motion?"
  - A. a parked car · B. a book on a shelf · C. a ball rolling · D. a stretched rubber band
- **Visual:** ICON SET — four options pictured.
- **Reveal:** answer next.

### Slide 15 — Q2 Answer
- **Answer:** C. A rolling ball is moving, so it has energy of motion.

### Slide 16 — Review Q3
- **Key text:** "Look at the before/after pictures: before, the target is still; after, it has moved. What is the best claim?"
  - A. the target created its own energy · B. energy moved from the rolling ball to the target · C. the target was heavier · D. no energy was involved
- **Visual:** BEFORE/AFTER diagram.
- **Reveal:** answer next.

### Slide 17 — Q3 Answer
- **Answer:** B. The moving ball's energy transferred to the target; the target moving is the evidence.

### Slide 18 — Review Q4
- **Key text:** "'The target marble moved.' This statement is an example of —"
  - A. an inference · B. an observation · C. a prediction · D. a definition
- **Reveal:** answer next.

### Slide 19 — Q4 Answer
- **Answer:** B. It states what was seen (observation). "The rolling marble had energy" is the inference.

### Slide 20 — Review Q5
- **Key text:** "Which best completes the chain? MOVING OBJECT → ___ → EVIDENCE"
  - A. STORED ENERGY · B. CAUSES CHANGE · C. MOTION · D. INFERENCE
- **Reveal:** answer next.

### Slide 21 — Q5 Answer
- **Answer:** B. A moving object causes a change, and the change is the evidence.
