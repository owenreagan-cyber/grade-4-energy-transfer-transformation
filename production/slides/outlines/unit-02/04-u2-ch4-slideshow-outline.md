## Review

### Slide 9 — Review Q1
- **Key text:** "Nuclear power plants make electricity using —"
  - A. wind
  - B. heat from boiling water into steam
  - C. sunlight
  - D. coal
- **Reveal:** answer next.

### Slide 10 — Q1 Answer
- **Answer:** B. Heat boils water into steam that spins a turbine..

### Slide 11 — Review Q2

  - D. a gas

### Slide 12 — Q2 Answer

### Slide 13 — Review Q3

### Slide 15 — Review Q4
