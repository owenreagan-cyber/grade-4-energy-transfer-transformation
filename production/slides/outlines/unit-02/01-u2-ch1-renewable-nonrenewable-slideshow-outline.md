## Review

### Slide 11 — Review Q1
- **Key text:** "Coal is plentiful now but forms over millions of years. Coal is —"
  - A. renewable · B. nonrenewable · C. both · D. not a resource
- **Reveal:** answer next.

### Slide 12 — Q1 Answer
- **Answer:** B. Nonrenewable — it is used much faster than it forms.

### Slide 13 — Review Q2
- **Key text:** "Which is a renewable resource?"
  - A. petroleum · B. natural gas · C. wind · D. coal
- **Reveal:** answer next.

### Slide 14 — Q2 Answer
- **Answer:** C. Wind is restored naturally and quickly.

### Slide 15 — Review Q3
- **Key text:** "Sunlight is renewable but not always available. Why?"
  - A. the sun disappears forever · B. some places/times get little sunlight · C. solar cells break · D. sunlight is nonrenewable
- **Reveal:** answer next.

### Slide 16 — Q3 Answer
- **Answer:** B. Location and time of day/year affect available sunlight.

### Slide 17 — Review Q4
- **Key text:** "What makes a resource renewable?"
  - A. it is abundant · B. it is cheap · C. it is restored as quickly as it is used · D. it comes from the ground
- **Reveal:** answer next.

### Slide 18 — Q4 Answer
- **Answer:** C. Renewability is about how fast it is restored vs. used.

### Slide 19 — Review Q5
- **Key text:** "A resource can be abundant and still be —"
  - A. renewable · B. free · C. nonrenewable · D. water
- **Reveal:** answer next.

### Slide 20 — Q5 Answer
- **Answer:** C. Abundant does NOT mean renewable.
