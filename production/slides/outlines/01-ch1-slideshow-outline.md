## Review (multiple choice)

### Slide 19 — Review Q1
- **Key text:** "A plant grows taller toward a sunny window. This growth is best described as —"
  - A. evidence of energy · B. a form of stored energy · C. a kind of motion energy · D. a way energy disappears
- **Student action:** THINK — signal A/B/C/D.
- **Visual:** PHOTOGRAPH — small plant leaning toward window light.
- **Reveal:** answer slide next.

### Slide 20 — Q1 Answer
- **Answer:** A. The growth is a CHANGE, and the change is evidence that energy was involved.
- **Distractor note:** B confuses a change with a stored form; D wrongly says energy disappears.

### Slide 21 — Review Q2
- **Key text:** "Which of these shows stored energy that can cause a change later?"
  - A. a ball rolling down a hill · B. a stretched rubber band · C. a ringing bell · D. a lit flashlight
- **Visual:** ICON SET — four options pictured.
- **Reveal:** answer slide next.

### Slide 22 — Q2 Answer
- **Answer:** B. A stretched rubber band has stored energy that can snap back (a change) later.

### Slide 23 — Review Q3
- **Key text:** "Look at the diagram: battery → wires → bulb → light. The LIGHT is —"
  - A. the energy that started · B. the change we observe · C. a form of stored energy · D. the cause of the change
- **Visual:** DIAGRAM — the battery circuit with output rays labeled LIGHT.
- **Reveal:** answer slide next.

### Slide 24 — Q3 Answer
- **Answer:** B. The light is the observable change; it is evidence that electrical energy was involved.

### Slide 25 — Review Q4
- **Key text:** "A student writes, 'The cup of warm water cooled down.' That is an example of —"
  - A. an explanation · B. an observation · C. a stored form of energy · D. a prediction
- **Reveal:** answer slide next.

### Slide 26 — Q4 Answer
- **Answer:** B. It states what was seen (observation); "the heat moved out" would be an explanation.

### Slide 27 — Review Q5
- **Key text:** "Which best completes the chain? ENERGY → ___ → EVIDENCE"
  - A. LIGHT · B. STORED · C. CHANGE · D. MOTION
- **Reveal:** answer slide next.

### Slide 28 — Q5 Answer
- **Answer:** C. Energy causes a CHANGE, and the change is the evidence.
