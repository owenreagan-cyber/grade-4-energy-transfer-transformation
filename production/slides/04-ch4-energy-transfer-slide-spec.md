## Visual strategy
- Model strip SOURCE → ENERGY MOVES → TARGET CHANGES on every concept slide.
- Sound: cup + paper (arrow labeled "model, not a mechanism").
- Light: flashlight → beam → card (with optional barrier).
- Heat: warm water + thermometer + cooler cup (no hot plate imagery).
- Electricity: battery holder + wires + bulb/buzzer/motor (no wall outlet imagery).
- Transfer vs transformation: two side-by-side boxes; BOTH = overlapping boxes.

## Rules honored
- one idea per slide · prediction before reveal (REVEAL used) · no answer leakage on student-facing slides
- TRANSFER ≠ TRANSFORMATION shown explicitly; BOTH shown as valid
- no compression-wave / electron-flow / conduction-convection-radiation / kinetic-potential language
- low-voltage electricity imagery only; no hot plates, wall outlets, or coin cells
