# Unit 2 Review Game — Teacher Guide

**Resource Detective: Solve the Energy Source Case!**
A clickable Jeopardy-style review game for the Unit 2 final assessment
(*Using Natural Resources for Energy* · CKSci Grade 4).

---

## Purpose

This 30-minute game reviews the exact concepts on the final Unit 2 written
assessment in a team, oral, low-stakes format — no CER writing, no long
passages. Every question traces to the final assessment, the Unit 2 written
study/review, or the Unit 2 guided notes (Ch1–Ch9) and Reader.

## Setup

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Deck | `unit2-review-game.pptx` (16:9, 55 slides) |
| Scorecards | `unit2-review-game-scorecards.pdf` (1 per team) |
| Cards | `unit2-review-game-cards.docx` (Teacher Explain + Challenge, cut out) |
| Projector / board | Present the deck full-screen; click tiles to navigate |
| Extra | dry-erase markers or pencils, small whiteboards (optional) |

## Recommended Grouping

- **5 teams** of 4 (matches the 5-file board) or **4–5 teams** of 4–5.
- Assign each team one scorecard and a marker. Choose a scorekeeper.

## 30-Minute Pacing

| Min | Segment |
|---|---:|
| 0–2 | Setup: teams, scorecards, open the board |
| 2–22 | **Game**: teams take turns picking tiles; teacher clicks the tile |
| 22–27 | **Final Challenge**: the Mystery Town scenario (see below) |
| 27–30 | Wrap-up: tally scores, unlock clues, test reminders |

## How to Use the Clickable Navigation

1. **Board (Slide 3)** — click any gold tile to open that question.
2. **Question slide** — read aloud; team discusses; click **REVEAL ANSWER**.
3. **Answer slide** — click **◀ BACK TO BOARD** (or **See the clue again**).
4. **FINAL CHALLENGE** button (below the board) opens the closing scenario.
5. **All links are internal** — no internet, no external files needed.

> Tiles stay gold after selection. Track used tiles on the scorecard
> (check the box when a tile is answered) — the scorecard is the authoritative
> tracking method.

## Scoring

- Award the tile's dollar value for a correct answer.
- `$300` tiles = Team Challenge (30-second demo). `$500` tiles = Team Explain
  (one or two spoken sentences).
- Every answered file unlocks that file's **clue** on the scorecard.
- **Final Challenge** = bonus (suggest +500); the teacher accepts any
  reasoned, fact-backed recommendation.

## Challenge-Card Instructions

- **Team Explain ($500)** — team discusses, then one student explains in one
  or two spoken sentences. No writing.
- **Team Challenge ($300)** — 30-second acting/demo; then one sentence to
  explain. Full points for a clear, correct explanation.

## Final Challenge — The Mystery Town

> A town wants to switch to renewable energy. It has strong, steady wind every
> day, many cloudy days, no large river nearby, and no volcanic activity.
> Which ONE resource should it investigate? Explain.

**Best answer:** **Wind power** — strong, steady wind makes it reliable there.
Cloudy days limit solar, no river limits hydroelectric, and no volcanic
activity limits geothermal.

## What to Skip If Short on Time

1. Skip the `$100` tiles (literal recall) first.
2. Play only the `$100 / $300 / $500` rows (see Quick Mode).
3. Skip the Final Challenge reveal; instead collect it as a written exit ticket.

## Quick Mode (15 Minutes)

- **2 min** setup → **10 min** game on the `$100`, `$300`, and `$500` rows only
  → **3 min** Final Challenge → wrap-up.
- Teams pick from only three rows; skip `$200` and `$400`.

## Answer Key

### FILE 1 — Resource Detective

| $ | Question | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| 100 | A resource restored as fast as it is used is a ____. | **A — renewable resource** |
| 200 | Main difference between renewable and nonrenewable? | **C — renewable restored quickly; nonrenewable takes millions of years** |
| 300 | Coal is plentiful today but forms over millions of years → coal is ____. | **B — nonrenewable** (abundant ≠ renewable) |
| 400 | Propane abundant in tanks but takes millions of years to form → ____. | **C — nonrenewable resource** |
| 500 | Why does it matter how quickly a resource is restored? | Renewable only if it comes back as fast as (or faster than) we use it |

### FILE 2 — Fossil Fuels

| $ | Question | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| 100 | Which fossil fuel is a liquid refined into gasoline/diesel? | **A — petroleum (crude oil)** |
| 200 | Three fossil fuels: coal, petroleum, and ____. | **natural gas** |
| 300 | Which energy source is a fossil fuel? | **B — coal** |
| 400 | Why are fossil fuels nonrenewable? | **B — used much faster than nature can create them** |
| 500 | Coal vs petroleum: alike / different? | Alike: both form from remains over millions of years. Different: coal solid (swamp plants); petroleum liquid (ocean organisms) |

### FILE 3 — Turbines & Generators

| $ | Question | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| 100 | Blades that spin when air/water/steam pushes → ____. | **turbine** |
| 200 | Final form of energy produced by a generator? | **A — electricity** |
| 300 | Which machine belongs in the empty box? (Moving air → ▢ → Generator → Electricity) | **A — turbine** |
| 400 | Shared step in coal AND nuclear plants? | **C — heat turns water into steam that spins a turbine-generator** |
| 500 | What stays the same in coal vs nuclear? | Heat → steam → turbine → generator → electricity |

### FILE 4 — Renewable Power

| $ | Question | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| 100 | Hydroelectric power is electricity from ____. | **moving water (usually through a dam)** |
| 200 | Geothermal heat comes from where? | **D — deep inside Earth** |
| 300 | A solar cell converts sunlight into what? | **A — electricity** |
| 400 | Mountain town: steady wind, cloudy, no rivers → best resource? | **D — wind power** |
| 500 | Why is solar renewable but not always available? | Sun keeps shining (renewable), but depends on location/weather/time of day |

### FILE 5 — Resource Challenge

| $ | Question | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| 100 | Common hybrid-car energy combination? | **B — gasoline fuel and battery power** |
| 200 | Major limitation of solar energy? | **C — depends on available sunlight** |
| 300 | Environmental cost of burning coal/oil? | **A — releasing smoke and air pollutants** |
| 400 | Sunny town, little moving water → most reliable? | **D — solar power** |
| 500 | One renewable resource: benefit + limitation + where it works. | e.g. wind — benefit: no fuel to burn; limitation: needs wind; works where windy |

### Final Challenge — The Mystery Town

**Wind power** — steady wind is reliable; cloudy days limit solar, no river
limits hydro, no volcanic activity limits geothermal.

## Content-Scope Notes

- Locked vocabulary avoided (no `kinetic`, `potential`, `mechanical/chemical/
  thermal energy`, `radiant`, `fission`, `U-235`, `electron`, `photon`,
  `semiconductor`, `baseload`, `dispatch`, `grid stabilization`, `peak demand`,
  `climate change`, `carbon footprint`, etc.).
- `geothermal energy` is an approved Unit 2 term and is used normally.
- No answer leakage: visual questions label neutral components or use a
  mystery `?` box; they never label the object being identified.
