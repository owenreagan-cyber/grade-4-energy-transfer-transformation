# UNIT 1 FINAL ASSESSMENT — SCORING GUIDE

> Grade 4 · 25 questions · 50 points (2 pts each) · objective-only (matching + multiple choice).
> Rebuilt to the locked Phase 13X assessment standard. See `docs/assessment/UNIT1_FINAL_ASSESSMENT_BLUEPRINT.md`.

## Structure & scoring

| Section | Questions | Points |
|---|---|---|
| Matching | 1–8 | 16 (2 pts each) |
| Multiple choice | 9–25 | 34 (2 pts each) |
| **Total** | 25 | **50** |

- Every item is objective and worth exactly **2 points** — no partial credit, no written rubrics, no CER.
- The teacher key (`07-unit-final-assessment-key.docx`) contains all correct answers plus a Reader/guided-notes source-alignment table and is the authoritative scoring reference.
- No answer key content appears in the student PDF.

## Correction / recovery policy

- Corrections follow `curriculum/chapter-07/student/recovery/07-final-assessment-corrections-recovery.md` (missed item → original thinking → correct idea → evidence → new example).
- Absent students take the final untimed. Makeup follows `curriculum/chapter-07/student/recovery/07-ch7-absent-student-recovery.md` and is labeled "completed independently / makeup."
