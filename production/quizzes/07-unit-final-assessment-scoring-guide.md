# UNIT FINAL ASSESSMENT — SCORING GUIDE

> Grade 4 · 26 items · 50 points · 30–45 min · split across Wednesday/Thursday if needed.

## Total points & section weights
| Section | Items | Points | Weight |
|---|---|---|---|
| Core concepts | 1,2,5,6,12,13,14,17,21,25 | 13 | 26% |
| Data / diagram | 3,4,8,9,10,15,18 | 15 | 30% |
| Application / evidence | 7,11,16,19,22 | 13 | 26% |
| Engineering / synthesis | 20,23,24,26 | 9 | 18% |
| **Total** | 26 | **50** | 100% |

> Weights differ slightly from the blueprint's raw item counts because diagrams/short answers carry more points, matching the official philosophy (written explanations weighted higher than MC).

## Written-response rubric (items 3, 23, 26)
- 4-point model (item 3): Above/Average/Adequate/Inadequate (see key).
- 3-point CER (item 23) and engineering (item 26) rubrics (see key).
- Full detail in `07-unit-final-assessment-key.md`.

## Correction policy
- Corrections use `curriculum/chapter-07/student/recovery/07-final-assessment-corrections-recovery.md` (MISSED ITEM → ORIGINAL THINKING → CORRECT IDEA → EVIDENCE → NEW EXAMPLE).
- Corrections are concept repair, not busywork; no arbitrary grade cutoffs imposed beyond project policy.

## Missing-assessment / recovery policy
- Absent students take the final untimed (or a teacher-selected subset: items 1–13 + one written explanation).
- Makeup follows `curriculum/chapter-07/student/recovery/07-ch7-absent-student-recovery.md`.
- Label makeup work "completed independently / makeup."
