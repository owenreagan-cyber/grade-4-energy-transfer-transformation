# Quiz Blueprint — Chapter 4: Energy Transfer

- **Artifact:** `production/quizzes/04-ch4-energy-transfer-quiz.docx`
- **Length:** 11 items · 10–15 minutes
- **Grade:** 4 · CKSci-aligned · PE 4-PS3-2

## Assessed concepts
1. Energy transfer (energy moves from place to place / object to object)
2. Evidence of transfer (observable change in a target)
3. Sound transfer
4. Light transfer
5. Heat transfer
6. Electric-current transfer
7. Transfer vs transformation
8. BOTH classification
9. Simple data interpretation (temperature table)
10. Short explanation (evidence-based)

## Out of scope (NOT assessed)
- detailed sound waves / compression-wave mechanics
- electron flow / circuit equations
- collisions (Ch.5)
- engineering design (Ch.6)
- kinetic/potential/mechanical/radiant vocabulary

## Spiral (light, optional)
- energy = the ability to cause change (Ch.1)
- change as evidence (Ch.1)
- (motion-energy relationship only if it supports an item; not required)

## Rubric note
- Item 11 scored for Claim + Evidence (a "because" statement naming an observed change). Reasoning = connecting the change to transfer.
- Item 9/10 accept BOTH where the system moves AND changes form; no false either/or.
