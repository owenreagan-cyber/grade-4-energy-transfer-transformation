# Answer Key — Chapter 1 Practice (v1.1)

- **Artifact:** `production/worksheets/01-ch1-energy-causes-change-practice.docx` — **ungraded** practice (no score line). Assign after Session 2 as take-home or any spare 12-minute window; recommended before the Friday quiz.
- **Source alignment:** Reader Ch.1 pp. 1–6; TG L1–L2. Light switch, toy car, fan, and stored-energy rows come from the Reader or Guided Notes; the **bell** and **ice cube** rows are EXTENSION (everyday experience) — accept any reasonable observable change.

## Part A — What changed?
| Scenario | What changed? (accept any observable change) | Form of energy (accept any reasonable one of the five) |
|---|---|---|
| A light switch is flipped. | the room changed from dark to bright | light (or electrical energy) |
| A toy car is pushed. | the car changed from still to moving | motion |
| A bell is rung. (EXTENSION) | quiet → a ringing sound | sound |
| An ice cube sits on a warm hand. (EXTENSION) | the ice melts / gets smaller (solid → liquid) | heat |

## Part B — Evidence or not enough evidence?
| Observation | Circle | Because … (sample) |
|---|---|---|
| The fan blades started spinning when we plugged it in. | EVIDENCE | we saw a change: still → moving |
| The battery looks shiny. | NOT ENOUGH | looking shiny is not a change that shows energy at work |
| The room got brighter when the light came on. | EVIDENCE | dark → bright is a change |
| I think the motor is strong. | NOT ENOUGH | it is an opinion; no change was observed |

## Part C — Stored energy
| Example | Stored energy? | Change later — or happening now? |
|---|---|---|
| a battery in a flashlight that is switched off | **YES** | when the flashlight is switched on, the stored energy can make the bulb light (Reader p. 4) |
| an apple held above the ground | **YES** | if it is let go, it falls (stored energy because of its position — Reader p. 5) |
| a stretched rubber band | **YES** | if it is let go, it snaps back and may make a sound |
| a toy car rolling across the floor | **NO** — its energy is in its motion **now** (not stored) | the change is already happening |

*Rationale (v1.1): the v1.0 "book lying flat on a table" row was removed — whether a book on a table has stored energy depends on the reference level and is outside the Reader's scope. The toy-car row gives a clear contrast: moving now vs stored for later.*

## Part D — Explain (sample)
"I flipped a switch and the room changed from dark to bright. A change is evidence of energy, so the light shows energy was at work."
- Accept any response that (a) names an observed change and (b) says the change is evidence of energy. Do not require "stored energy." Accept "proof" once; restate "evidence."
