# Answer Key — Ramp It Up! 2.0

- **Artifact:** `production/worksheets/03-ch3-ramp-it-up-student.docx`
- **Source alignment:** AP 4.1 (Ramp It Up, Day 1); Reader Ch.3 pp. 13–16; TG L4. ADAPTED (3 heights + trials + cm + graph).

| Item | Correct/expected response | Acceptable alternatives | Reasoning | Common misconception | Scoring note | What NOT to penalize |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| PREDICT | a guess (e.g., "3 books moves the cup farthest") | any prediction | prediction = testable guess | wrong prediction = failure | 1 pt | a "wrong" prediction |
| FAIR TEST (change/keep/measure) | change ramp height; keep ball/cup/surface/release point/method; measure cup displacement (cm) | paraphrases | fair-test isolation of one variable | thinking speed is directly measured | 3 pts (1 each) | informal phrasing |
| DATA TABLE | displacement increases with ramp height; averages computed correctly | any internally consistent data | repeated trials + average | recording "speed" in a data cell | 6 pts (table complete + averages) | noisy/uneven trial data |
| GRAPH | title "Ramp Height vs. Average Cup Displacement"; x = books, y = cm; 3 bars/points rising | any correct axes/plot | graph shows the pattern | labeling it "Speed vs. Energy" | 4 pts | bar vs. dot style |
| OBSERVE | higher ramp → farther cup | "the cup moved more at 3 books" | pattern in data | — | 1 pt | — |
| INFER | faster motion of the same ball is associated with greater energy of motion | "higher ramp → faster → more energy (inferred)" | inference from measured change | claiming we measured energy | 2 pts | informal phrasing |
| EXPLAIN (CER) | Claim + evidence (measured displacement) + reasoning (greater change → greater energy of motion, inferred) | any claim + measured change + reasoning | change = evidence of energy | stating energy as directly observed | 3 pts | full formal CER not required |

## Notes
- Students must NOT claim they measured speed or energy. If they write "we measured the speed," do not award the INFER/EXPLAIN inference credit — redirect to "we measured the change."
- The "faster" determination is an INFERENCE from ramp height, not a measurement.
- A wrong prediction is NOT penalized if the observation/explanation are correct.
