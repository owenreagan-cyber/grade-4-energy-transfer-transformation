## Look-fors (teacher check, not scored)
| Part | Look for | Common slip | Response |
|---|---|---|---|
| A (class example) | a real before/after: dull pencil → shorter, sharper pencil, shavings, sound | "I saw the energy" | "You saw the change; energy is what caused it." |
| A — form | electricity, or motion + sound | — | accept any reasonable form |
| B (hunt) — 3 rows | object, a real before and after, an energy form | copying the class example | ask for a different object |
| B — question | any question about energy or change | — | celebrate curiosity |
| C (flashlight demo) — prediction | any prediction | treating a wrong prediction as a failure | never penalize a prediction |
| C — observation / evidence | the light came on / the bulb changed from off to on | claiming "the battery got hot" without observing it | only credit what was observed |
| C — explanation | the battery has stored energy; it caused a change later | only moving objects have energy | restate: "A still battery can store energy for later." |
