| Item | Correct/expected response | Acceptable alternatives | Expected pattern | Reasoning | Common misconception | Scoring note | What NOT to penalize |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Big Question | Electric current transferred energy from the battery to the device; evidence = the device changed (lit / sounded / moved). | "The battery powered the bulb through the wires." | battery → circuit → device → change | the distant device's change = evidence of transfer | "Electricity was used up before reaching the device." | Credit the change + the path | "energy traveled through the wires" |
| Prediction | Any reasonable prediction ("the bulb will light when the circuit is complete"). | — | stated before test | — | — | Not graded for correctness | — |
| Model (BATTERY → CIRCUIT → DEVICE → CHANGE) | Battery = source; circuit = path; device = target; change = light/sound/motion. | order correct with any wording | battery → circuit → device → change | the model orders the transfer | naming the device as the source | Credit correct order | informal labels |
| Observe (complete vs broken circuit) | Complete circuit → device changes; broken circuit → change stops. | "It stops when I disconnect a wire." | complete → change; broken → none | energy transfer follows the circuit path | "the wire carries 'used' electricity" | Credit cause-effect of breaking the circuit | — |
| Classify TRANSFER / TRANSFORMATION / BOTH | **BOTH** — energy moved through the wires (transfer) AND changed form to light/sound/motion (transformation). | BOTH with either justification | BOTH | the device changes form at a distance | "only transfer" (or "only transformation") | Credit BOTH + a reason; do NOT force false either/or | saying "both" without full justification |

## Notes
- This is the anchor system for "BOTH." Ensure students can name BOTH the movement (transfer) and the form change (transformation).
- Do NOT require electron flow, circuit equations, or current/voltage terms.
- Safety: low-voltage battery only — the key should never reference wall outlets/chargers as the investigation.
