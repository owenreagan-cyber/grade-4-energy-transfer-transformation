# ANSWER KEY / SCORING GUIDANCE — Chapter 6 Engineering Performance Task

> Teacher scoring support for the tabletop signal-device capstone. Use with `production/rubrics/06-ch6-engineering-rubric.docx` and AP 13.4.

## Page 1 — Problem / Criteria / Constraints
- **Problem** restated in the team's own words (the tabletop signal device).
- **Criteria** listed and addressed (respond to trigger; observable signal; one transformation; repeated; explain pathway).
- **Constraints** listed and respected (materials only; no electricity/flame/hot plate/launching; time; quantity; footprint).

## Page 2 — Blueprint + Materials
- Materials match the kit; blueprint labels the TRIGGER, OUTPUT, and energy-change location.
- "How I will know it works" names an observable, testable outcome.

## Page 3 — Energy Path
- INPUT (stored energy), TRANSFORMATION (stored → motion → sound), OUTPUT (the signal) — or equivalent correct pathway.
- Transfer/Transformation/BOTH verdict is correct AND explained.

## Page 4 — Test 1
- 3 trials recorded; evidence is observational (did it trigger, what output, reliability), NOT invented.
- "What failed" treated as data, not as a shame item.

## Page 5 — Redesign
- ONE change, tied to a Test 1 observation (evidence-based), with a prediction.

## Page 6 — Test 2
- Same conditions as Test 1; Test 1 vs Test 2 compared with evidence.

## Page 7 — Final Explanation
- Claim / Evidence / Reasoning present; uses "supports" (not "proves"); engineering vocabulary used.

## Common correct pathways (examples)
- **Bell Swing:** stored (rubber band) → energy of motion (arm) → sound (cup) — BOTH.
- **Flag Drop:** stored (raised object) → energy of motion (falling) → visible signal (flag moves) — BOTH.
- **Domino Signal:** stored (tipped first domino) → energy of motion (transfers down the line) → sound/visible last domino — BOTH.

## What NOT to score for
- Decoration ("prettiness") — score reasoning + evidence.
- A failed prototype — initial failure is data; score the redesign reasoning.
- Unsupported "heavier = better" claims — require controlled evidence.

## Rubric total
- 15 points (Design 5 · Energy Model 3 · Testing/Redesign 3 · Final Explanation 4). See rubric DOCX for the cell-level rubric.
