## Notes
- Do NOT require a perfect near/middle/far distance trend — distance is qualitative here.
- Do NOT require "compression wave" or "vibration of air particles."
- Do NOT penalize "sound travels through air" — it's a reasonable Grade 4 statement (the TG uses "sound waves from tapping the cup pushed air").
