# Unit Synthesis Review — TEACHER KEY

> Model answers for `production/worksheets/07-unit-synthesis-review.docx` (Week 9 · Unit 1 synthesis).
> Grade 4 target vocabulary: **energy of motion**, **transfer**, **transformation**, **evidence**, **change**, **criteria**, **constraints**, **refine**.
> No advanced terms (kinetic, potential, mechanical, radiant, momentum, impulse).

## A. ENERGY → CHANGE → EVIDENCE

1. Energy is the ability to **cause change**.

2. Circle the BEST evidence that energy is involved:
   - a) A ball rolls across the floor → the ball **moved / rolled**.
   - b) A bell rings across the playground → we **hear a sound / ringing**.
   - c) A plant grows taller → the plant **grew / got taller**.

3. Pick ONE change above and write the evidence you would observe.
   - *Key idea:* name a change and the observable evidence of it. *Example:* "The ball rolled across the floor — I saw it move."

## B. MOTION + ENERGY OF MOTION

4. A moving object has **energy of motion** that a still object does not.

5. One example of a moving object causing a change:
   - *Example:* "A rolling ball knocks a block over." (Any moving object + linked change = full credit.)

## C. SAME OBJECT / DIFFERENT SPEED

6. Circle one pair: **(A) measure cup movement, infer speed**. We measure the cup's movement; we infer speed/energy of motion (we do not measure speed directly).

7. For the SAME object, as speed goes up, energy of motion goes **up** (increases).

## D. TRANSFER PATHS

8. The FOUR ways energy can transfer from place to place: **sound · light · heat · electric current**.

9. Warm water warms a cool spoon — **heat** transfers to the spoon (heat moves, stays heat).

## E. TRANSFER / TRANSFORMATION / BOTH

10. a) Flashlight turns on (battery → light): **TRANSFORMATION**
    b) Warm water warms a spoon (heat moves, stays heat): **TRANSFER**
    c) A domino falls and clicks (motion passes + sound): **BOTH**
    d) A buzzer buzzes (electrical → sound): **TRANSFORMATION**

## F. COLLISION PREDICTIONS

11. Add weight (change ONLY weight), same release — **predict:** the top block falls off more easily / more blocks move. **Explain:** the heavier car has more energy of motion at about the same speed, so it transfers more to the blocks. *(Key idea: more weight → more energy of motion → bigger change, with only weight changed.)*

12. During a collision, energy of motion transfers to the target, and some becomes **sound** (and a tiny, hard-to-measure bit of heat).

## G. DATA / GRAPH READING

13. As ramp height increases, cup movement **increases** (the line goes up). This is evidence that **a higher ramp makes the car faster, so it has more energy of motion** (SAME car).

## H. ENGINEERING — criteria / constraints / test / refine

14. Match:
    - criteria → what a good solution must do
    - constraints → the limits (materials, time, safety)
    - refine → to improve based on test evidence

15. A fair test changes **one** thing and keeps everything else the same.

## I. EDISON / ENGINEERING PERSISTENCE

16. Did Edison invent the FIRST light bulb? **NO**

17. What did Edison actually do? — *Key idea:* he **improved** existing light bulbs through testing/redesign. *Example:* "Edison improved the light bulb so it worked better and lasted longer."

18. About how many materials did Edison's team test for the filament? **about 6,000**

19. Persistence in engineering means **keep testing and redesigning until the solution works** (not giving up after a try that fails).

## J. SHORT EXPLANATION (claim / evidence / reasoning)

20. Pick ONE device and describe the energy change:
    - *Example (flashlight):* **Claim:** the flashlight transforms stored energy into light. **Evidence:** when I turn it on, it lights up. **Reasoning:** the light is the change I observe, so the energy changed form (transformation). *Also valid: ramp toy (transfer), buzzer (transformation), warm-water spoon (transfer).*
    - *Scoring flexibility:* 3 pts = claim + evidence + reasoning, linked; 2 = claim + evidence; 1 = claim only. Credit "supports/shows," not "proves."
