# Energy's Big Questions — TEACHER KEY

> Model answers for `production/worksheets/07-energy-big-questions.docx` (Unit 1 synthesis · aligned to AP UR.1).
> Grade 4 target vocabulary: **energy of motion**, **transfer**, **transformation**, **evidence**, **change**. Use *evidence*, never *proof*.
> For open items, give credit for the **key idea**, not exact wording. No advanced terms (kinetic, potential, mechanical, radiant, momentum, impulse).

| # | Question | Key idea(s) | Example response (Grade 4) | Scoring flexibility |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | What is energy? | energy = the ability to cause change | "Energy is the ability to cause a change." | Accept "energy makes things change / move / heat up / light up." |
| 2 | How do observable changes provide evidence that energy is involved? | a change we can observe (motion, sound, light, heat) is evidence | "When I see a ball move or hear a bell ring, that change is evidence that energy was there." | Any one observable change named = full credit. |
| 3 | How does a moving object show it has energy? | a moving object has energy of motion and can cause a change | "A moving marble can knock over a cup, so it has energy of motion." | Accept "it moves / it can change something." |
| 4 | How does speed affect energy of motion for the SAME object? | SAME object, faster = more energy of motion (bigger change) | "For the same ball, faster means more energy of motion, so it moves the cup farther." | Must say "same object." Do not credit mass comparisons. |
| 5 | How can energy transfer from place to place? | four transfer paths: sound, light, heat, electric current | "Energy can transfer by sound, light, heat, and electric current." | Any 3 of 4 paths acceptable. |
| 6 | What happens to energy in a collision? | energy transfers to the target; some becomes sound; it does not disappear | "The moving car's energy transfers to the target and some becomes sound." | Credit "does not disappear." |
| 7 | What is transformation? | transformation = energy changes form | "Transformation is when energy changes from one form to another." | Accept "changes form." |
| 8 | When can BOTH transfer and transformation occur? | when energy moves AND changes form at the same time | "A domino falls: motion transfers to the next domino AND becomes sound." | Any valid BOTH example (domino, flashlight, buzzer). |
| 9 | How do engineers use testing and evidence? | test → observe evidence → redesign → retest | "Engineers test, look at the evidence, then improve and test again." | Accept "test and improve." |
| 10 | What did Edison's work show about engineering persistence? | he did NOT invent the first bulb; he improved it by testing thousands of materials | "Edison kept testing and improving, so his light bulb worked better and lasted." | Credit "kept trying / many tries / improved, not invented." |

## Notes for the teacher

- Question 4 is the core same-object relationship; do **not** accept "heavier = more energy" here (mass is a different variable, taught separately).
- Question 8 needs BOTH the movement and the form change to be full credit; one alone = partial.
- Questions 2, 3, 8 are open — accept any *reasoned, on-topic* Grade 4 answer with the key idea.
