## Section D — FIX THE EXPLANATION
| Item | Correct/expected response | Acceptable alternatives | Reasoning | Common misconception | What NOT to penalize |

## Section E — SHORT EXPLAIN
| Item | Correct/expected response | Acceptable alternatives | Reasoning | Common misconception | What NOT to penalize |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| E | Names a source, a change in a target, and states the change is evidence of transfer (e.g., "The flashlight lit the card; the card lighting up is evidence light transferred energy.") | any example with source + change + transfer | change = evidence → transfer inferred | "energy is directly visible" | spelling; informal wording |

## Notes
- Accept BOTH for flashlight/buzzer/motor without forcing either/or.
- Warm water → spoon is TRANSFER only at Grade 4 (heat moves; no required form-change framing).
- Reject any claim that energy "disappears" or is "used up."
