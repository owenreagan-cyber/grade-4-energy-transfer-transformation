| Circle changes | MOTION (speed/direction) · SHAPE · SOUND | any subset is acceptable if the example matches | collisions can change motion, shape, and produce sound | "collisions only change shape" | — |

## Section B — SPEED (same car)
| Item | Correct/expected response | Acceptable alternatives | Reasoning | Common misconception | What NOT to penalize |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| B1 | The fast roll moves the target farther — the same object moving faster has greater energy of motion. | "faster = more energy" | same object, faster → more energy → greater change | "faster car is heavier" | informal phrasing |
| B2 | Kept the same: the CAR, the TARGET, and the SURFACE (only speed changed). | naming all three | variable isolation | "changed everything" | listing any two of the three |

## Section E — ENERGY DOES NOT DISAPPEAR
| Item | Correct/expected response | Acceptable alternatives | Reasoning | Common misconception | What NOT to penalize |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| E | The car's energy of motion transferred to the cup (it moved) and some became sound. | "moved to the cup + became sound" | energy transfers, never disappears | "energy disappeared" | "a tiny bit of heat" optional |

## Notes
- Reject any "energy disappeared / used up / destroyed" language.
- Do NOT require momentum/force/deformation/kinetic-potential terms.
