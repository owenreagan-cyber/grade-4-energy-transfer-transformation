## Written-response rubrics

### Item 3 — Draw a model of energy causing a change (4 pts)
- **4 — Above:** labeled before→after drawing; energy type named; sentence clearly describes the change.
- **3 — Average:** accurate drawing; energy type named; change described.
- **2 — Adequate:** drawing present; energy type OR description present.
- **1 — Inadequate:** no/inaccurate drawing; energy not named; change not described.

### Item 23 — CER short explanation (3 pts)
- **3 — full:** claim + evidence + reasoning, linked; uses supports/shows.
- **2 — partial:** claim + evidence, reasoning weak/absent.
- **1 — minimal:** claim only.
- **0 — none/off-topic.**

### Item 26 — Engineering application (3 pts)
- **3:** names testing (many materials/tries) + redesign (change one thing) + persistence.
- **2:** two of the three present.
- **1:** one present.
- **0:** off-topic or myth ("invented it alone").

## What NOT to penalize
- Spelling/grammar (unless it changes the science).
- "Energy of motion" vs "motion energy" (both accepted).
- "Stored energy" vs "chemical energy" (project term = stored energy).
- Extra correct answers beyond the minimum.
- Using a Core Vocabulary card as reference (allowed).
