# START HERE — Teacher Guide

Grade 4 Science · Energy Transfer & Transformation + Using Natural Resources for Energy
(8.5-week track · 20 students · 5 groups of 4 · 30-minute blocks)

---

## 1. Course overview

Two units, back to back.

| Unit | Title | Weeks | Big question |
|---|---|---|---|
| Unit 1 | Energy Transfer & Transformation | 1–4 | How does energy move and change form? |
| Unit 2 | Using Natural Resources for Energy | 5–8.5 | How do people get energy from natural resources? |

Each unit ends with a written assessment and has a study guide, a clickable
review game, and a teacher key. Everything is print- or projector-ready.

## 2. 8.5-week pacing summary

| Week | Content |
|---|---|
| 1 | U1 Ch1–2 (energy causes change · moving objects) |
| 2 | U1 Ch3 (energy & speed) |
| 3 | U1 Ch4–5 (transfer · collisions) |
| 4 | U1 Ch6–7 + Unit 1 assessment |
| 5 | U2 Ch1–2 (renewable/nonrenewable · fossil fuels) |
| 6 | U2 Ch3–4 (using fossil fuels · nuclear) |
| 7 | U2 Ch5–6 (wind · hydro) |
| 8 | U2 Ch7–9 (solar · geothermal · innovations) |
| 8.5 | U2 synthesis + assessment + corrections |

Day-by-day detail: `Shared/Master Curriculum Map.md` · `Shared/Master Pacing.md`.

## 4. Printing workflow

1. Open `Shared/Printing Guide.md` — it lists every print job, page counts, and
   whether a key should be laminated.
2. Print guided notes and assessments single-sided; staple multi-page sets.
3. Laminate teacher-only keys and lab setup diagrams (binder).
4. Per-student total ≈ 56 pages (notes + labs/activities + assessments + guides).

## 5. Assessment workflow

| When | What |
|---|---|
| **Day before test** | Hand out the study guide for independent / partner / homework review. |
| **Review day** | Play the clickable review game; track with scorecards (guide has 30-min + 15-min quick mode). |
| **Assessment day** | Administer the final assessment (student copy); score against the key. |
| **After** | Corrections against the key; absent students use the recovery index. |

Unit 1 administration note (timing/spillover): `Energy/Unit 1 Teacher Guide/UNIT1_ASSESSMENT_ADMINISTRATION_NOTE.md`.

## 6. Absent student workflow

- Unit 1: per-chapter recovery files live under `curriculum/chapter-0X/student/recovery/`.
- Unit 2: `Natural Resources/Unit 2 Teacher Guide/UNIT2_ABSENT_RECOVERY_INDEX.md` + per-day "ABSENT:" lines in the master pacing.
- Make-up assessments use the same split/timing as the administration note.

## 7. Video usage

- 5 classroom-cut videos are **embedded** in the slide decks (teacher-clicked).
  See `Shared/Video Integration Matrix.md` for exact slide locations.
- 3 additional videos are script-ready (optional, no binary) — placeholders are
  already in the decks.
- No separate loose video files are needed; everything plays from the deck.
