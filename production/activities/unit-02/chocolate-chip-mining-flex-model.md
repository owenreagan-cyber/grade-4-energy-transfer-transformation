# Chocolate Chip Mining — OPTIONAL FLEX MODEL (simplified)

> Unit 2 Wk 5 · **FLEX ONLY** (not core). A simple hands-on model of resource extraction.
> Removed: Mining Impact Ratio, fake precision, "cleaner/more efficient mining" claims, full CER requirement, notebook branding.

## What the model stands for
| Model piece | Stands for |
|---|---|
| Cookie | Rock underground that holds a resource |
| Chocolate chips | The resource being mined (like coal or metal) |
| Toothpick | Mining tools |
| Cookie crumbs | Disturbance to the land |

## What to do
1. Place the cookie on a napkin. **Draw** what it looks like before mining (BEFORE box).
2. Use a toothpick to dig out as many whole chocolate chips as you can.
3. **Draw** what the cookie looks like after mining (AFTER box).
4. Count the chips you removed.

## Think about it
- **What does this model help us understand?** Mining takes a resource out of the ground and disturbs the land around it.
- **What does this model NOT show?** A real mine is much bigger, deeper, and affects more land, water, and air than a cookie can show.

## Teacher note
- Qualitative only — no ratio, no precision, no "better mining" claims.
- Keep the focus on: extraction removes a resource, and the land is disturbed (crumbs).
- Do NOT let students eat the materials.
