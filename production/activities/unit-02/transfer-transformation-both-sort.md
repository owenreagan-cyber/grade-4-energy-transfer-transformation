# Transfer / Transformation / BOTH Sort — 3-Category Card Sort

> Unit 1 Wk 3 · 15 cards · familiar systems · Grade-4 vocabulary only · includes teacher key.
> Categories: **TRANSFER** (energy moves place-to-place/object-to-object) · **TRANSFORMATION** (energy changes form) · **BOTH** (moves AND changes form).

## How it works
1. Print and cut the 15 cards + one category mat per group.
2. Students place each card under TRANSFER, TRANSFORMATION, or BOTH.
3. Discuss: some systems do both.

## Card set (15)

1. A ball rolls across the floor and hits another ball, which starts moving. *(TRANSFER)*
2. A toaster turns electrical energy into heat and light. *(TRANSFORMATION)*
3. Sunlight warms your skin. *(TRANSFER)*
4. A battery powers a flashlight, and light shines on the wall. *(BOTH — electric current → light, then light travels)*
5. You shout, and a friend across the room hears you. *(TRANSFER)*
6. A wind turbine spins and a generator makes electricity. *(TRANSFORMATION — motion → electric current)*
7. A pot on a stove gets hot, and the handle warms too. *(TRANSFER)*
8. A car engine burns fuel to make the car move. *(TRANSFORMATION — stored fuel energy → energy of motion)*
9. A solar panel turns sunlight into electricity that powers a fan. *(BOTH — light → electric current → motion)*
10. A candle burns and lights the room. *(BOTH — stored energy → light/heat, then light/heat travel)*
11. A cup of warm water warms a cooler spoon placed in it. *(TRANSFER)*
12. A hair dryer turns electric current into heat and moving air. *(TRANSFORMATION)*
13. A stretched rubber band is let go and flies across the room. *(BOTH — stored energy → motion, then it moves)*
14. A loud speaker makes sound you can hear across the room. *(TRANSFER)*
15. A hand pushes a swing, and the swing moves. *(TRANSFER)*

## Teacher key

| Category | Cards |
|---|---|
| TRANSFER | 1, 3, 5, 7, 11, 14, 15 |
| TRANSFORMATION | 2, 6, 8, 12 |
| BOTH | 4, 9, 10, 13 |

## Notes
- **Transfer** = energy moving from place to place or object to object (form stays the same).
- **Transformation** = energy changing form (e.g., motion → light, stored → motion).
- **Both** = the system changes form AND the energy then travels.
- Accept reasoned placements; discuss edges (e.g., a speaker = sound transfer after the transformation happens inside the speaker).
