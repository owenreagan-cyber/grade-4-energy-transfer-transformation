# Fast & Curious — Ramp Height, Displacement, and Inferred Energy of Motion (polished)

> Unit 1 Wk 2 · Friday PRACTICE. Students compute averages and sort measured vs. inferred. No invented m/s. No pre-filled speed/energy conclusions — students infer them from the data.

## Make a Claim
Use the data to finish the claim:

The SAME marble rolled from a HIGHER ramp made the cup move **______** (more / less). A faster roll causes a **______** (greater / smaller) change, so it has **______** (more / less) energy of motion.

## Teacher key
| Ramp height | Trial 1 | Trial 2 | Average |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 book | 3 | 5 | **4** |
| 2 books | 7 | 9 | **8** |
| 3 books | 11 | 13 | **12** |
| 4 books | 15 | 17 | **16** |

**Measured:** ramp height, cup push (both read with a ruler/tape).
**Inferred:** relative speed (from ramp height) and relative energy of motion (from the cup push — we infer energy from the change we see).

**Claim:** more / greater / more. (Same object, faster = more energy of motion = greater change.)

> Note: "Relative Motion Energy" is renamed **"Relative Energy of Motion"** throughout. No speed is directly measured and no m/s value is invented.
