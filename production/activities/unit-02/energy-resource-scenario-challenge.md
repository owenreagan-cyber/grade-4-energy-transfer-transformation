# Energy Resource Scenario Challenge — Friday PRACTICE / EXTEND

> Wk 8 · fictional locations · neutral (NOT a policy/community-plan assignment).
> Prompt wording: "which resource would be **reasonable to investigate**?" — not "best energy solution."

## How it works
1. Groups of 4 draw a scenario card.
2. Choose ONE resource that would be **reasonable to investigate** for that place.
3. Write: (a) the chosen resource, (b) **one fact that supports it**, (c) **one limitation they would still need to consider**.

## Scenario cards (print & cut)

**Sunny Valley** — A warm town that gets strong sunlight almost every day. Nights are long, and there is no large river nearby. → reasonable to investigate: **solar**.

**Windy Coast** — A seaside town with steady, strong ocean breezes most of the year. The water near shore is calm with no strong currents. → **wind**.

**River Gorge** — A community sits beside a large, fast river that flows all year. The area has steep hills above the river. → **hydroelectric**.

**Volcano Ridge** — A town near a mountain with hot rocks and steam vents close to the surface. → **geothermal**.

**Coal Country** — A region with thick coal seams underground and a long history of mining. → **coal** (with a note about air/land effects).

**Oil Basin** — A flat region with large deposits of petroleum below the surface. → **petroleum** (with a note about spills/transport).

## Student answer frame (per group)

- Place: ____________________
- Resource we would investigate: ____________________
- One fact that supports it: ____________________
- One limitation to still consider: ____________________

## Teacher key (reasonable answers — accept any defensible choice)

| Scenario | Reasonable resource | Supporting fact | Limitation to consider |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sunny Valley | Solar | Strong sunlight almost every day | Long nights / clouds reduce output |
| Windy Coast | Wind | Steady, strong breezes | Wind can be calm some days |
| River Gorge | Hydroelectric | Large, steady river | Dam could flood land / block fish |
| Volcano Ridge | Geothermal | Hot rocks near surface | Only works in that special location |
| Coal Country | Coal | Thick coal seams | Burning releases substances into air |
| Oil Basin | Petroleum | Large oil deposits | Spills can harm land/water |

> Note: Students may choose a *different* resource as long as they give one supporting fact and one limitation. This is about reasoning, not a single right answer.
