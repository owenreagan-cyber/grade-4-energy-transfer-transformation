## Recommendations
- **Duplex** default for text-heavy (notes, practice, final) to save paper.
- **Single-sided** for anything cut/pasted (notebook inserts, card sorts) and quizzes (staple+collect).
- **Color NOT required** — graphs/charts are high-contrast B/W (verified). Only optional color: ch.1 forms-of-energy icons if the teacher wants a visual anchor.
- **Absent recovery:** print per-chapter recovery packet only for absent students (on demand).
- **Answer keys:** do NOT print for students; keep teacher-only.
