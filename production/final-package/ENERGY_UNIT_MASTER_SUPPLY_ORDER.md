## MUST ORDER vs ON-HAND
- **MUST ORDER (confirm):** play sand · spare AA/AAA/D batteries · enough **identical** marbles/balls · rubber bands.
- **ON-HAND (likely classroom):** flashlight, ruler/meter stick, books/blocks, cups, index cards, tape, scissors, chart paper, pencil sharpener, light switch.

## Removed obsolete/unsafe supplies (do NOT re-add)
- hot plate · wall-outlet / surge-protector / phone-charger experiment · chalk-dust requirement · steel shooter marble · solar-oven default materials · heavy real-door pendulum hardware · glass/steel multi-variable collision trials.
