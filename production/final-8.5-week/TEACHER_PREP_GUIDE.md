# TEACHER PREP GUIDE — Final 8.5-Week Track

> 20 students · 5 groups of 4 · ~30-min daily blocks · Thursday action/lab · Friday FLEX.
> Use with `ENERGY_AND_NATURAL_RESOURCES_MASTER_PACING.md`, `MASTER_SUPPLY_LIST_20_STUDENTS.md`, and `PRINTING_GUIDE.md`.
> All print jobs are listed in `PRINTING_GUIDE.md`; this guide is about **materials + time + recovery**.

---

## WEEK 1 — Unit 1 Ch1–2 (Energy Causes Change · Moving Objects Have Energy)

- **Print ahead:** `01-ch1` guided notes (3 pp), `02-ch2` guided notes (2 pp) — 20 copies each.
- **Gather:** marbles, plastic cups, meter stick (Thu Moving Marbles). Printed sort cards (Tue, FLEX).
- **Pre-build:** 5 Moving Marbles stations (Thu).
- **Charge/test:** project the `01-ch1` and `02-ch2` decks; confirm speaker/audio if a video is staged.
- **Est. prep:** 20 min (print + station setup).
- **Cleanup:** collect cups/marbles; leave projector ready.
- **Substitutions:** any identical small balls work for marbles; stacked books work for a ramp.
- **Absent:** send that day's notes page + journal prompt (see each day's "Absent" line).

## WEEK 2 — Unit 1 Ch3 (Energy and Speed Are Related)

- **Print ahead:** `03-ch3` notes (3 pp), `03-ch3-ramp-it-up-student` (2 pp), Fast & Curious (Fri).
- **Gather:** ramp boards/books, ONE identical ball type, cups, rulers, graph paper.
- **Pre-build:** 5 ramp stations Tue; keep them for Thu trials.
- **Charge/test:** `03-ch3` deck.
- **Est. prep:** 25 min.
- **Cleanup:** store ramps for Fri analysis; recycle graph scrap.
- **Substitutions:** 3 stacked identical books per height (1/2/3) replace a board.
- **Absent:** partner data + Fast & Curious sheet (self-contained).

## WEEK 3 — Unit 1 Ch4–5 (Energy Transfer · Collisions)

- **Est. prep:** 25 min.
- **Cleanup:** collect cars/targets; file sort cards.
- **Substitutions:** any small rolling objects for cars; a domino line for the cradle demo.
- **Absent:** partner data + sort cards (self-contained).

## WEEK 7 — Unit 2 Ch5–6 (Wind · Hydroelectric)

- **Print ahead:** `unit-02-ch5` notes (2 pp), `unit-02-ch6` notes (2 pp), Water Wheel student sheet (3 pp) + teacher guide.
- **Gather:** water-wheel kits (paper plate/lid, blade cups, axle, thread, paperclips), basin, pour cup, towels, water.
- **Pre-build:** assemble 5 water wheels Wed; fill water pitchers Thu.
- **Charge/test:** `05-u2-ch5`, `06-u2-ch6` decks.
- **Est. prep:** 35 min (wheel build + water).
- **Cleanup:** dry wheels; dispose water; dry towels. **Wipe spills immediately.**
- **Substitutions:** a small fan/windmill toy demos wind; a pitcher poured over a spoon demos the wheel.
- **Absent:** partner data + student lab sheet.

## WEEK 8 — Unit 2 Ch7–9 (Solar · Geothermal · Innovations)

- **Print ahead:** `unit-02-ch7` notes (2 pp), `unit-02-ch8` notes (2 pp), `unit-02-ch9` notes (2 pp), solar mini-investigation sheet (1 p).
- **Gather:** small solar cells + LEDs, flashlights (required as indoor backup), Scenario Challenge cards (Fri).
- **Pre-build:** 5 solar kits (Thu). **Laminate** scenario cards once.
- **Light contingency (Thu):** PRIMARY = window or outdoor available light. BACKUP = a consistent indoor lamp/flashlight per group (same lamp, same distance for every trial). The question is "how does available light affect the device," NOT "how sunny is today" — bright/close → dim/far → shaded works with a lamp. Test each kit with the lamp the day before so the LED responds visibly.
- **Charge/test:** `07-u2-ch7`, `08-u2-ch8`, `09-u2-ch9` decks.
- **Est. prep:** 20 min.
- **Cleanup:** store solar kits; file cards.
- **Substitutions:** a calculator solar panel or a solar garden light demos the cell.
- **Absent:** partner data + `unit-02-ch7` p.2; scenario cards self-contained.

## WEEK 8.5 — Unit 2 synthesis + assessment (3 days)

- **Print ahead:** Unit 2 written assessment (student, 3 pp) + key; Detective Chart (reuse); certificates (as earned).
- **Gather:** certificates; assessment keys.
- **Pre-build:** none.
- **Charge/test:** review deck (or `09-u2-ch9` review slides).
- **Est. prep:** 15 min (print + sign certificates).
- **Cleanup:** collect assessments; file keys.
- **Substitutions:** corrections can be done verbally with the key on the board.
- **Absent:** schedule a make-up for the assessment; corrections via key.
