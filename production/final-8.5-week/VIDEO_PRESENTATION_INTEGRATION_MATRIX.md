## Embedding rule applied
1. MP4 is **embedded into the PPTX package** (portable — no absolute local path). 2. Clean poster frame extracted from the video. 3. Small teacher cue ("WATCH — m:ss" pill + subtitle). 4. 16:9 preserved; large frame (not a corner thumbnail). 5. Video is OPTIONAL/SUPPORT — no static content slides were deleted; deck teaches without it. 6. Playback = teacher-clicked (default), no autoplay.

## Post-video question pattern
"TEACH → OPTIONAL VIDEO / WATCH → THINK/NOTICE → continue." One short question only; no per-video worksheet.
