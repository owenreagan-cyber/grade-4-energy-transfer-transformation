#### Water Wheel station setup & containment
- One station per group; every station has: wheel + axle, pour cup (same opening every trial), ruler, basin, paperclips, towel.
- Pour over the **basin only**; keep the basin centered under the wheel. **Wipe spills immediately.**
- Same **500 mL** water per trial; refill from a pitcher, not a running tap.

#### Cleanup / reset / substitutions
- Dry wheels, blades, and basins with towels; dispose water in a sink; respool thread; return paperclips.
- **Substitutions:** paper plate ↔ plastic lid · popsicle-stick blades ↔ paper cups · dowel ↔ straw · paperclips ↔ washers (equal mass) · pour cup ↔ pitcher with a narrow spout.

#### Solar device testing + available-light contingency
- **PRIMARY:** window or outdoor available light if practical.
- **BACKUP:** one consistent indoor lamp/flashlight per group — same lamp, same distance for every trial. **Test each kit with the lamp the day before** so the LED responds visibly.
- The question is "how does available light affect the device," **not** "how sunny is today." Bright/close → dim/far → shaded works with a lamp.
- Keep the panel at a shallow angle toward the light so no one looks directly at the sun; low-voltage cells only.
