## Big Questions
- **L1:** Where can we observe evidence of energy causing change?
- **L2:** What are some forms of energy?

## Essential Understandings
1. Energy is the ability to cause change.
2. Observable change can be evidence that energy is present.
3. Energy exists in different forms: motion, sound, light, heat, electrical energy.
4. A nonmoving object can still have stored energy (battery, stretched rubber band, raised object).

## Student-Friendly Learning Targets
- I can tell how energy causes a change.
- I can observe changes that show evidence of energy.
- I can name forms of energy (motion, sound, light, heat, electrical energy).
- I can explain that an object can have stored energy even when it is not moving.

## Misconceptions to Address
- "Only moving objects have energy" (TG Note to Teachers explicitly corrects).
- "Energy is a visible substance."
- "Stored energy means the object is currently moving."
