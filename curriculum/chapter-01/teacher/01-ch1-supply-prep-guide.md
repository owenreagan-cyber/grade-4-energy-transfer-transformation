## Categories
- **REUSABLE:** flashlight, cup+balloon drumhead, rubber band.
- **CONSUMABLE:** index cards, table salt (pinch), printed student pages.
- **LIKELY CLASSROOM ITEM:** pencil sharpener, light switch, fan/clock, projector, book/globe.
- **MUST ORDER:** none new for Chapter 1 (all standard classroom items). Confirm flashlight batteries on hand.

## Teacher prep
- Monday: gather sharpener + pencils; ready projector.
- Tuesday: print detectives pages; mentally inventory classroom devices students can safely observe.
- Wednesday: build cup+balloon drumhead (stretch balloon over cup mouth; secure with tape/rubber band); ready audio device; check flashlight.
- Thursday: check flashlight + battery; have rubber band + raised object.

## Cleanup
- Return devices; store vocabulary decks alphabetically; dispose of candle/matches safely (if used); sweep any salt; collect student pages for review.

## Safety
- Candle lit by teacher only (if used); keep clear of hair/paper.
- Salt demo — no eating; keep salt out of eyes.
- Battery handling by teacher as needed; dry hands.

## Storage / reset
- Store cup+balloon drumhead flat and dry (reusable next year).
- Vocabulary deck stays in student possession (alphabetical, added to in later lessons).
