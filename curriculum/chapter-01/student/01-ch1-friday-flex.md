## PRACTICE — Energy Charades
| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Time | 15–20 min |
| Materials | scenario cards (teacher-printed) |
| Teacher prep | low — print/cut scenario cards |
| Student independence | guided |
| Printable | yes (cards) |
| Reusable | yes (laminate optional) |
| Concepts reinforced | forms of energy, change, evidence |

**Teacher rules:**
1. A student draws (or is given) an energy/change scenario card (e.g., "flipping a light switch," "ringing a bell," "pushing a toy car," "warming hands near a heater").
2. The student acts it out or describes it safely (no running, no touching).
3. Peers identify **two things:** (1) the energy form, (2) the observable change.
4. Discuss: "What was the cause? What was the effect?"

## PRACTICE / EXTEND — Energy Evidence Sort
| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Time | 15–20 min |
| Materials | sort cards (scenarios + form labels) |
| Teacher prep | low — print/cut |
| Student independence | guided→independent |
| Printable | yes |
| Reusable | yes |
| Concepts reinforced | evidence of energy, forms of energy |

**Activity:** Students sort scenario cards ("ball rolling," "bulb glowing," "ice melting," "bell ringing," "battery in a toy") under form headings (motion, sound, light, heat, electrical energy) and identify the observable change for each.

## EXTEND — Energy Mystery Files
| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Time | 15 min |
| Materials | clue strips |
| Teacher prep | low |
| Student independence | guided |
| Printable | yes |
| Reusable | yes |
| Concepts reinforced | evidence → energy inference |

**Activity:** Give a short mystery ("The room got brighter. The fan started spinning. What energy is at work?"). Students use the ENERGY → CHANGE → EVIDENCE model to solve and explain.

**Alt EXTEND — "What Changed?" challenge:** students find one change at home/around school and bring the evidence description next week (non-graded, optional).
