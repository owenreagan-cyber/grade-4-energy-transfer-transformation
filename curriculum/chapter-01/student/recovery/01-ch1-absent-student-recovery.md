# Chapter 1 Absent-Student Recovery

## Big Question (exact wording)
**Where can we observe evidence of energy causing change?**

## What you missed (the big idea)
**Energy is the ability to cause change. A change is evidence that energy is present.** Energy comes in different forms (motion, sound, light, heat, electrical energy), and an object can have **stored energy** even when it is not moving.

## Step 3 — Use the class data (below)
Our class looked around the room for changes. Here is what some classmates found:

| Object / System | Before | After | Observable change | Possible energy form |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Light bulb | dark / off | bright / on | room got brighter | light (electrical) |
| Electric fan | still | spinning | blades moving | motion (electrical) |
| Pencil in sharpener | dull tip | sharp tip | tip changed shape; sharpener hummed | motion + sound (electrical) |

## Step 4 — Answer (short)
1. What is energy? (Write the sentence.)
2. In the table above, pick ONE row. What **changed**, and what **energy** do you think caused it?
3. A battery is sitting still on a table. Does it have energy? Explain using the word **stored energy**.
4. What is the **evidence** that energy was present in the light bulb when our class turned it on?

## Step 5 — The essential conclusion (copy this into your notes)
```
ENERGY → CHANGE → EVIDENCE
```
Energy is the ability to cause change. When I see a change, that change is evidence of energy.
