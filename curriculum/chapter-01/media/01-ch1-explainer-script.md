# Chapter 1 Custom Micro Explainer Script

**Title:** "What Changed? How Scientists Find Evidence of Energy"
**Target runtime:** 2–3 minutes
**Tone:** Grade 4 friendly · no advanced terminology

---

## 0:00–0:20 — HOOK (everyday change)
> *Visual: a dark room flips to light; a toy car is pushed and rolls.*

"Watch closely. The lights flip on. A toy car starts to roll. Something made each of these things change. What was it?"

## 0:20–0:45 — BIG QUESTION
> *Visual: the words appear, one per line.*

"Our big question this week is: **Where can we find evidence of energy causing change?** First we need to know what energy is. Here is the whole idea in one sentence: **Energy is the ability to cause change.**"

## 0:45–1:15 — EXAMPLE 1 (motion)
> *Visual: a rolling ball knocks over a small block.*

"Here is a ball rolling. The ball is moving, so it has **energy of motion**. When it hits the block, the block falls over. The block changed from standing up to lying down. That change is **evidence** that energy was there."

## 1:15–1:55 — EXAMPLE 2 (light, sound, heat)
> *Visual: a flashlight beam on a wall; a ringing bell; a warm mug with steam.*

"Energy comes in different forms. **Light** from a flashlight changes a dark wall into a bright one. **Sound** from a bell changes quiet into ringing. **Heat** can change something cold into something warm. Each change is evidence of energy."

## 1:55–2:20 — STORED ENERGY
> *Visual: a battery next to a flashlight; then the flashlight lights up.*

"Does a battery have energy if it is just sitting still? It does! The battery has **stored energy**. When we put it in a flashlight, the stored energy is released and the bulb lights up. The light is our evidence."

## 2:20–2:40 — MODEL
> *Visual: three boxes with arrows: ENERGY → CHANGE → EVIDENCE.*

"Remember this chain: **ENERGY causes a CHANGE, and the change is our EVIDENCE.** That is how scientists find energy, even when they cannot see it."

## 2:40–3:00 — FINAL CHALLENGE
> *Visual: a classroom.*

"Here is your challenge: look around you. **What change can you find? What energy do you think caused it?** Write it down, and bring it to our next science time."

---

## Production notes
- Keep captions on for accessibility.
- Grade 4 words only (energy, change, evidence, stored energy, energy of motion, light, sound, heat).
- No kinetic/potential/radiant/mechanical/compression-wave/inertia.
