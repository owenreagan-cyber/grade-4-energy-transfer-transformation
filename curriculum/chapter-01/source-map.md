## AUTHORITATIVE

### Student Reader — Chapter 1 "Energy Causes Change"
- **Printed pages:** 1–6 · **PDF pages:** 7–12
- **Core Vocabulary introduced (Reader page #):** change (1), energy (1), stored energy (4)
- **Content covered:** energy as the ability to cause change; change as evidence; forms of energy (light, sound, heat, electrical, motion); stored energy (battery example); "Energy Can Move and Change" (napping cat / sunlight).

### Teacher Guide — Lessons 1–2
- **Lesson 1 — Energy Causes Change**
  - Big Question: *Where can we observe evidence of energy causing change?*
  - Learning objective: Give examples of energy causing change.
  - NGSS: DCI **PS3.A** (Definitions of Energy); CCC **Stability and Change**.
  - Activities: student observation · reading/discussion/writing · vocabulary instruction · teacher demonstration.
  - Materials: electric pencil sharpener + unsharpened pencil; internet/projector (rocket launch); index cards (3/student, vocabulary deck).
  - AP: **AP 1.1** Energy Scavenger Hunt.
- **Lesson 2 — Forms of Energy**
  - Big Question: *What are some forms of energy?*
  - Learning objectives: Identify as forms of energy — light, sound, heat, electrical energy; Create a visual model that shows examples of energy causing change.
  - NGSS: DCI **PS3.A** (Definitions of Energy); CCC **Cause and Effect**.
  - Activities: teacher demonstration · student observation · discussion and writing.
  - Materials: flashlight (removable batteries); large plastic cup; tape/rubber band; balloon (dark); table salt (pinch); mobile phone/audio device (small speaker); candle; matches/lighter.
  - AP: **AP 2.1** Surprise! · **AP 2.2** Energy Causes Change.
