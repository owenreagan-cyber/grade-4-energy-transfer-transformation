# Big Question(s)
- Where can we observe evidence of energy causing change?
- What are some forms of energy?

# Core Concepts
- energy is the ability to cause change
- observable changes provide evidence of energy
- energy exists in different forms (light, sound, heat, electrical, motion)
- nonmoving objects can have stored energy

# Required Vocabulary
- energy · change · evidence · stored energy

# Supporting Language
- motion · sound · light · heat · electrical energy · forms of energy

# Misconceptions
- "Only moving objects have energy"
- "Energy is a visible substance"
- "Stored energy means the object is moving"
