## PRACTICE — Collision Predictor (CUSTOM)
- **Time:** 10–15 min · **Materials:** printed cards / worksheet · **Independence:** guided
- Given a mini-scenario (same car, higher ramp; same ramp, heavier car; faster car vs slower car), students predict which collision causes a greater change and circle the ONE variable that changed.
- **Concepts reinforced:** variable isolation, prediction from prior observation.

## PRACTICE — Fair-Test Detective (CUSTOM)
- **Time:** 10 min · **Materials:** worksheet · **Independence:** independent
- Given 5 flawed mini-plans (e.g., "change the car AND the ramp at once"), students circle what was changed and fix the plan to change ONE thing.
- **Concepts reinforced:** one-variable-at-a-time.

## PRACTICE — Cause → Effect Matching (CUSTOM)
- **Time:** 10 min · **Materials:** cards · **Independence:** independent
- Match CAUSE (faster car · heavier car · slower car · lighter car) to EFFECT (target moves farthest · next · less · least).
- **Concepts reinforced:** speed/weight → change relationship.

## EXTEND — Newton's Cradle Discussion (EXTENSION)
- **Time:** 10 min · **Materials:** Newton's cradle (teacher demo) · **Independence:** guided
- Watch the cradle; discuss: "Where did the motion energy go?" (through the balls and back). Reinforce: energy transfers, never disappears.
- **Concepts reinforced:** energy transfer, energy does not disappear.

## EXTEND — Everyday Collision Hunt (EXTENSION)
- **Time:** 15 min · **Materials:** printed list · **Independence:** independent
- Find collisions at home/school (ball → bat, hammer → nail, foot → soccer ball). For each: what changed? where did the energy go?
- **Concepts reinforced:** collision changes (motion/shape/sound), energy transfer.
