- **Content covered:**
  - p.25 — collision = two objects make contact; changes occur (speed, direction, shape, sound, sparks, feel)
  - p.26 — "Collisions Involve Changes in Motion" — bowling ball → pins: some energy of motion transfers to the pins; pins gain motion energy, ball keeps less; collision produces sound (motion → sound)
  - p.27 — "A Collision Can Change an Object's Shape" — clay vs brick wall; egg vs floor; cars designed to crumple
  - p.28 — "Many Collisions Make Sounds" — vibrations in objects → air vibrates → energy to ears; sound = energy from vibrating object; "Think About Space" (no air → no sound)
  - p.29 — "Speed Affects Collisions" — "An object moving at faster speed has greater energy of motion than the same object moving at slower speed"; faster → transfers more energy
  - p.30 — "Weight Affects the Changes Caused by Collisions" — "If two similarly sized objects are moving at the same speed, the one with the greater weight will have more energy of motion"; moon craters (heavier meteoroids → deeper craters at same speed)

## AUTHORITATIVE → ADAPTED (explicit, reviewed deviations)

1. **Two-part variable isolation (ADAPTED from AP 11.2).** Official AP 11.2 lets students "change one variable (i.e., height or speed)" in a single open investigation. Phase 7 splits this into **Part A (Speed): same car, vary ramp height** and **Part B (Mass/Weight): same ramp, vary mass/weight**, each with a fixed-set comparison. Rationale: enforce the Phase 7 guardrail that speed and mass are NEVER confounded. Science preserved (speed and weight each affect collision outcomes — Reader pp. 29–30).
2. **Target-displacement measurement (ADAPTED).** Official answer key measures target distance "by using car lengths"; Phase 7 uses **cm target displacement** with 3 trials + averaging (consistent with Phase 5's measured-outcome approach). Depth and loudness are NOT primary data (Phase 6 decision: clay-depth/loudness rejected as primary collision data).
3. **Sound qualitative / heat not measured (SOURCE, emphasized).** TG L10 "Know the Standards 2" states sound is heard and heat is "a very small amount … difficult to measure." Phase 7 keeps sound qualitative and does NOT require heat measurement.

### Locked safety / scope (Phase 7)
- Clear crash lane; cars ROLL (never thrown); fixed release points; Newton's cradle = teacher demo only.
- No momentum/impulse/force calculations, no contact-force/deformation terms, no kinetic/potential/mechanical vocabulary, no heat measurement.
