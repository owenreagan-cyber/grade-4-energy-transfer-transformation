# Big Question(s)
- How is energy involved in collisions?
- What happens when objects collide? How are collisions predictable?

# Core Concepts
- a collision = two objects come together with impact
- collisions cause changes (motion, shape, sound)
- energy of motion transfers to the target in a collision
- COLLISION → MOTION CAN CHANGE → ENERGY CAN TRANSFER → ENERGY DOES NOT DISAPPEAR
- same object faster → greater energy of motion → greater change
- same speed heavier → greater energy of motion → greater change
- collisions are predictable (PREDICT → TEST → OBSERVE → COMPARE → EXPLAIN)
