# 05-ch5-crash-lab-preview-script.md

> Pre-lab preview for the Crash Lab. ~90 s. CUSTOM (planned; not generated).

## Narration / visuals

1. **The question.** — [Toy car, ramp, and cup on a table.]
   "What happens when objects collide? We will test it with a fair test."

2. **Part A — Speed.** — [Same car rolling down 1-book, 2-book, 3-book ramps at a cup.]
   "Part A: the SAME car, the SAME target, the SAME surface. We change only the ramp height — one book, two books, three books. A higher ramp means a faster car."

3. **What we measure.** — [Ruler next to the cup.]
   "We measure how far the target moves — in centimeters. We listen for the sound too — loud or quiet."

4. **Part B — Mass.** — [One car, then two cars taped together, then three, same ramp.]
   "Part B: the SAME ramp, the SAME target, the SAME surface. We change only how much the car weighs — one car, two cars, three cars."

5. **The fair-test rule.** — [Text: CHANGE ONE THING AT A TIME.]
   "The rule: change ONE thing at a time. That way we know what caused the change."

## Guardrails
- No momentum/force; no "measure speed/energy directly" (we measure target movement, infer the rest).
