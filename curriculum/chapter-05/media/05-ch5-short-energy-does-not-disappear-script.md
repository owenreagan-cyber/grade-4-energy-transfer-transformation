## Narration / visuals

1. [A car hits a cup. The cup slides. A CLUNK is heard.]
   "A car hits a cup. The cup moves. We hear a sound."

2. [Text: WHERE DID THE ENERGY GO?]
   "Where did the energy go? Did it disappear?"

3. [Arrows: car → cup (motion); car → sound wave icon; tiny heat icon.]
   "No. Some energy moved to the cup — it moved. Some became sound — we heard it. A tiny bit became heat."

4. [Text: ENERGY DOES NOT DISAPPEAR]
   "Energy does not disappear. It moves or changes form."

## Guardrails
- No momentum/force; heat = "a tiny bit" (not measured); sound = qualitative.
