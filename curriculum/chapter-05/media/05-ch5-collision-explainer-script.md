# 05-ch5-collision-explainer-script.md

> Core concept explainer. ~90–120 s. CUSTOM (planned; not generated). Grade 4 language only.

## Narration / visuals

1. **What is a collision?** — [Two objects meet with impact: car hits cup; ball hits bat.]
   "A collision happens when two objects come together with impact. In every collision, something changes."

2. **Changes in motion.** — [Car hits a still car; the still car moves.]
   "A moving object has energy of motion. When it hits something, some of that energy moves to the other object. The other object starts to move — its motion changes."

3. **Changes in shape.** — [Clay flattens on a wall; an egg cracks.]
   "A collision can also change an object's shape."

4. **Changes we hear.** — [Mallet hits a drum. BONG.]
   "And collisions often make a sound. Some of the energy of motion becomes sound energy."

5. **The rule.** — [Text: COLLISION → MOTION CAN CHANGE → ENERGY CAN TRANSFER → ENERGY DOES NOT DISAPPEAR]
   "Collision. Motion can change. Energy can transfer. Energy does NOT disappear."

## Guardrails
- No momentum/impulse/force calculations; no kinetic/potential terms.
- Sound = qualitative ("becomes sound energy"); heat = "a tiny bit, hard to measure" (optional mention only).
