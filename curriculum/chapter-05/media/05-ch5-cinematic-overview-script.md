# 05-ch5-cinematic-overview-script.md

> Chapter 5 opening cinematic — mystery hook. ~60–90 s. CUSTOM (planned; not generated). Grade 4 language only; no momentum/force/kinetic-potential terms.

## Visual beat / narration

**BEAT 1 — CLAP (2 s)**
- [Close-up: two hands clap. Sound: CLAP.]
- "Two hands come together with impact."

**BEAT 2 — STOMP (3 s)**
- [Foot stomps the floor. Sound: THUMP.]
- "A foot comes down. A collision."

**BEAT 3 — TOY CARS (8 s)**
- [One toy car rolls down a ramp and bumps a still car. The still car moves.]
- "A rolling car bumps a still car. The still car moves — that is a change."

**BEAT 4 — NEWTON'S CRADLE (10 s)**
- [Newton's cradle: one ball swings in; the far ball swings out.]
- "Watch. The motion travels through the balls and out the other side. Energy moved — it did not disappear."

**BEAT 5 — BIG QUESTION (5 s)**
- [Text on screen:] "How is energy involved in collisions?"
- "How is energy involved in collisions? Let's find out."

## Guardrails
- No "momentum," "impulse," "force," "kinetic," or "potential" words.
- Model: COLLISION → MOTION CAN CHANGE → ENERGY CAN TRANSFER → ENERGY DOES NOT DISAPPEAR.
