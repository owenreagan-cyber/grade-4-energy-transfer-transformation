# Big Question(s)
- How are energy, change, and movement of objects related?

# Core Concepts
- motion = an object changing position
- a moving object has energy of motion
- a moving object can cause another object to change
- observable change is evidence the moving object had energy

# Chapter 2 Boundary (locked)
- NO fast/slow · ramp height · speed comparisons · mass · kinetic/potential · transfer/transform · collisions as formal content.

# Required Vocabulary (new)
- motion · energy of motion

# Revisited Vocabulary
- energy · change · evidence

# Supporting Language
- position · moving · observe · predict

# Misconceptions
- "only moving objects have energy" (inverse clarification)
- "energy is a visible substance"
- conflating motion (process) with energy of motion (possession)
