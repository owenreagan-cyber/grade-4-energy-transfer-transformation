### Teacher Guide — Lesson 3 "Moving Objects Have Energy"
- **Big Question:** *How are energy, change, and movement of objects related?*
- **Learning objective:** Create a visual model that demonstrates an example of motion energy causing change.
- **NGSS:** DCI **PS3.B** (Conservation of Energy and Energy Transfer) + DCI **PS3.C** (Relationship Between Energy and Forces); CCC **Energy and Matter**. No PE line attached at L3 in source (PE 4-PS3-1 attaches at L4–L5, speed).
- **Lesson activities:** teacher demonstration (roll one marble into others) · student observation · reading/discussion/writing · vocabulary instruction.
- **Materials:** assorted marbles · index cards (1/student vocabulary deck).
- **Core Vocabulary (blue, new in Ch.2):** motion · energy of motion.
- **Language of Instruction (L3, teacher-modeled):** cause and effect · distance · energy change (plus revisited energy/motion).
- **AP:** AP 3.1 "On the Move".

### Official Activity Page — AP 3.1 "On the Move" (Lesson 3)
- Identify an object that moves often → draw before/after diagrams showing how its motion causes a change → write descriptions explaining the model.
- **Answer Key:** student identifies a moving object; before/after drawings show the change; descriptions include correct before/after reasoning.

### Vocabulary (Reader Glossary, exact)
- **motion**, n. the process of an object changing position (7)
- **energy of motion**, n. the energy an object possesses while it is moving (9)
- **Revisited (Ch.1):** energy (1) · change (1) · stored energy (4)
- **evidence** = Language of Instruction (TG glossary; no Reader page #) — project-preferred student term, NOT Core Vocabulary.
- **position** used on Reader p.7 ("motion occurs when something changes position") — supporting language.

### Standards (unit-level; Ch.2 is Part B, Lesson 3)

- **CCC (L3):** Energy and Matter.

### Known misconceptions (from TG + `docs/planning/MISCONCEPTION_MAP.md`)
- "Only moving objects have energy" (TG Note to Teachers corrects; but Ch.2 focus is the inverse clarification: moving objects DO have energy of motion).
- "Energy is a visible substance" (we observe the change, not the energy).
- Conflating motion (a process) with energy of motion (what the object has).
- Over-extension into speed/mass comparisons — deferred to Ch.3 (do NOT teach here).
