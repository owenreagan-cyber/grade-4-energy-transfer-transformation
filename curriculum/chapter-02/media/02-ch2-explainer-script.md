# Chapter 2 Custom Micro Explainer Script

**Title:** "How Can We Tell a Moving Object Has Energy?"
**Target runtime:** 2–3 minutes
**Tone:** Grade 4 friendly · no speed comparison · no advanced terminology

---

## 0:00–0:20 — HOOK
> *Visual: a rolling ball bumps a block, and the block slides.*

"Watch closely. A ball rolls across the table. It bumps a block — and the block slides away. The ball was moving, and it made something change."

## 0:20–0:45 — QUESTION
> *Visual: the question appears.*

"Here is our question: **How can we know the moving ball had energy?** We can't see energy. So how can we tell it was there?"

## 0:45–1:10 — OBSERVATION
> *Visual: before/after — block still, then block moved.*

"We use a clue. Before, the block was sitting still. After the ball hit it, the block changed position. The block moved! That change is our clue."

## 1:10–1:35 — CORE IDEA
> *Visual: the words "energy of motion" highlighted.*

"Energy is the ability to cause change. The ball caused the block to change, so the ball had energy. A moving object has **energy of motion**."

## 1:35–2:00 — MODEL
> *Visual: three boxes with arrows.*

"Remember the chain: **MOVING OBJECT → CAUSES A CHANGE → EVIDENCE OF ENERGY.** The change is the evidence that energy was there."

## 2:00–2:40 — REAL-WORLD EXAMPLES
> *Visual: basketball into a net (swish); raindrops rippling a puddle; wind spinning a turbine.*

"Think about a basketball: it changes the net. Raindrops: they change the smooth puddle into ripples. Wind: moving air spins the big blades of a wind turbine. Every moving thing can cause a change."

## 2:40–3:00 — FINAL CHALLENGE
> *Visual: a classroom or playground.*

"Here is your challenge: find **one moving object** around you. Describe **what change it could cause**. That change is your evidence of its energy."

---

## Production notes
- Keep captions on for accessibility.
- Grade 4 words only (motion, energy of motion, change, evidence, moving object).
- No speed/fast/slow comparison; no kinetic/potential/force/velocity/mass.
