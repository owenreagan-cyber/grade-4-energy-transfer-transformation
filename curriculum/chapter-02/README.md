# Chapter 02 — Moving Objects Have Energy

## Identity
- **Chapter:** 2
- **Official title:** Moving Objects Have Energy
- **Reader:** printed pp. 7–12 · PDF pp. 13–18
- **Teacher Guide:** Lesson 3 ("Moving Objects Have Energy")
- **Week:** Week 2

## Big Question
How are energy, change, and movement of objects related?

## Core concept
A moving object has energy of motion because it can cause change.

## Chapter 2 boundary (locked)
Chapter 2 establishes **"a moving object can cause change."** It does NOT teach:
fast vs slow · ramp height · speed comparisons · "faster = more energy" · mass · kinetic/potential energy · formal energy transfer · formal collisions. Those are Chapters 3–5.

## Core Vocabulary (new)
motion · energy of motion

## Revisited / project-preferred
energy · change · evidence

## Supporting language
position · moving · observe · predict

## Primary ideas (source-verified)
1. Motion means an object changes position.
2. A moving object has energy of motion.
3. A moving object can cause another object to change.
4. That observable change is evidence that the moving object had energy.

## Model
```
MOVING OBJECT → CAUSES A CHANGE → EVIDENCE OF ENERGY
```
