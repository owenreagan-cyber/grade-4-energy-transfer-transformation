## Big Question
How are energy, change, and movement of objects related?

## Essential Understandings
1. Motion is the process of an object changing position.
2. A moving object has energy of motion.
3. A moving object can cause another object to change.
4. The observable change is evidence that the moving object had energy.

## Student-Friendly Learning Targets
- I can tell what motion means.
- I can explain that a moving object has energy of motion.
- I can find evidence that a moving object had energy by looking at the change it caused.
- I can draw a before/after model of a moving object causing change.

## Misconceptions to Address
- "Only moving objects have energy" — clarify: moving objects DO have energy of motion (and nonmoving objects can have stored energy, from Ch.1).
- "Energy is a visible substance" — we see the change, not the energy.
- Conflating motion (the process) with energy of motion (what the moving object has).
