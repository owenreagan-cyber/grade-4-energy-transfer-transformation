## Categories
- **REUSABLE:** marbles, trays/lanes, rulers.
- **CONSUMABLE:** printed student pages, index cards.
- **LIKELY CLASSROOM ITEM:** flat surface, cone/box props.
- **MUST ORDER:** none new (standard marbles). Confirm enough **identical** marbles on hand.

## Teacher prep
- Monday: ready a flat rolling surface + 2 identical marbles.
- Tuesday: prepare contained lanes; confirm identical marbles per group; print student pages.
- Wednesday: print notes; ready vocabulary deck.
- Thursday: gather example props (cone, box, floating object).

## Cleanup
- Collect marbles and trays; return props; collect pages for review; store vocabulary deck alphabetically.

## Safety
- No projectile-style launching; marbles roll in a lane; hands dry; supervise to prevent marbles leaving the table.

## Failure modes (see lab spec)
- marble missing target · uneven surface · target rolling too far · inconsistent hand pushes · marble leaving table · student launching/competition.

## Storage / reset
- Marbles and lanes stored together (reusable). Vocabulary deck stays in student possession (alphabetical).
