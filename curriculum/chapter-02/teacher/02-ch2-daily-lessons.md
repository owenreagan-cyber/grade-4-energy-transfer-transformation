# Week 2 Daily Lessons — Chapter 2: Moving Objects Have Energy

> Working sequence. Options map to `docs/planning/DAILY_LESSON_ARCHITECTURE.md`. Times assume 40–45 min (see classroom simulation for 30-min notes).

## MONDAY — Moving Objects Can Cause Change (Option A: concept day)
**Big Question:** How are energy, change, and movement of objects related?

1. **Hook / phenomenon (5 min):** Roll one marble across a table so it bumps a stationary marble. Ask only: "What changed?"
2. **Observe before / predict (5 min):** Show the setup before release. "What do you think will happen when the moving marble reaches the target?"
3. **Release + observe (5 min):** Release the marble; the target marble moves.
4. **Ask "what changed?" (5 min):** Collect: target was still → now moving; target changed position.
5. **Connect (10 min):** "The moving marble caused a change. Because energy is the ability to cause change, the moving marble had energy." Introduce **energy of motion** (no speed comparison).
6. **Initial model (5 min):** Build the chain on the board: **MOVING OBJECT → CAUSES CHANGE → EVIDENCE OF ENERGY**.
7. **Exit (3 min):** "What was the evidence that the moving marble had energy?"

## TUESDAY — Moving Marbles Investigation (Option B: lab day)
1. **Question (5 min):** "How does a moving marble provide evidence that it has energy?" Preview the student page.
2. **Safety / setup (5 min):** Explain contained lane; one moving marble, one target at rest; identical marbles; no launching.
3. **Predict (5 min):** Students predict what happens when the moving marble reaches the target.
4. **Test / record (15 min):** Run repeated trials; record: what moved before · what changed after contact · how the target changed · evidence the moving marble had energy.
5. **Debrief (5 min):** "How did the target's change provide evidence that the moving marble had energy?"
6. **Exit (5 min):** "What was the cause? What was the effect?"

**Core result:** the target object changes position/motion. No fast/slow, no heavy/light, no ramp-height comparison.

## WEDNESDAY — Reader + Energy of Motion (Option C: reading/synthesis day)
1. **Retrieve evidence (5 min):** Revisit Tuesday's Marbles evidence.
2. **Preview vocabulary (5 min):** motion · energy of motion (write on board).
3. **Reader section (15 min):** Read Reader pp. 7–12. Pause: p.7 (motion = position change) · p.8 (moving objects cause changes) · p.9 (energy of motion) · p.10–12 (hammer, pendulum, wind turbine).
4. **Guided notes (10 min):** Complete MOTION, ENERGY OF MOTION, MODEL, OBSERVATION vs INFERENCE sections.
5. **Exit (5 min):** "The marble had energy of motion because it was moving and caused another object to change."

## THURSDAY — Apply + Model + Synthesize (Option C→A)
1. **Retrieve (5 min):** "How do we know a moving object has energy?"
2. **Unfamiliar examples (15 min):** rolling soccer ball moves a cone · moving shopping cart bumps an empty box · flowing water moves a floating object · swinging object moves a target. For each: (1) what is moving? (2) what changes? (3) what evidence shows energy is involved?
3. **Example / non-example (5 min):** one that shows energy of motion vs. one that does not (e.g., a book sitting on a table — not moving, no change).
4. **Synthesis + exit (10 min):** Complete notes' EXAMPLE/NON-EXAMPLE, LAB CONNECTION, EXIT REFLECTION. Recite the model.
