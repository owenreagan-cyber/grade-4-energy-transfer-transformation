## Investigation bottlenecks found (Tuesday)
1. **Setup time** — lanes and marble distribution. → Mitigated: pre-set lanes; PER GROUP OF 4; teacher pre-tests surfaces.
2. **Trial reset** — target rolls away. → Mitigated: lane edge / cup stop behind target.
3. **Writing load** — 3-trial table + before/after + explain. → Mitigated: landscape page, ruled rows, Claim+Evidence only.
4. **Marble control** — inconsistent pushes, missed targets, leaving table. → Mitigated: teacher roll or single "finger-flick"; short lanes; no launching.
5. **Safety / behavior** — launching/competition. → Mitigated: "roll, don't launch" norm; redirect to observation.
6. **Fast finishers** — → Mitigated: optional cm measurement + "write a question" / Friday EXTEND.

## Fixes applied
- Identical marbles (no mass/type variation); flat consistent surface (no ramp-height).
- Optional measurement marked "optional evidence," not required for the concept.
- 30-min Tuesday split documented; no change to the locked week at 45 min.
