# Big Questions

- **Unit (AP UR.1):** the 14 unit Big Questions, ending with "What have I learned about energy?"

# Essential Understandings
- Engineers use knowledge of energy to solve problems.
- Engineering is iterative: PROBLEM → DESIGN → TEST → EVIDENCE → IMPROVE.
- Failure/test results are information, not final failure (persistence).
- Edison did NOT invent the first light bulb; he **improved** existing designs (source-supported, non-mythologized).
- Many contributors (e.g., Lewis Latimer) advanced designs; designs improve over time (incandescent → CFL → LED).
- The whole unit is one connected story: energy causes change; motion/speed relate to energy; energy transfers and transforms; engineers use transformations to solve problems.

# Historical Notes (source-verified; see `qa/historical-claims.md`)
- Edison (Ohio, 1847; homeschooled from age 7) designed 1,000+ inventions.
- Did NOT invent the first light bulb — improved earlier designs to be safe, practical, affordable.
- Team tested **more than 6,000 materials**; **1879** bamboo + carbon filament.
- **1901** first alkaline battery; electric-car idea did not succeed at the time (gasoline won).
- **Lewis Howard Latimer** (1848, Chelsea MA; 7 patents; 2 improved the bulb — wire-to-filament securing + filament strength/durability).
- Quotes in Reader: "When you have exhausted all the possibilities…" and "I haven't failed. I've just found ten thousand ways that won't work." (The "10,000 ways" figure is Reader-sourced.)
- **Avoid:** lone-genius myth · invented quotes · "Edison invented the light bulb all by himself."
