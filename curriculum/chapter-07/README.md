## Core concept
Engineering solves problems through a **problem → design → test → evidence → improve** cycle. **Failure is information**, and designs improve over time. Edison **improved** (did not invent) the light bulb, testing more than 6,000 materials; Lewis Latimer and others built on his work. The chapter closes the unit by applying every earlier Big Idea to engineering.
