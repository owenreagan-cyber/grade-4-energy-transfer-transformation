## Notes
- **"10,000 ways"** — the Reader itself contains "ten thousand ways that won't work" as a direct Edison quote (p.41). We quote ONLY that Reader-sourced phrasing and do not inflate it into "10,000 failures of the light bulb" folklore.
- **Latimer framing** — we present Latimer as a collaborator/improver (source-supported), not as a rival or as the "true inventor." No unsourced claim that Latimer's patent made the bulb work.
- **Myth avoidance** — "Edison invented the light bulb all by himself" is explicitly contradicted in items/slides ("did NOT invent the first bulb; improved it"). No lone-genius framing.
- **No invented quotes** — the only two quotes used are verbatim from the Reader.
