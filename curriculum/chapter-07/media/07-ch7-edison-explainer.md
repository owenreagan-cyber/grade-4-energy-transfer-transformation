## Who he was (0:00–0:20)
"Thomas Alva Edison was born in Ohio in 1847. He was a busy, curious boy, homeschooled by his mother."

## What he did NOT do (0:20–0:40)
"Edison did NOT invent the first light bulb. Other scientists and engineers had made bulbs before him."
"Those early bulbs burned out quickly and cost a lot."

## What he DID do (0:40–1:10)
"Edison improved the design: a filament that glows inside glass, safe and affordable."
"He tested more than six thousand materials. In 1879, bamboo coated with carbon worked."
"In 1901 he made the first alkaline battery — lighter and more powerful."

## Not alone (1:10–1:30)
"Others built on his work. Lewis Howard Latimer improved the bulb — securing the wire and making the filament stronger and longer-lasting."

## The point (1:30–1:40)
"Engineering is a team effort that improves over time. Incandescent → CFL → LED."
