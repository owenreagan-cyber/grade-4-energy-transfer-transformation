# Big Question(s)
- How did Thomas Edison use his knowledge of energy transfer and transformation to solve problems?
- What have I learned about energy? (unit synthesis)

# Core Concepts
- engineering solves problems · engineers test ideas · designs need repeated testing · failure provides information · materials and design choices matter · energy transformations make devices useful · engineering is iterative · Edison improved (did NOT invent) the light bulb · historical context over lone-genius myth
