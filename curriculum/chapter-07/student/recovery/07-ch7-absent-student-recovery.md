## Chapter 7 — Edison + Engineering Persistence (concept pathway)
1. **Read** Reader Ch.7 (pp. 37–42). Highlight each design problem and how testing/redesign helped.
2. **Engineering cycle:** Copy PROBLEM → DESIGN → TEST → EVIDENCE → IMPROVE. Under each, write ONE Edison example.
3. **Persistence questions:**
   - What did Edison NOT invent first? (the light bulb — he improved it)
   - How many materials did his team test for filaments? (more than 6,000)
   - What did Lewis Latimer improve? (wire-to-filament securing + filament strength/durability)
4. **Unit concept map:** Center ENERGY; add branches for change, motion, speed, transfer, collisions, transformation, engineering; add one example under each.

## Written Final — Makeup Pathway
