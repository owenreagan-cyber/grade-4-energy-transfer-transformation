# FINAL ASSESSMENT — CORRECTIONS / RECOVERY

> Concept repair, not punitive busywork. For each missed item, complete one row.

## How to correct a missed item
For every item you missed, fill in this template:

**MISSED ITEM:** (number + what it asked) ____________________

**MY ORIGINAL THINKING:** (what I wrote/thought) ____________________

**CORRECT IDEA:** (the right idea, in my own words) ____________________

**EVIDENCE / REASON:** (why the correct idea makes sense — an example, observation, or rule) ____________________

**NEW EXAMPLE:** (one new example of the correct idea) ____________________

## Rules
- Correct ONLY the items you missed (no need to redo correct ones).
- Use the Core Vocabulary deck, your notebook, and the Reader.
- Ask the teacher for help on up to 2 items if you're stuck.
- Corrections are about **learning**, not points — but completing them may earn credit per your teacher's policy.
