Big Question: Where can we observe evidence of energy causing change?

Part A · What changed?

Read each one. Write the change you would observe. Then name a form of energy that fits.

# What happened? What changed? (write the change) Form of energy
1 A light switch is flipped.
2 A toy car is pushed.
3 A bell is rung.
4 An ice cube sits on a warm hand.
5 A rubber band is stretched, then let go.

Part B · Evidence, or not enough evidence?

Circle EVIDENCE if we saw a change. Circle NOT ENOUGH if no change was seen. Then tell because.

Part C · Stored energy

Stored energy can cause a change later. For each one, circle YES or NO. Then say if the change is later or now.

# Example Stored energy? Change is later — or now?
1 A battery in a flashlight that is switched off. YES / NO
2 An apple held above the ground. YES / NO
3 A stretched rubber band. YES / NO
4 A toy car rolling across the floor. YES / NO
