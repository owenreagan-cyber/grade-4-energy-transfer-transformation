### PRACTICE — Energy Path Sort (CUSTOM)
- **Time:** 10–15 min · **Materials:** printed cards (source/target pairs) · **Independence:** guided
- Sort cards into SOURCE → ENERGY MOVES → TARGET for: bell→paper · flashlight→card · sun→your skin · speaker→your ears.
- Extension: write the evidence (the change) on the back of each card.
- **Concepts reinforced:** source/target model, evidence of transfer.

### PRACTICE — Transfer Detective (CUSTOM)
- **Time:** 10 min · **Materials:** worksheet or whiteboard · **Independence:** independent
- Given 5 mini-scenarios, students circle the source and underline the change (evidence).
- **Concepts reinforced:** identifying evidence of transfer.

### EXTEND — Sound / Light Mystery Files (EXTENSION)
- **Time:** 15 min · **Materials:** printed cards · **Independence:** independent
- "Mystery" scenarios: a window rattles when a truck passes; a solar calculator works in sunlight; a shadow appears when you block a flashlight. Students name the source, the change, and whether sound or light transferred energy.
- **Concepts reinforced:** transfer inference from evidence.

### EXTEND — Flashlight Challenge (EXTENSION)

- How many barriers before the target stops lighting? Predict, test, explain.

### PRACTICE — Transfer / Transformation / BOTH Sort (CUSTOM)
- **Time:** 15 min · **Materials:** printed cards · **Independence:** guided
- Sort: flashlight, buzzer, motor, lamp, warm-water setup, sound system → TRANSFER / TRANSFORMATION / BOTH.
- Rule: BOTH is allowed. No false either/or.
- **Concepts reinforced:** transfer vs transformation vs BOTH.

### PRACTICE — Energy Chain Error Detective (CUSTOM)
- **Time:** 10 min · **Materials:** worksheet · **Independence:** independent
- For each chain (e.g., battery → flashlight → light), students: (1) find where energy transferred, (2) find where transformation occurred, (3) fix a planted false statement (e.g., "energy disappeared").
- **Concepts reinforced:** transfer + transformation can happen together; energy isn't destroyed.

### EXTEND — Everyday Device Cards (EXTENSION)
- **Time:** 15 min · **Materials:** printed cards · **Independence:** guided
- Cards: flashlight, fan, speaker, wind-up toy, toaster, solar calculator. For each: what moves? what changes form? transfer / transformation / BOTH? (wording verified before use).
- **Concepts reinforced:** BOTH classification in everyday systems.

### EXTEND — Low-Voltage Circuit Challenge (EXTENSION)
- **Time:** 15 min · **Materials:** battery holder + wires + bulb/buzzer/motor (low-voltage only) · **Independence:** guided
- Make the bulb light AND the buzzer sound together; name the evidence of transfer in each.
- **Concepts reinforced:** electric-current transfer, evidence of change.
