# Absent-Student Recovery — Chapter 4: Energy Transfer

> Structured by concept (Chapter 4 spans two weeks). Use the Reader + setup diagrams + sample class observations/data + short analysis questions. Do NOT pretend absent students performed missed labs — use "Our class observed…"

## SOUND (Week 4)
1. **Read:** Reader Ch.4 pp. 17–19 ("Energy Transfer", "Sound Transfers Energy").
2. **Setup diagram:** cup + pencil + paper target (L-shape), cup a few inches from the paper, NOT touching.
3. **What our class observed:** When we tapped the bottom of the cup, the paper moved even though nothing touched it.
4. **Answer:**
   - What was the source? ___
   - What was the target? ___
   - What changed? ___
   - How is the paper's movement evidence that sound transferred energy? ___

## LIGHT (Week 4)
1. **Read:** Reader Ch.4 p.19 ("Light Transfers Energy").
2. **Setup diagram:** flashlight → beam → target card; optional barrier in the path.
3. **What our class observed:** The card lit up when the beam reached it. When a barrier blocked the path, the lit spot disappeared.
4. **Trace the path:** SOURCE → ___ → TARGET. Then answer: What evidence shows light transferred energy? ___

## HEAT (Week 5)
1. **Read:** Reader Ch.4 p.20 ("Heat Transfers Energy").
2. **Setup diagram:** warm water + cooler object + thermometer.
3. **Sample class data (do not copy — use to think):**

   | Material | Temperature at start | Temperature later |
   |---|---|---|
   | Warm water | 38 °C | 34 °C |
   | Cool metal cup | 21 °C | 27 °C |

4. **Answer:** Which material cooled? Which warmed? What does the temperature change tell you about where the energy went? ___

## ELECTRICITY (Week 5)
1. **Read:** Reader Ch.4 p.21 ("Electricity Transfers Electrical Energy").
2. **Setup diagram:** BATTERY → CIRCUIT (wires) → DEVICE (bulb/buzzer/motor).
3. **What our class observed:** When the circuit was complete, the bulb lit / buzzer sounded / motor turned. When we broke the circuit, the change stopped.
4. **Answer:** What was the source? What changed in the device? What is the evidence electric current transferred energy? ___

## TRANSFER / TRANSFORMATION / BOTH (Week 5)
1. **Definitions:**
   - TRANSFER — energy moves from place to place or object to object.
   - TRANSFORMATION — energy changes form.
   - BOTH — energy moves AND changes form.
2. **Sort:** flashlight · buzzer · warm water → TRANSFER, TRANSFORMATION, or BOTH? (Remember: BOTH is allowed.)

## CER (Week 5)
1. **Review:** claim = statement that answers a question; evidence = observations/data; reasoning = how the evidence supports the claim.
2. **Write a claim** using the model "I know ___ because ___." Choose one: sound, light, heat, or electricity.
   - Claim: ___
   - Evidence (the change we observed): ___
   - Reasoning (how the change shows transfer): ___
