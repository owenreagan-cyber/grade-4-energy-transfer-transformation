# Chapter 4 Supply / Prep Guide

> Per group of 4. Class totals = GROUP_COUNT × amount (see `docs/planning/CURRENT_DECISIONS.md` for the locked GROUP_COUNT; do NOT invent total quantities here). Reusable vs consumable marked.

## SOUND investigation (per group)
- plastic cup (1) — **reusable**
- stretched membrane / paper square over cup (1) — **consumable**
- tapping object: pencil / craft stick (1) — **reusable**
- lightweight paper target (sticky note or thin paper strip, 1) — **consumable**

## LIGHT investigation (per group)
- flashlight with working batteries (1) — **reusable**
- target card / light-colored object (1) — **reusable**
- optional opaque barrier card (1) — **reusable**

## HEAT investigation (per group)
- thermometer (1) — **reusable**
- warm-water container / insulated cup (1) — **reusable**
- cooler sample container (metal or plastic cup, 1) — **reusable**
- access to teacher-controlled warm water — **consumable**

## ELECTRICITY investigation (per group)
- battery holder for AA/AAA/D cell (1) — **reusable**
- batteries (AA/AAA/D cell as holder requires) — **consumable (slow drain)**
- insulated wires with leads / alligator clips (2) — **reusable**
- LED or mini bulb + socket, buzzer, or small DC motor (1) — **reusable**

## Whole-class / teacher
- index cards for vocabulary deck (2 per student) — **consumable**
- chart paper / board markers — **reusable**
- student worksheets (sound/light/heat/electricity + practice + quiz) — **consumable**
- Interactive Science Notebook inserts (1 set per student) — **consumable**

## Prep
1. Pre-fold sound targets (L-shape) and pre-cut membranes.
2. Test every flashlight + battery before the lesson (dead batteries are the #1 failure).
3. Warm water to "warm, not hot" (~40 °C max) just before the heat lesson; keep in an insulated dispenser. NEVER allow students near a heating source.
4. Pre-test each circuit (battery holder, wires, device) before students touch it; mark polarity.
5. Pre-print worksheets and notebook inserts; pre-cut notebook flaps if time allows.

## Cleanup
- Sound: discard paper targets/membranes; wipe cups.
- Light: return flashlights with batteries removed if storing long-term.
- Heat: pour water down a sink (teacher), dry containers; store thermometers upright.
- Electricity: disconnect circuits; remove batteries from holders for storage; coil wires.

## Storage
- Flashlights, thermometers, battery holders, wires, and devices in labeled bins.
- Batteries stored cool/dry, out of reach of students when not in use; count in/out.

## Battery handling (safety)
- AA/AAA/D cells only; no coin cells by default (if ever used: teacher-only, enclosed holder, strict count-in/count-out, ingestion hazard documented).
- No damaged/leaking batteries; dispose per school policy.
- No wall outlets, extension cords, phone chargers, or surge protectors in student hands.

## Water / heat handling (safety)
- Teacher controls water temperature. Warm, not dangerously hot (~40 °C max); no hot plates, no open flames, no student heating.
- Thermometers handled with care (no shaking near faces); glass thermometers only if age-appropriate, otherwise use non-glass digital/strip thermometers.

## Failure modes & fixes
| Failure | Likely cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Sound target doesn't move | tap too soft / target too far / drafts | tap bottom of cup firmly; move target closer; still the room |
| Flashlight dead | drained battery | swap battery; pre-test all |
| No device change in circuit | open circuit / reversed polarity (LED) | check clip contacts; flip LED; pre-test |
| Temperature barely changes | water too cool / too much water | start warmer; use less water; wait longer |
| Confounded sound observation | desk bumps / breeze | "freeze" bodies; shield with a barrier |

## Replacement parts
- spare AA/AAA/D cells, spare LEDs/bulbs, extra wires/alligator clips, spare thermometers, extra paper targets/membranes.
