# Week 4 Daily Lessons — Chapter 4: Energy Transfer (Sound + Light)

> Working sequence. Options map to `docs/planning/DAILY_LESSON_ARCHITECTURE.md`. Times assume 40–45 min (see classroom simulation for 30-min notes). Core model: SOURCE → ENERGY MOVES → TARGET CHANGES (evidence).

## WEEK 4 MONDAY — Sound Transfers Energy (Option A: concept day)
**Big Question:** What evidence shows that sound transfers energy from one place to another?

1. **Hook / phenomenon (5 min):** Tap the bottom of a cup (open end aimed at a lightweight paper target, not touching). Ask only: "What changed?" (the paper moved).
2. **Observe / predict (5 min):** "If I tap the cup harder / move the paper closer, what do you predict?"
3. **Notice / wonder (5 min):** "The paper moved — but nothing touched it. I notice… I wonder…"
4. **Introduce transfer (8 min):** **energy transfer** = movement of energy from one object to another or one place to another. Model: **SOUND SOURCE → ENERGY MOVES → TARGET CHANGES.** Do NOT introduce compression-wave mechanics.
5. **Connect sound (8 min):** "Sound is a form of energy that comes from a vibrating object." The paper's movement is the CHANGE = evidence of transfer.
6. **Notebook cue (5 min):** Insert 1 vocab (energy transfer fill-in) + draw one transfer example.
7. **Exit (3 min):** "What was the source? What was the target? What changed?"

## WEEK 4 TUESDAY — Sound Investigation (Option B: lab day)
1. **Question / prediction (5 min):** "Can sound cause a change without direct contact?" Preview the student page.
2. **Safety / setup (5 min):** cup + membrane/paper + tapping source + lightweight paper target; quiet room; no bumping desks.
3. **Predict (5 min):** "What will the paper do at each distance?"
4. **Test / record (15 min):** tap cup at near / middle / farther distances; record paper movement (clear / slight / none) + evidence. Treat distance as QUALITATIVE unless reliably measured.
5. **Debrief (5 min):** "What changed? What is the evidence sound transferred energy?"
6. **Exit (3 min):** "Did anything touch the paper? How do you know sound caused the change?"

**Core result:** the paper moved without contact — the change is evidence sound transferred energy.

## WEEK 4 WEDNESDAY — Light Transfers Energy (Option B: lab day)
1. **Question / prediction (5 min):** "How can light transfer energy from one place to another?" Preview the student page.
2. **Setup (5 min):** flashlight + target card/object; optional barrier (opaque card) and optional shadow test. No chalk dust required.
3. **Trace the path (5 min):** SOURCE (flashlight) → PATH (beam) → TARGET (card) → OBSERVED CHANGE (lit/bright, shadow, warmth optional).
4. **Test / record (15 min):** shine light on target; place a barrier — what changes? record evidence.
5. **Connect (5 min):** light reaches the target = energy transferred from source to target; students INFER transfer from the change (they do not "see energy").
6. **Exit (3 min):** "What was the source? The target? What evidence shows light transferred energy?"

## WEEK 4 THURSDAY — Energy Transfer Model + Reader (Option C: synthesis day)
1. **Retrieve (5 min):** revisit sound + light evidence.
2. **Define transfer (8 min):** **TRANSFER** = energy moves from place to place/object to object. Preview **TRANSFORMATION** = energy changes form (introduce, do not overdevelop).
3. **Reader Ch.4 (15 min):** Read pp. 17–24. Pause: p.17 (transfer definition) · p.18 (contact vs non-contact) · p.19 (light + sound) · p.20 (heat) · p.21 (electricity) · p.22–24 (hydroelectric).
4. **Examples/non-examples (5 min):** classify "transfer" vs "not an example" using the reader.
5. **Notebook (5 min):** Insert 2 (sound + light transfer two-column page).
6. **Exit (3 min):** "What is energy transfer? Give one example and its evidence."
