# Week 5 Daily Lessons — Chapter 4: Energy Transfer (Heat + Electricity + Synthesis + CER)

> Working sequence. Times assume 40–45 min (see classroom simulation for 30-min notes). Core model: SOURCE → ENERGY MOVES → TARGET CHANGES (evidence).

## WEEK 5 MONDAY — Heat Transfers Energy (Option B: lab day)
**Big Question:** What evidence shows that heat transfers energy?

1. **Question / prediction (5 min):** "What evidence shows energy transferred between warmer and cooler materials?" Preview the student page.
2. **Safety / setup (5 min):** warm water (teacher-controlled, warm not hot) + cooler object/container + thermometer. NO hot plate. NO student heating.
3. **Predict (5 min):** "What will happen to the temperatures?"
4. **Test / record (15 min):** record initial temperatures (warm water + cool object); place cool object in warm water; record later temperature; repeat readings if practical (record in °C).
5. **Debrief (5 min):** "One thing cooled, another warmed — what does that mean?" (energy transferred from warmer to cooler).
6. **Exit (3 min):** "What changed? What is the evidence heat transferred energy?"

**Core result:** temperature changes — the warmer material cools and the cooler warms = evidence of heat transfer. No conduction/convection/radiation vocabulary required.

## WEEK 5 TUESDAY — Electric Current Transfers Energy (Option B: lab day)
1. **Question / prediction (5 min):** "How can electric current transfer energy from place to place?" Preview the student page.
2. **Safety / setup (5 min):** low-voltage battery holder + wires + LED/bulb/buzzer/motor. NO wall outlets, NO chargers, NO coin cells (default). Model: BATTERY → CIRCUIT → DEVICE → CHANGE.
3. **Predict (5 min):** "What will the device do when the circuit is complete?"
4. **Test / record (15 min):** complete the circuit; observe device change (light / sound / motion); break the circuit and observe.
5. **Classify (5 min):** does this show TRANSFER, TRANSFORMATION, or BOTH? (both — energy moves through wires AND changes form to light/sound/motion).
6. **Exit (3 min):** "What changed? What is the evidence electric current transferred energy?"

## WEEK 5 WEDNESDAY — Transfer / Transformation / BOTH (synthesis day)
1. **Retrieve (5 min):** review heat + electricity evidence.
2. **Define explicitly (10 min):** **TRANSFER** = energy moves · **TRANSFORMATION** = energy changes form · **BOTH** = energy moves AND changes form.
3. **Worked examples (10 min):** flashlight, buzzer, motor, lamp, warm-water setup, sound system — for each, name what moves and what changes form.
4. **Sort (10 min):** classify familiar systems as TRANSFER / TRANSFORMATION / BOTH. Do NOT force false either/or — BOTH is allowed and common.
5. **Notebook (5 min):** Insert 4 (transfer vs transformation + classification flap).
6. **Exit (3 min):** "Is a flashlight TRANSFER, TRANSFORMATION, or BOTH? Explain."

## WEEK 5 THURSDAY — Evidence + CER (Option C: synthesis + assessment)
1. **Retrieve (5 min):** "What is evidence? What is a claim? What is reasoning?"
2. **Examples table (8 min):** AP 9.1 adapted — record sound/light/heat/electricity examples with evidence.
3. **CER scaffold (12 min):** AP 9.2 adapted — write a claim using the model "I know ___ because ___." Require Claim + Evidence + Reasoning (stronger CER than Ch.1–3).
4. **Notebook (10 min):** Insert 5 (NOTICE/EVIDENCE/EXPLAIN/CONNECT + CER planning box).
5. **Exit (3 min):** share one claim with evidence.
