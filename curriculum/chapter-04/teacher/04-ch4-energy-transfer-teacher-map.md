## Big Questions
- **Chapter:** What evidence shows that energy transfers from place to place?
- **Week 4 focus:** What evidence shows sound and light transfer energy?
- **Week 5 focus:** What evidence shows heat and electric current transfer energy — and how do transfer and transformation differ?

## Essential Understandings
1. Energy can move from one place to another and from one object to another (energy transfer).
2. Sound, light, heat, and electric current can all transfer energy.
3. The evidence of energy transfer is an observable change in a target.
4. Energy transfer (energy moves) and energy transformation (energy changes form) are DIFFERENT ideas.
5. BOTH can happen in the same system — students classify transfer / transformation / BOTH.
6. Students do NOT directly observe "energy" — they observe changes and infer transfer.

## Student-Friendly Learning Targets
- I can explain that energy transfer means energy moves from place to place or object to object.
- I can find evidence that sound, light, heat, and electric current transfer energy.
- I can tell the difference between energy transfer and energy transformation.
- I can sort systems into transfer, transformation, or BOTH.
- I can write a claim, evidence, and reasoning about energy transfer.

## Misconceptions to Address
- "Energy disappears" (it transfers or transforms, but isn't gone).
- "Transfer = transformation" (transfer = moves; transformation = changes form).
- "Every system is only one or the other" (BOTH is common).
- "Sound is matter moving from source to listener" (sound is a form of energy; we observe the change).
- "Electricity is 'used up' before reaching the device" (energy transfers through the circuit).
- "Heat and temperature are the same thing" (temperature is a measure; heat transfer changes it).
- "We directly see energy" (we see the change, not the energy).
