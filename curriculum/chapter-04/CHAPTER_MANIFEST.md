# Big Question(s)
- What evidence shows that energy transfers from place to place?

# Core Concepts
- energy transfer = energy moves from one place/object to another
- sound, light, heat, and electric current all transfer energy
- evidence of transfer = an observable change in a target
- transfer (moves) ≠ transformation (changes form); BOTH is common
- students observe changes and INFER transfer (never "see energy")
