## Big Questions
- **Chapter:** What evidence shows that energy transfers from place to place?
- **Week 4:** What evidence shows sound and light transfer energy?
- **Week 5:** What evidence shows heat and electric current transfer energy — and how do transfer and transformation differ?

## Core concept
Energy can transfer (move) from place to place or from object to object. Sound, light, heat, and electric current all transfer energy. The evidence of transfer is an observable change in a target. Transfer (energy moves) and transformation (energy changes form) are different ideas — and BOTH can happen in the same system.

## Chapter 4 boundary (locked)
Chapter 4 teaches **energy transfer** and the **transfer vs transformation distinction**. It does NOT require:
compression-wave science · particle-level sound · electron flow · current/voltage calculations · conduction/convection/radiation as required vocabulary · kinetic/potential/mechanical/radiant energy · collision mechanics (Ch.5) · engineering design (Ch.6).

## Safety (locked)
- No student wall outlets, extension cords, phone chargers, or hot plates.
- Heat = warm water (teacher-controlled, ~40 °C max) + thermometer. No open flames.
- Electricity = low-voltage battery (AA/AAA/D cell) + LED/bulb/buzzer/motor. No coin cells by default.
- Sound = cup/membrane + tapping source + paper target.

## Primary ideas (source-verified)
1. Energy transfer = energy moves from one place/object to another.
2. Sound, light, heat, and electric current can all transfer energy.
3. Evidence of transfer = an observable change in a target.
4. Transfer (moves) ≠ transformation (changes form); BOTH is common.
5. Students observe changes and INFER transfer — they never "see energy."
