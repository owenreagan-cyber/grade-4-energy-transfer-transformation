# Lab Preview — Sound

> NotebookLM steering prompt: "Produce a 45–60 second lab preview for Grade 4 students about a sound-energy investigation. Show the setup (cup + paper target) but DO NOT reveal the result. Model a prediction, then prompt students to observe carefully. Use only Grade 4 language. Avoid compression-wave / vibration-mechanics detail. End on a predict question."

## SCRIPT

**[0:00–0:10] SETUP**
[Table view: a plastic cup, a pencil, and a paper target standing upright a few inches away.]
NARRATOR: "Here's your challenge. A cup… a pencil… and a small piece of paper. The paper is NOT touching the cup."

**[0:10–0:25] PREDICT**
NARRATOR: "You're going to tap the bottom of the cup with the pencil — pointing the open end at the paper. Before you tap, make a prediction: **What do you think the paper will do?**"

**[0:25–0:40] OBSERVE (NO RESULT REVEALED)**
[Close-up on the setup, then freeze before the tap.]
NARRATOR: "Watch closely. Keep your body very still — no bumping the table. When you tap, look at the paper. Did it move? A lot, a little, or not at all?"

**[0:40–END] EVIDENCE**
NARRATOR: "Here's the big question: **Did anything touch the paper?** If the paper moved and nothing touched it — what does that tell you about sound and energy? Record what you observe. That observation is your evidence."

**END CARD:** Predict → Observe → Record evidence

## SAFETY / BOUNDARY NOTES
- Keep the room still (drafts confound the result).
- No compression-wave or particle-level explanation — "sound is a form of energy" is the ceiling.
- Do NOT reveal the expected result.
