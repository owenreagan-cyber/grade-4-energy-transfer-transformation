# Basic Explainer — What Is Energy Transfer?

> NotebookLM steering prompt: "Produce a clear, friendly ~90-second explainer for Grade 4 students. Teach: source → energy moves → target changes → evidence. Then briefly distinguish TRANSFER (energy moves), TRANSFORMATION (energy changes form), and BOTH. Use a flashlight and a warm-water example. Use only Grade 4 language. Avoid compression waves, electron flow, kinetic/potential energy. End with a check question."

## SCRIPT

**[0:00–0:10] HOOK**
NARRATOR: "Here's a big idea: energy can MOVE. It can go from one place to another, or from one object to another."

**[0:10–0:30] THE MODEL**
[On screen, three boxes appear: SOURCE → ENERGY MOVES → TARGET CHANGES]
NARRATOR: "Every energy transfer has three parts. A **source** — where the energy starts. Then the energy **moves**. Then a **target** changes."

**[0:30–0:50] EXAMPLE 1 — FLASHLIGHT**
[Flashlight shines on a card; the card lights up.]
NARRATOR: "Look at a flashlight. The flashlight is the **source**. Light moves to the card. The card is the **target** — and it **changes** by lighting up. That change is our **evidence** that energy moved."

**[0:50–1:10] TRANSFER vs TRANSFORMATION vs BOTH**
[Three labels appear:]
- **TRANSFER** — energy moves
- **TRANSFORMATION** — energy changes form
- **BOTH** — energy moves AND changes form

NARRATOR: "Now here's a tricky part — and it's important. Sometimes energy just **moves**: that's **transfer**. Sometimes energy **changes form**: that's **transformation**. And sometimes it does **both at once**."

**[1:10–1:25] EXAMPLE 2 — WARM WATER**
[Warm water; a spoon warms.]
NARRATOR: "Warm water warms a spoon. Heat energy **moved** from the water to the spoon — that's transfer. The spoon's temperature changed — that's the evidence."

**[1:25–END] CHECK**
NARRATOR: "So remember: transfer means energy **moves**. Transformation means energy **changes form**. And sometimes, both happen together. Now — can you find one example of each?"

## SAFETY / BOUNDARY NOTES
- Electricity: low-voltage battery only; no wall outlets.
- Heat: warm (not hot) water; no hot plates.
- No compression-wave / electron / kinetic-potential language.
