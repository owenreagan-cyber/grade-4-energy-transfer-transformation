# Short — Transfer, Transformation, or BOTH?

> NotebookLM steering prompt: "Produce a ~45-second short for Grade 4 students. Topic: 'Transfer, Transformation, or BOTH?' Show three systems — a flashlight, a buzzer, and a warm-water example — and ask students to classify each. Define TRANSFER = energy moves; TRANSFORMATION = energy changes form; BOTH = moves AND changes form. Use only Grade 4 language. End on a challenge."

## SCRIPT

**[0:00–0:08] THE THREE WORDS**
[Three labels pop on screen:]
- **TRANSFER** — energy moves
- **TRANSFORMATION** — energy changes form
- **BOTH** — moves AND changes form

NARRATOR: "Three words. Transfer. Transformation. Both. Let's sort!"

**[0:08–0:20] FLASHLIGHT**
[Flashlight lights a card.]
NARRATOR: "A flashlight. The energy moves from the battery to the bulb… and changes form to light. Transfer, transformation, or BOTH?"

**[0:20–0:30] BUZZER**
[Low-voltage buzzer sounds.]
NARRATOR: "A buzzer. Energy moves through the wires… and changes form to sound. Transfer, transformation, or BOTH?"

**[0:30–0:40] WARM WATER**
[Warm water warms a spoon.]
NARRATOR: "Warm water warms a spoon. Heat energy moves from the water to the spoon. Transfer, transformation, or BOTH?"

**[0:40–END] CHALLENGE**
NARRATOR: "Here's the trick — some systems are only transfer, some are only transformation, and some are **both**. Can you sort them all?"

**END CARD:** TRANSFER · TRANSFORMATION · BOTH

## SAFETY / BOUNDARY NOTES
- Electricity: low-voltage only. Heat: warm water, teacher-controlled.
- No electron-flow / compression-wave language.
- Emphasize that BOTH is valid — do not force false either/or.
