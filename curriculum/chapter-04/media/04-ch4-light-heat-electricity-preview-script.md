# Lab Preview — Light / Heat / Electricity

> NotebookLM steering prompt: "Produce a ~75-second lab preview for Grade 4 students covering three safe investigations: light (flashlight → target), heat (warm water + thermometer), and electricity (low-voltage battery + bulb/buzzer/motor). Emphasize what students will actually observe/measure. Use only Grade 4 language. Show safety rules. Avoid compression waves, electron flow, conduction/convection/radiation, hot plates, and wall outlets."

## SCRIPT

**[0:00–0:08] INTRO**
NARRATOR: "Three investigations. Three kinds of evidence that energy transfers."

**[0:08–0:28] LIGHT**
[Flashlight shines on a card; a barrier card slides in front and the spot disappears.]
NARRATOR: "First, LIGHT. You'll trace the path — from the **flashlight** (the source), along the **beam** (the path), to the **card** (the target). Watch what changes. What happens when a barrier blocks the path?"

**[0:28–0:48] HEAT**
[Warm water in a cup; a thermometer shows the temperature; a cooler cup goes in.]
NARRATOR: "Next, HEAT. Warm water — not hot, and your teacher controls the temperature. You'll measure the temperature before and after a cooler object touches the warm water. Which one cools? Which one warms? The temperature change is your evidence."

**[0:48–1:08] ELECTRICITY**
[LOW-VOLTAGE ONLY: battery holder, wires, and a small bulb. The circuit completes and the bulb glows.]
NARRATOR: "Last, ELECTRICITY — using only a small battery. You'll connect a battery to a bulb, a buzzer, or a motor. When the circuit is complete, the device **changes** — it lights, sounds, or moves. That change is your evidence."

**[1:08–END] SAFETY + CHECK**
NARRATOR: "Safety first: only small batteries — no wall outlets. And never touch anything hot. Now — what will you look for as evidence that energy transferred?"

**END CARD:** Observe → Measure → Record evidence

## SAFETY / BOUNDARY NOTES
- Electricity = LOW-VOLTAGE battery only; NO wall outlets/chargers.
- Heat = warm (not hot) water; NO hot plates/flames.
- No electron-flow / conduction-convection-radiation language.
