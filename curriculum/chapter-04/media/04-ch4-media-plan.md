## Visual / audio examples (Chapter 4)
- **Sound:** bell, cup + paper, horn, firework.
- **Light:** flashlight → target, sun → Earth (careful: "150 million km" is Reader p.19), shadow/barrier test.
- **Heat:** warm water + thermometer, hot tea → cup/spoon (Reader p.20).
- **Electricity:** low-voltage battery + LED/bulb/buzzer/motor; power lines (Reader p.21, not as a student experiment).

## Safety in media
- Show ONLY low-voltage battery circuits — never wall outlets/chargers as student experiments.
- Heat = warm water (teacher-controlled) — never hot plates or open flames.
- No coin cells.

## Boundaries in media
- Do NOT teach compression waves, electron flow, or conduction/convection/radiation terms.
- Do NOT say students "see energy" — they see changes and infer transfer.
