# Cross-Artifact Alignment — Chapter 4: Energy Transfer

> Every artifact must consistently teach TRANSFER (energy moves) vs TRANSFORMATION (energy changes form) vs BOTH (moves AND changes form). No artifact may collapse the distinction.

## Alignment targets (locked)
1. TRANSFER = energy moves from place to place / object to object.
2. TRANSFORMATION = energy changes form.
3. BOTH = energy moves AND changes form (valid, common).
4. Evidence of transfer = an observable change in a target.
5. Students observe changes, then INFER transfer (never "see energy").
