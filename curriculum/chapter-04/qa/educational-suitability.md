# Educational Suitability — Chapter 4: Energy Transfer

> Grade 4, CKSci-aligned, PE 4-PS3-2. Checked against age appropriateness, accessibility, and source boundaries.

## Reading level / language
- Body text Grade 4-appropriate; short sentences; concrete source/target/change framing.
- No compression-wave, electron-flow, conduction/convection/radiation, kinetic/potential/mechanical/radiant, or inertia language anywhere. **PASS.**
- "energy transfer" is the only new Core Vocabulary term; "sound" is taught per TG L6. **PASS.**

## Cognitive load
- One concept per slide (46 slides, two-week chapter). **PASS.**
- Each investigation isolates ONE evidence idea (sound → paper; light → target; heat → temperature; electricity → device). **PASS.**
- Transfer vs transformation vs BOTH introduced gradually: preview (W4 Thu) → explicit (W5 Wed) → sort + quiz. **PASS.**

## Writing / drawing demands
- Generous ruled answer lines (≥2 lines for explain prompts; ≥3 for CER). **PASS.**
- Drawing boxes in sound/light worksheets and notebook inserts (1.3–1.6 in tall). **PASS.**
- Low-motor alternative provided for the notebook classification (draw-a-line instead of cut-and-sort). **PASS.**

## Hands-on safety & accessibility
- Sound/light = no hazards; heat = teacher-controlled warm water (no hot plate); electricity = low-voltage only. **PASS.**
- No wall outlets, chargers, hot plates, open flames, or coin cells. **PASS.**
- All stations doable by a group of 4; rotating roles noted in lab specs. **PASS.**

## Misconception guardrails
- "We do not see energy" modeled on slides 10, 17, and teacher notes. **PASS.**
- "Energy does not disappear" — slide 45 + practice Section D. **PASS.**
- "Heat ≠ temperature" — heat lab + key. **PASS.**
- "BOTH is allowed" — slide 39, notebook Insert 4, practice C, quiz 9–10. **PASS.**

## Verdict: PASS
- Age-appropriate, source-aligned, safe, accessible, and misconception-aware.
