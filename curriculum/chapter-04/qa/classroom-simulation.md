## 30-minute day (bottlenecks + split strategy)
| Day | 30-min result | Fix / split |
|---|---|---|
| W4 Mon | concept day ~32 min | cut "notice/wonder" to 3 min; defer notebook to exit |
| W4 Tue (sound) | lab ~28 min | **FITS** if targets pre-folded (prep saves 3–4 min) |
| W4 Wed (light) | lab ~28 min | **FITS** if flashlights pre-tested |
| W4 Thu (reader) | reader day ~34 min | split: define transfer today; reader pp. 17–21 next day (drop 22–24 or assign) |
| W5 Mon (heat) | **BOTTLENECK** — thermometer reading + water distribution ~33 min | **SPLIT:** predictions + setup day 1; readings + debrief day 2. Pre-fill warm water at stations (teacher dispenses before bell). Use non-glass digital thermometers |
| W5 Tue (electricity) | **BOTTLENECK** — circuit troubleshooting ~32 min | **SPLIT:** assemble + predict day 1; complete + classify day 2. Pre-test circuits; mark polarity |
| W5 Wed (sort) | synthesis ~34 min | cut examples to 6 min; sort as whole-class with cards on board |
| W5 Thu (CER) | **BOTTLENECK** — CER writing ~33 min | **SPLIT:** table + scaffold day 1; write claim day 2. Provide sentence frames |

## Specific attention points
- **Thermometer reading:** non-glass digital preferred; read at eye level; teacher pre-dispenses ~40 °C water.
- **Warm-water distribution:** teacher dispenses into insulated cups BEFORE students arrive at stations (no student near a heat source).
- **Circuit troubleshooting:** pre-test every circuit; flip LEDs; secure clips. Keeps electricity under 30 min.
- **Cleanup:** sound (discard paper) <1 min; light (return) <1 min; heat (teacher pours) ~2 min; electricity (remove batteries, coil wires) ~2 min.
- **Notebook completion:** 5 inserts across 2 weeks — no single day exceeds 5 min of notebook work.
- **CER writing:** sentence frames ("I know ___ because ___") reduce the bottleneck; split if under 30 min.
- **Transfer/transformation confusion:** the Wed sort + slide 39 (BOTH allowed) + practice Section C are the primary de-confusors; reserve a 3-min "BOTH" check at the end of W5 Wed.

## Verdict: PASS (with documented split strategies)
- All eight days fit at 45 min. At 30 min, sound/light fit as-is; heat, electricity, and CER require the documented splits (predictions/setup vs. data/debrief).
