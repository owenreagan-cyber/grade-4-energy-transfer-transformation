## AUTHORITATIVE → ADAPTED (explicit, reviewed deviations)

The official TG Lesson 8 stations use materials that are unsafe or inappropriate for Phase 6 constraints. These are deliberate, science-preserving redesigns (the transfer concept and the evidence-of-change idea are unchanged).

1. **Light (ADAPTED from AP 8.1 Station 1).** Official uses chalk dust to reveal the beam. Phase 6 uses **flashlight + target card/object + optional barrier/shadow test** (no chalk dust required; a teacher demo beam-visualization is optional and low-mess). Rationale: chalk dust is messy/optional, not essential to "light travels from source to target."
2. **Heat (ADAPTED from AP 8.1 Station 2 + L7 hot-plate demo).** Official uses a hot plate + ice. Phase 6 uses **warm water + cooler object/container + thermometer** (record initial/later temperature in °C). Rationale: Phase 6 locked safety rule — NO student hot plates. Teacher controls water temperature (warm, not hot).
3. **Electricity (ADAPTED from AP 8.1 Station 3).** Official uses a wall outlet + surge protector + phone charger. Phase 6 uses **AA/AAA/D-cell battery holder + wires + LED/bulb/buzzer/motor** (low-voltage only). Rationale: Phase 6 locked safety rule — NO wall outlets/chargers/mains for students.
4. **Transfer vs Transformation vs BOTH (EXTENSION/ADAPTED).** The official distinction appears in TG L7 "Know the Standards 1"; full transformation treatment is Chapter 6. Phase 6 teaches the **distinction explicitly** (transfer = energy moves; transformation = energy changes form; BOTH = moves AND changes form) using Grade 4 language, but does **NOT** require "energy transformation" as a formal glossary term (that's Ch.6). This satisfies the Phase 6 mission while honoring the Ch.4 boundary (no conduction/convection/radiation, no engineering).

### Locked safety (Phase 6)
- NO student wall outlets, extension cords, phone chargers, hot plates, open flames, uncontrolled heating. Coin cells avoided by default (teacher-only, enclosed holder, count-in/out if ever used). Electricity = low-voltage battery systems only.
