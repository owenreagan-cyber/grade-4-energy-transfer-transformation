# CHAPTER 6 DAILY LESSONS — WEEK 7 (Energy Transformations)

> Concept instruction week. Aligns to TG Lesson 12 (TG pp. 79–84), Reader Ch.6 (pp. 31–36), AP 12.1. 45-min blocks; 30-min adjustments noted.

## MONDAY — What does it mean for energy to transform?
**Primary question:** How can energy change from one form to another?

1. **Hook (5 min).** Wind up the timer and let it ring. Ask: *What did the energy do?* (stored → motion → sound).
2. **Define (10 min).**
   - **TRANSFORMATION** = energy changes form.
   - **TRANSFER** (review) = energy moves.
   - Show a flashlight: stored energy in the battery → electrical energy → light (+ a little heat). Trace it aloud.
3. **Familiar systems (20 min).** Rotate through flashlight, buzzer, motor/fan, wind-up toy. For each, chant the chain: **INPUT → CHANGE IN FORM → OUTPUT/CHANGE**.
4. **Notebook (5 min).** Insert 1 — Transfer vs Transformation foldable.
5. **Exit (5 min).** One sentence: *Energy transforms when ___ changes into ___.*

**30-min note:** Combine hook + define; drop one demo device.

## TUESDAY — Systems and energy paths
**Primary question:** What energy is present at the beginning, what changes, and what observable output occurs?

1. **Model (10 min).** Draw the chain on the board: battery-powered flashlight → INPUT (stored) → TRANSFORMATION (→ electrical → light) → OUTPUT (light we see).
2. **Device analysis (20 min).** In pairs, analyze 3 devices (buzzer, motor, wind-up toy). For each ask the six questions: (1) What energy is at the beginning? (2) What changes? (3) What observable output occurs? (4) Is there transfer? (5) Is there transformation? (6) Could BOTH occur?
3. **Notebook (10 min).** Insert 2 — Energy System Model (draw one device, label INPUT → TRANSFORMATION → OUTPUT/CHANGE).
4. **Share (5 min).** Two pairs share a chain; class checks for transfer AND transformation.

## WEDNESDAY — Transfer / Transformation / BOTH
**Primary question:** How do I decide whether a system transfers, transforms, or does BOTH?

1. **Review (5 min).** Re-state the two definitions + BOTH.
2. **Sort activity (25 min).** Card sort: warm water → cooler spoon (TRANSFER); flashlight (BOTH); falling dominoes (BOTH); buzzer (BOTH); motor (BOTH); toy-car collision (BOTH); a battery sitting still (stored energy — neither, yet). Classify carefully; state the system boundary before each card.
3. **Discuss ambiguity (10 min).** *Why is BOTH so common?* Energy often moves AND changes form in the same system. Do not force either/or.
4. **Exit (5 min).** One card each: name the verdict + one reason.

**30-min note:** Pre-sort half the cards as a whole group; students sort the rest.

## THURSDAY — Reader + application + checkpoint
**Primary question:** How can an energy transformation be useful?

1. **Read (15 min).** Reader Ch.6 (pp. 31–36): toaster, generators, batteries, solar cells, engineering design. Pause at captions (problem → solution).
2. **Vocabulary (5 min).** convert/conversion, energy transformation, engineering design (index cards).
3. **Design-thinking bridge (10 min).** *A classroom needs a signal without using wall electricity. What energy change could help?* (brainstorm only — no building).
4. **Checkpoint (10 min).** AP 12.1 (Lesson 12 Check).
5. **Exit (5 min).** *How can a transformation be useful?* (one sentence).
