# CHAPTER 6 ENGINEERING STUDENT EXEMPLAR

> A worked example of the Week 8 tabletop signal-device capstone, for teacher modeling / scoring calibration. Uses the previously reviewed pendulum-bell concept **only as inspiration** — rebuilt as a safe, contained **tabletop model door/gate** trigger.

> **Teacher note (when to show this):** Do NOT show this before students have proposed their own designs (it would cause copying). Best used **after** designs are proposed, during scoring/modeling, or for absent/struggling students who need a provided prototype diagram.

## Design used
**"Bell Swing"** — a craft-stick arm hangs from a string on a cardboard post. A rubber band (stored energy) is released by a model door/gate (a small cardboard flap that swings open). The flap releases the arm, the arm swings down, and a washer on the arm strikes a metal cup (bell) — producing a clear **sound** signal.

## Safety (contained)
- Tabletop model door/gate (a cardboard flap on a book), NOT a real classroom door.
- No heavy bolt swinging in a doorway; no wall-mounted cowbell.
- Rubber band stretched gently, never aimed at a person.
- Small washer swings a short distance inside the tray footprint.

## Controlled reasoning (no "heavier = more energy")
- **Claim:** "Changing the attached mass changed the loudness of our signal."
- **Controlled evidence:** "We kept the release setup the same and changed only the attached mass (one washer → two washers). In our test, the signal became louder."
- **Reasoning:** "More mass moving the same way delivered more energy of motion to the cup, so the sound was louder."
- **Language:** "supports / evidence" — **NOT** "proves." We never claim heavier is automatically better; we only report what we observed under controlled conditions.

## Energy pathway
```
STORED ENERGY (stretched rubber band) → ENERGY OF MOTION (swinging arm + washer) → SOUND (metal cup rings)
```
Transfer: energy of motion moves from the arm to the cup (transfer). Transformation: energy of motion becomes sound (transformation). **BOTH.**

## Test → Change → Retest (evidence-based)
| | Test 1 | Test 2 |
|---|---|---|
| Change | (baseline) 1 washer | +1 washer (same release) |
| Trigger | worked | worked |
| Output | soft "tick" | clear "ding" |
| Evidence | cup barely moved | cup rang louder, longer |

**Redesign rationale:** Test 1 showed the arm struck the cup too softly. We changed ONE thing (mass) and kept everything else the same. Test 2 evidence supported our prediction.

## Final evaluation
- Met criteria: responded to trigger ✓ · observable sound signal ✓ · one transformation ✓ · repeated (3/3 trials) ✓ · explained pathway ✓.
- Met constraints: supplied materials only ✓ · non-electrical ✓ · tabletop footprint ✓.

- Use it to show the difference between **evidence** ("the cup rang louder") and **unsupported claim** ("heavier things are better").
