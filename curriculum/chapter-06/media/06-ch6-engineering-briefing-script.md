"Design a non-electrical tabletop device that makes a clear signal when a model door/gate moves."

## Criteria (0:20–0:40)
"Your device must: respond to the trigger · make a clear signal (sound, motion, or visible) · show one energy transformation · work again and again · let you explain the energy pathway."

## Constraints (0:40–1:00)
"You may use only the provided materials. No wall electricity, no flame, no hot plate, no launching. Keep it on the tabletop."

## Test and improve (1:00–1:25)
"Build. Test. Record your evidence. Change ONE thing based on evidence. Test again."
"A failed test is data — not a penalty."

## Energy pathway (1:25–1:40)
"Know your chain: INPUT → TRANSFORMATION → OUTPUT. Be ready to explain it."
