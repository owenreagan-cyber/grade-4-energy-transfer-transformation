# 06-CH6 MEDIA SCRIPT — EXPLAINER: "Transfer, Transformation, or BOTH?"

> Type: EXPLAINER · Role: Core concept — the three-way model · Status: PENDING (script only)

## Definitions (0:00–0:30)
"Energy can do two things."
- "**Transfer** — energy MOVES from one object or place to another. Warm water warms a cool spoon: heat moves."
- "**Transformation** — energy CHANGES FORM. A flashlight changes stored energy into light."

## BOTH (0:30–0:50)
"Most systems do BOTH."
"Falling dominoes: motion transfers down the line AND a little motion becomes sound."
"A toy car hits a cup: motion transfers to the cup AND becomes sound."

## Rule (0:50–1:05)
"Ask: Did energy MOVE? Did energy CHANGE FORM? If yes to both — it's BOTH."

## Retrieval (1:05–1:20)
"Warm spoon? Transfer. Flashlight? Both. Buzzer? Both."
