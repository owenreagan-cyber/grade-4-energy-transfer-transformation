# CHAPTER 6 ABSENT STUDENT RECOVERY

> Two pathways: Week 7 (concept) and Week 8 (engineering). Do NOT pretend an absent student physically built/tested a device.

## Week 7 — Energy Transformations (concept pathway)
1. **Read** Reader Ch.6 (pp. 31–36). Highlight each device and its energy change.
2. **Diagram** three systems using INPUT → TRANSFORMATION → OUTPUT/CHANGE (flashlight, buzzer, wind-up toy).
3. **Classify** as TRANSFER / TRANSFORMATION / BOTH: warm water → cooler spoon; flashlight; falling dominoes; toy-car collision.
4. **Short explanation:** *How can changing energy from one form to another solve a problem?*
5. Complete notebook inserts 1–3 (see `production/student-notebook/chapter-06/`).

## Week 8 — Engineering (alternate design-evaluation pathway)
If a student misses build/test time, use a legitimate alternate pathway (no invented build/test claims):

1. **Provided prototype diagrams.** Give the student a labeled diagram of a sample device (see `teacher/06-ch6-engineering-student-exemplar.md` for the "Bell Swing" concept).
2. **Class test data.** Share the class's actual Test 1 / Test 2 results for that design.
3. **Design-evaluation task.** The student writes: the problem, criteria, constraints, the device's energy pathway, and whether the evidence shows the redesign helped (and why).
4. **Redesign proposal.** Propose ONE further change with a reason and a prediction.
5. **Energy-path explanation.** Trace the device's INPUT → TRANSFORMATION → OUTPUT/CHANGE.

**Honesty note:** Label the work clearly as "design evaluation (from class data)" — not as the student's own build.
