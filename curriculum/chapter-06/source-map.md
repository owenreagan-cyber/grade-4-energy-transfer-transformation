## AUTHORITATIVE → ADAPTED (explicit, reviewed deviations)

1. **Capstone device = tabletop alert/signal device (ADAPTED from TG L13 Scenario 2).** The source default is a **solar oven** (Scenario 1, weather-dependent) and a **wired doorbell light** (Scenario 2, real door + wall electricity). Phase 8 makes the default a **non-electrical tabletop signal device** that produces a clear signal (sound / motion / visible signal) when a **model door/gate/trigger** moves. Rationale: no weather requirement, no wall electricity, no heavy hanging objects near real doors, no open flame/hot plate. The energy-transformation requirement (demonstrate at least one transformation) is preserved from the source. Solar oven is retained only as an optional teacher-choice EXTENSION.
2. **Non-electrical constraint (ADAPTED).** The source Scenario 2 "wires a light to the doorbell" (electrical). Phase 8's tabletop device is **non-electrical** so the transformation is mechanical/stored→motion/sound (safe, low-prep, reusable). This honors the source's materials list (springs, rubber bands, string, sticks, cups, blocks) and the ETS1.A constraint concept while removing wall electricity and hot-plate risk.
3. **"Prove/disprove" → "support" (SOURCE, reworded).** AP 13.5 clue 15 and the supplemental slideshow both use "prove." Phase 8 standardizes **evidence supports an idea** (evidence ≠ proof) across all student-facing artifacts.

### Locked safety / scope (Phase 8)
- No open flame, no hot plate, no wall electricity, no projectile launching, no heavy swinging door hardware, no real-door mounting.
- No kinetic/potential/mechanical/radiant energy vocabulary; no joules/power/efficiency; no voltage/current calculations; no electron flow.
- No solar oven as the default capstone; no invented test data; no fake "successful prototype" results.
