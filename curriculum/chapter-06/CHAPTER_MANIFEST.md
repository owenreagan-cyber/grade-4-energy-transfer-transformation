# Big Question(s)
- How do energy transformations help people?
- How can I design a device that transforms energy to solve a problem?

# Core Concepts
- energy can TRANSFER (move) and TRANSFORM (change form)
- most systems do BOTH
- INPUT → CHANGE IN FORM → OUTPUT / OBSERVABLE CHANGE
- engineered devices use energy transformations to solve problems
- success judged against CRITERIA under CONSTRAINTS
- DESIGN → BUILD → TEST → REFINE
- test results (including failures) are useful evidence
- evidence SUPPORTS an idea (evidence ≠ proof)
