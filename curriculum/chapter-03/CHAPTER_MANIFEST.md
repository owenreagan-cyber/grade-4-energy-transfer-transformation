# Big Question(s)
- How are energy and speed related?

# Core Concepts
- speed = a measurement of the distance an object travels over an amount of time
- a moving object has energy of motion
- for the SAME object, faster motion → greater energy of motion
- greater energy of motion → greater observed change (the change is the evidence)
- students measure the change, NOT speed/energy; the relationship is INFERRED

# Chapter 3 Boundary (locked)

# Misconceptions
- "ramp height = speed" (height is what we change; speed is inferred)
- "we measured energy/speed" (we measured the change)
- "a heavier ball has more energy" (mass confound — out of scope)
- "energy is a visible substance" (we see the change)
