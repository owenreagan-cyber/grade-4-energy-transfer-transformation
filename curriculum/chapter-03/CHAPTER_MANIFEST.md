## Scope

- **Big Question:** How are energy and speed related?
- **Required new vocabulary:** speed
- **Required relationship:** for the **same object**, faster motion is related
  to greater energy of motion; a greater observed change is the evidence.
- Students measure ramp height, cup movement, drop height, or crater width.
  They do not claim to have measured speed or energy quantitatively.
- Required content is taught Monday–Thursday. Friday is recovery, review,
  completion, quiz/makeup, or optional FLEX only.
