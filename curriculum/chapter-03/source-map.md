## AUTHORITATIVE → ADAPTED (explicit, reviewed deviations)

These are deliberate Phase 5 refinements, recorded so nothing is silently overridden. The science (SAME OBJECT → faster motion → greater energy of motion → greater observed change; measured-vs-inferred) is unchanged.

1. **Ramp It Up! 2.0 (ADAPTED from AP 4.1).** The official AP uses 2 qualitative trials (low vs. steeper ramp) with markers ("farther than / away from"; TG SUPPORT: "speed and distance are not measured in precise units"). Phase 5 adds **3 ramp heights (1/2/3 books), 3 repeated trials, centimeter cup displacement, averaging, and a graph.** Rationale: displacement is a genuine observable; the graph axes are both **measured** variables (ramp height, cup displacement) — never "speed vs. energy." This is a rigor upgrade, not a claim of measuring speed or energy.
2. **Drop It (ADAPTED from AP 4.2).** The official AP uses body-relative heights (knee-height / waist-height). Phase 5 uses **fixed heights (30 cm / 60 cm, optional 90 cm)** and records **crater width in cm**. Rationale: body-relative heights vary by student size and undermine the fair test; fixed heights + cm width preserve the same science (higher → faster → bigger crater) with a more controlled setup. Depth stays qualitative unless the source requires otherwise (it does not).

### Locked measured-vs-inferred boundary (both investigations)
- **MEASURED:** ramp height (books) · cup displacement (cm) · drop height (cm) · crater width (cm).
- **INFERRED:** relative speed · the speed↔energy relationship · any "energy" quantity. Students must NOT claim they measured speed or energy.
