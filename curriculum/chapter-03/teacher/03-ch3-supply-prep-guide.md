## Categories
- **REUSABLE:** balls, ramps, books/blocks, cups, meter sticks, sand trays, height guides.
- **CONSUMABLE:** printed student pages, index cards, graph paper.
- **LIKELY CLASSROOM ITEM:** flat surface, lane.
- **MUST ORDER:** play sand (if not on hand); confirm **identical** balls and enough books/blocks.

## Teacher prep
- Monday: ready one ball + target; prepare vocabulary deck.
- Tuesday: assemble ramp + 3-height book sets per group; mark tape start points; print student pages.
- Wednesday: have Ramp It Up graph pages and Readers ready.
- Thursday: set up sand trays; mark fixed-height guides (30/60/90 cm); print student pages.

## Cleanup
- Collect balls/ramps/books/cups; smooth and cover sand trays; collect pages; store vocabulary deck alphabetically.

## Safety
- No projectile launching; balls roll in lanes; hands dry; supervise sand trays (no throwing sand); fixed-height guides keep drops consistent and prevent over-reaching.

## Failure modes (see lab specs)
- ramp slips · ball misses cup · cup tips over · inconsistent release · sand spills · crater edges collapse · measuring from inconsistent points.

## Storage / reset
- Ramp kits (ball, ramp, books, cup, ruler) stored together; sand trays covered; vocabulary deck stays with students (alphabetical).
