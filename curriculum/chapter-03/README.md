## Big Question
How are energy and speed related?

## Core concept
For the SAME object, faster motion is associated with greater energy of motion — and greater energy of motion can cause a greater change (the change is the evidence).

## Chapter 3 boundary (locked)
Chapter 3 establishes the **same-object speed↔energy relationship**. It does NOT teach:
mass comparisons · collisions · energy transfer/transformation · engineering · kinetic/potential/mechanical energy · velocity/acceleration/momentum/force.

## Primary ideas (source-verified)
1. Speed is a measurement of the distance an object travels over an amount of time.
2. A moving object has energy of motion.
3. For the SAME object, faster motion is associated with greater energy of motion.
4. Greater energy of motion can cause a greater observed change.
5. Students measure the change, NOT speed or energy — the speed↔energy relationship is INFERRED.

## Model
```
SAME OBJECT → FASTER MOTION → GREATER ENERGY OF MOTION → GREATER OBSERVED CHANGE
```

## Measured vs inferred (locked)
- **MEASURED:** ramp height · cup displacement · drop height · crater width.
- **INFERRED:** relative speed · the speed↔energy relationship.
- Graph title: **"Ramp Height vs. Average Cup Displacement"** — never "Speed vs. Energy."
