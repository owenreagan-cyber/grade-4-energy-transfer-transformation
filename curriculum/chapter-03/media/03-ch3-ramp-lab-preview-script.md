# Chapter 3 Custom Lab Preview Script

**Title:** "Ramp It Up! — What Are We Actually Measuring?"
**Target runtime:** 1–2 minutes
**Tone:** setup-focused, Grade 4 friendly · fair test · no advanced vocabulary

---

## 0:00–0:20 — HOOK
> *Visual: ramp + books + cup setup on a table.*

"Today we're going to investigate a question: **How does ramp height affect the change caused by the same ball?**"

## 0:20–0:50 — THE FAIR TEST
> *Visual: three labeled boxes — WE CHANGE / WE KEEP THE SAME / WE MEASURE.*

"To make it a fair test, we change ONE thing and keep everything else the same. We **change** the ramp height: 1 book, 2 books, 3 books. We **keep the same**: the ball, the cup, the surface, and where we release the ball."

## 0:50–1:15 — WHAT WE MEASURE
> *Visual: ruler measuring how far the cup moved.*

"We **measure** how far the cup moves — in centimeters. That's the change the ball causes."

## 1:15–1:40 — THE IMPORTANT REMINDER
> *Visual: a caution sign over "speed" and "energy."*

"One important reminder: we are NOT measuring the ball's speed, and we are NOT measuring its energy. We are measuring the change the ball causes. We'll use that pattern to INFER the relationship between speed and energy of motion."

## 1:40–1:55 — GO
> *Visual: the prediction prompt.*

"Before you begin — predict: which ramp height will move the cup the farthest? Then test it. Release the ball — no pushing — and record your measurements."

---

## Production notes
- Keep captions on for accessibility.
- Grade 4 words only (fair test, change, keep the same, measure, ramp height, cup displacement, infer).
- Do NOT tell students the expected result in advance.
- No kinetic/potential/velocity/mass language.
