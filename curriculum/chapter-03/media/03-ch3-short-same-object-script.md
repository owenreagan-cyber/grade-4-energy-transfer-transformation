"Same ball. Different speed."

## 0:10–0:30

"The slow ball causes a small change. The fast ball causes a big change. The faster ball has greater energy of motion."

## 0:30–0:50
> *Visual: the chain — SAME OBJECT → FASTER → GREATER ENERGY → GREATER CHANGE.*

"Remember the rule: it only works when the object stays the SAME. Change the ball, and you've changed too many things."

## 0:50–0:60
> *Visual: the flip-the-flap question.*

"Did we measure speed or energy? No — we measured the change and inferred the relationship."
