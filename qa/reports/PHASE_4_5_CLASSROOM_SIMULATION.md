# Phase 4.5 Classroom Simulation — Interactive Science Notebook

> Simulates notebook distribution, trimming, gluing, and slide modeling within a lesson. Simulated at 30-min and 45-min periods.

## Assembly-time targets (measured design, not wall-clock measurement)
| Element | Target | Design result |
|---|---|---|
| Flat insert (trim + glue) | 2–4 min | ~3 min (one straight trim + one top-edge glue) |
| Simple flap (trim + fold + glue) | 4–7 min | ~5 min (fold on dashed line + glue top edge) |

No single element approaches 10+ min assembly.

## Failure-mode watch (addressed)
- **Scissors distraction** → single straight trim per insert; pre-cut option for motor support.
- **Glue delays** → top-edge glue only; no multi-piece placement.
- **Copying drawings exactly** → cue slides label drawings as "YOUR example"; ideas only.
- **Assembly takeover** → each notebook moment ≤7 min; assembly bundled with instruction, not separate.
- **Drawing too long** → drawing boxes sized for a quick sketch; "draw one" limits scope.
- **Losing small pieces** → no loose pieces; every flap is attached to its insert.

## Cleanup / transition
- Glue sticks returned before the model discussion resumes; evidence tables are the "stopping point" signal.
