## Omission guidance

All three are optional. If time is short: skip every video; the required science is fully taught by the canonical slides + live demos + Guided Notes. Video must never displace required notes, the BQ return, or the Friday quiz-by-minute-12 clock.

## Why nothing is embedded

Every candidate video was content-audited (frames + OCR). Most were **wrong-chapter** (collisions, wind turbines, renewable grid) or **off-scope terminology** ("acoustic output", "baseline inventory", "electrical to light", "proves"); the rest have **unverified narration** (no transcription tool available). Per the phase directive, no unverified clip may be embedded. The deck therefore ships **without embedded media**; the three on-scope clips are provided as standalone fallbacks.
