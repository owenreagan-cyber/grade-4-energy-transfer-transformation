## Added runtime (only if the teacher opts to play)

| Video | Slide | Duration | Day | Core vs FLEX |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `Stored_Energy.mp4` | 25 (after STORED→RELEASE→CHANGE trace) | 4:37 | Tue | OPTIONAL reinforcement — not required |

- **Monday core video time: 0.** Tuesday core video time: **0 required** (up to +4:37 optional).
  Friday optional video time: 0 (the portrait `Why_Still_Objects` is an un-embedded FLEX option only).
- The video does **not** extend the *required* Tuesday block: slide 25 is a 2-minute required slide,
  and the video is a teacher-choice add-on inside the existing 5.5-minute buffer (or skipped).

## Guardrails

- Video is **teacher-controlled, click-to-play, no autoplay, no auto-advance**.
- Guided Notes are not completed "while a video plays" — the video is a quick reinforcement *after*
  the model trace; N3 write (slide 26) remains a separate, dedicated writing task.
- **No-video fallback keeps the lesson intact:** the STORED→RELEASE→CHANGE model + live
  battery / rubber-band / raised-apple demos already teach N3. If the video is skipped, zero minutes
  are lost and no objective is missed.
- Friday first-exposure rule is unaffected (no Friday slide gained new content; the video is Tuesday).

## Conclusion

Pacing is **unchanged from v4/v5**. The single embedded video is an optional, buffer-contained
reinforcement; it can never push any session past its required-minute budget and never displaces
required notes, the BQ return, or the Friday quiz-by-minute-12 clock.
