# Chapter 1 — Teacher Media Operation & Fallback Guide (Phase 5H)

Candidate `ch1-premium-hybrid-v6-embedded-media`. One genuinely embedded, OPTIONAL video.

## The one embedded video

| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|
| Video | `Stored_Energy.mp4` (4:37, 16:9, H.264 + AAC) |
| Slide | **25 — "Stored energy can cause a change later"** (STORED→RELEASE→CHANGE) |
| Location on slide | bottom-left player (under the rubber-band photo) |
| Behavior | **click-to-play**, no autoplay, no auto-advance |
| Poster | still frame from the video (battery/flashlight) — shows before playing |

## How to operate

1. Teach slide 25 normally (trace STORED → RELEASE → CHANGE).
2. **Optional:** click the video thumbnail to play the 4:37 stored-energy reinforcement
   (battery → bulb later; stretched rubber band; raised apple because of its position).
3. The slide stays put; you advance manually when ready.
4. Skip anytime — press the stop control or simply advance.

## Pre-view checklist (one time, before class)

- Confirm the video opens and plays on your classroom machine (PowerPoint Windows/macOS).
- Confirm audio is audible from the back of the room.
- Note the two minor wording points you may want to frame aloud: the video calls the *limp* rubber
  band "zero stored energy" (correct for elastic stored energy — the *stretched* band stores it), and
  says the stretched band is "ready to change into motion and sound" (colloquial — it *releases* and
  produces motion + sound; it is not a formal energy transformation).

## No-video fallback (always available)

If the video does not play (or you prefer not to use it), **skip it — nothing is lost**:

- The editable **STORED → RELEASE → CHANGE** model on slide 25 already teaches the concept.
- Live demos cover every example: a still **battery** in a flashlight, a **stretched rubber band**,
  and a **raised apple** (position) — slides 23–25.
- Guided Notes N3 (slides 26–27) proceed unchanged.

## Optional (un-embedded) alternate

`Why_Still_Objects_Secretly_Have_Energy.mp4` (1:16, portrait) is content-clean but **not embedded**
(portrait orientation + it directly answers the slide-23 prediction). If you want it, play it
externally as a Friday-FLEX reinforcement **after** slide 25 — never before the N3 prediction.

## Rule

No required learning depends on video. Video is a teacher-choice reinforcement inside the existing
session buffer; it never displaces required notes, the Big Question return, or the Friday quiz.
