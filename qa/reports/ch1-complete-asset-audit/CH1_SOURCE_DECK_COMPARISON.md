## 3. Canonical vs Codex-v5 slide-by-slide divergence

Candidate v5 follows the **blueprint** numbering, which **differs from the canonical frozen
sequence** in five structural places. This is the single most important rebuild decision.

| Canonical slide (authority) | Codex v5 slide (blueprint) | Divergence |
|---|---|---|
| 8 — "What changed?" (BEFORE/AFTER evidence) | 9 | v5 **inserts a media shell at 8** and shifts evidence to 9 |
| 9 — "How do we know energy was involved?" (EXPLAIN) | *(folded into v5 slide 9)* | v5 merges "changes are clues that energy was involved" into the evidence slide; the standalone EXPLAIN slide is dropped |
| 10 — V1 cards (energy **and** change on one slide) | 10 **and** 11 | v5 **splits** V1 into two vocab cards |
| 13 — "What is energy?" (RECITE) | *(folded into v5 slide 10)* | v5 drops the standalone RECITE slide |
| 18 — "Clap three times" (optional check) | 18 — "Five forms of energy" typography | different optional slide |
| 19 — N2 WRITE | 19 — "Five forms recap" (5 cards); **20** = N2 WRITE | v5 adds a teaching recap before N2 write |
| 23 — battery PREDICT · 24 — position · 25 — STORED→RELEASE→CHANGE | 25 — "Objects can store energy" (3 cards) | v5 **collapses three teaching slides into one** |
| 32 — "Which change shows energy at work?" (application) | 32 — "CHAPTER 1 OPTIONAL REVIEW" media shell | v5 **replaces the application slide** with a media shell |
| 34–45 review | 34–45 review | same text; v5 adds raster to Q2/Q3/Q6 |

Slides that match in function (1–7, 14–16, 26–31, 33) carry identical anchor text and write/reveal
ordering; only their slide *numbers* shift where the blueprint inserted/split slides.

### 3.1 Concrete v5 text vs canonical (verified)

- v5 S8 = "WATCH CLOSELY … MEDIA SLOT: CH1 ELECTRIC PENCIL SHARPENER — FINAL APPROVED BINARY".
  Canonical has **no** media shell in Session 1.
- v5 S9 ends "The changes are clues that energy was involved." — canonical S9 (EXPLAIN) message
  folded in; acceptable content-wise, different slide count.
- v5 S25 = "Objects can store energy — battery / rubber band / raised object — What change could
  happen later?" — no PREDICT, no explicit "position" teaching, no STORED→RELEASE→CHANGE trace.
  Canonical teaches all three across S23/S24/S25.

### 3.2 Assessment of the divergences

- **Content drift risk (highest):** v5's collapse of N3 teaching (S23/S24/S25) removes the
  **predict-first pedagogy** and the explicit **"position" concept slide** before N3 write. The
  "position" fill-answer then appears on N3 write (v5 S26) without prior teaching.
- **Low risk:** folding the EXPLAIN slide (canonical S9) and the RECITE slide (canonical S13) into
  vocab/evidence slides preserves the message but changes pacing/slide count.
- **Neutral:** media shells at 8/32 are additive if a fallback is kept (it is).
- **Must-decide:** canonical S32 ("Which change shows energy at work?") is replaced by a media shell
  in v5. The canonical application slide is valuable Friday synthesis; its loss should be explicit.

## 5. Recommendation

1. **Keep the canonical frozen 45-slide sequence as the matrix authority.**
2. Adopt v5's premium *visual treatments* (images, cards, layout) onto the **canonical** sequence,
   not v5's re-sequencing, unless the owner explicitly approves the blueprint re-sequencing.
3. Resolve the five divergences explicitly before rebuild (see `CH1_REBUILD_READINESS_REPORT.md`).
4. Treat Gemini decks and standalone intake images as an **alternate photograph library**, graded
   by the visual-art director against the Codex set (resolution + editability + grade-4 fit).
