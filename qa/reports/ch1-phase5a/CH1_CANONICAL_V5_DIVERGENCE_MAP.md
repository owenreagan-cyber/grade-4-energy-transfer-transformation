## Five Phase-4 divergence categories — exact resolutions

1. **Slide 8 media-shell insertion — REJECTED.** Canonical slide 8 is "What changed?" (BEFORE/AFTER +
   CHANGES list). v5's `WATCH CLOSELY / MEDIA SLOT: CH1 ELECTRIC PENCIL SHARPENER` shell at 8 is
   removed. The sharpener is a **live demo** (per `MEDIA_GUIDE.md`); no media slot exists in Session 1.

2. **V1 vocabulary split — REJECTED.** Canonical slide 10 shows **both** `energy` and `change` vocab
   cards on one slide. v5's two-slide split (S10 energy, S11 change) is collapsed back to one slide.

3. **Combined/omitted EXPLAIN + RECITE — RESTORED.** Canonical slide 9 (EXPLAIN: "How do we know
   energy was involved?") and slide 13 (RECITE: "What is energy?") are both standalone. v5 folded them
   away; both are restored at their canonical positions.

4. **Collapsed N3 progression — RESTORED.** Canonical N3 = three teaching slides — 23 (battery
   PREDICT), 24 (position), 25 (STORED→RELEASE→CHANGE) — then 26/27 (write/check). v5's single
   "Objects can store energy" card slide is rejected; the full predict→position→model progression is
   restored (Decision B).

5. **Slide 32 replacement — REJECTED.** Canonical slide 32 is the application slide "Which change
   shows energy at work?" (toy car / rock / dark room). v5's optional-review media shell is rejected;
   the application slide is restored (Decision C).
