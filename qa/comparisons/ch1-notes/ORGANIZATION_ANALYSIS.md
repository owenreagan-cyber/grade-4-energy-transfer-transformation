# CH1 NOTE-FORMAT COMPARISON — ORGANIZATION / RETENTION ANALYSIS

> Part K. Inference from observable format characteristics (labeled as inference — no learning-gain claims).

## INB
- **Permanent composition-book integration** — inserts glued into one notebook; all 9 weeks accumulate in a single bound artifact.
- **Visual memory clues** — shaded model boxes + flaps add a spatial retrieval cue (inference: supports revisiting the anchor model).
- **Tactile interaction** — lift-flap reveal gives an active retrieval moment (inference: mild engagement benefit).
- **Loose-page risk** — LOW once glued (inserts are permanent).
- **Navigation** — GOOD after assembly (chapter sections in one book).
- **Revisit prior models** — GOOD (ENERGY→CHANGE→EVIDENCE physically persists on the inserted page).
- **Absent-student reconstruction burden** — HIGH: a student absent on trim/glue day returns to an incomplete notebook; the teacher must reproduce the insert + supervise assembly out of sequence.

## Guided Notes
- **Straightforward completion** — write directly on the sheet.
- **Larger writing space** — full 7″ lines (vs 7×9 insert constrained).
- **Lower motor/assembly load** — no cutting/gluing.
- **Loose-sheet risk** — HIGH: 3 sheets/week × 9 weeks = ~27 loose sheets; requires a binder/folder and student discipline to keep.
- **Binder/folder dependency** — YES (must be filed).
- **Absent-student ease** — HIGH: photocopy the missed sheet, no assembly.
- **Review usability** — GOOD if filed (flip through sheets); POOR if lost.

## Hybrid (assessed in §15 of the study)
- Combines a curated INB anchor archive (permanent, organized) with full-size Guided Notes for daily work.
- Resolves the INB absent-recovery burden and the Guided Notes loose-sheet risk by putting the *high-value* model in the permanent notebook and the *high-volume* daily data in filed sheets.

## Inference labels
- "Loose-sheet risk", "absent-recovery burden", and "revisit usability" are **inferences** from format mechanics, not measured learning outcomes.
