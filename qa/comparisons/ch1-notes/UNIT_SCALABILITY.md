# CH1 NOTE-FORMAT COMPARISON — UNIT-WIDE SCALABILITY

> Part N. Modeling (not building) what each system implies across Chapters 1–7 + engineering Week 8. Uses released chapter structures (Ch3 speed/displacement, Ch4 sound/light/heat/electricity, Ch5 collisions, Ch6 transformations + capstone, Ch7 Edison/synthesis).

## INB PRIMARY
- **Likely insert count:** 3/chapter × 7 chapters ≈ **21 inserts** (+ engineering cycle, ≈22–24).
- **Assembly burden:** ~10–12 motor actions/chapter; ~54+ min of trim/glue/fold across the unit (from TIME_SIMULATION).
- **Notebook growth:** one composition book fills to ~24 glued inserts; pages thicken, spine stress is manageable but real.
- **Repeated notebook-management time:** every chapter re-pays distribution/cut/glue/cleanup (≈6 min/week).
- **Data-heavy Ch3–5 fit:** WEAK — displacement tables, ramp/height data, sand-crater measurements, and collision Part A/B tables want full-width rows; a 7×9 insert cramps them.
- **Engineering Week 8 fit:** WEAK — blueprint + Test 1/2 logs + redesign + rubric are full-page data artifacts; poor insert fit.

## GUIDED NOTES PRIMARY
- **Likely page count:** 2–3 pages/chapter × 7 ≈ **16–21 pages** (+ data sheets already exist as separate lab pages).
- **Paper volume:** ~16–21 sheets/student/unit (duplex → ~10) + existing lab pages.
- **Binder/folder burden:** MODERATE — ~20 sheets + lab pages must be filed; a 3-prong folder or binder section is required.
- **Data/diagram suitability:** STRONG — full-width tables and drawing areas fit speed, heat, collision, and engineering data.
- **Review usability:** GOOD if filed; a complete sheet set is a clean quiz/final study packet.

## HYBRID
- **Likely insert/page mix:** ~1 INB anchor insert/chapter (the canonical model: ENERGY→CHANGE→EVIDENCE, MOTION→CHANGE→EVIDENCE, speed model, transfer-vs-transform, collision model, engineering cycle, unit concept map ≈ 7 inserts) + full-size Guided Notes/lab sheets for daily data.
- **Complexity:** LOW — 7 inserts/year is trivial to manage vs 21.
- **Teacher-management burden:** LOW — same print flow as Guided Notes, plus one insert per chapter.

## Scalability verdict
| | INB | Guided Notes | Hybrid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Data-heavy Ch3–5 | WEAK | STRONG | STRONG |
| Engineering W8 | WEAK | STRONG | STRONG |
| Assembly/time | HIGH | NONE | LOW |
| Permanent archive | STRONG | WEAK (loose) | STRONG (anchors only) |
| Teacher burden | HIGH | LOW | LOW |

**Guided Notes scale best for the data chapters; Hybrid captures the INB organization benefit at a fraction of the cost.**
