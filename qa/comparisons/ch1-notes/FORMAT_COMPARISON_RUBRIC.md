## Read
- **Guided Notes lead decisively** on readability, writing space, independence, assembly, time, absent recovery, accessibility, and scalability.
- **INB leads only on organization (permanent archive)** and matches on visual learning/retention support.
- The gap is **not** "INB is more fun"; it is that INB spends student and teacher time on assembly that produces no additional science learning, while Guided Notes direct that time into writing and reasoning — a decisive advantage for a data-heavy 4th-grade science unit.
