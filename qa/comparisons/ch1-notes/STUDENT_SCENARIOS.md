# CH1 NOTE-FORMAT COMPARISON — STUDENT-USE SCENARIOS

> Part O. Simulated profiles (no invented personal/medical data; format-mechanics reasoning only).

| Scenario | INB | Guided Notes | Hybrid |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Typical 4th-grade writer** | Works, but spends ~2 min/session on assembly before writing | Clean fit; full writing space | Clean fit + one periodic anchor insert |
| **B. Large handwriting / slower writer** | WARN — 0.28″ lines and 2-line max responses force smaller writing; may not finish | PASS — 0.36–0.42″ lines, full-width, 4-line reasoning | PASS (daily work on full-size sheets) |
| **C. Fine-motor difficulty** | FAIL — cut/glue/fold are the exact skills that are hard; needs adult support or the flat option | PASS — write-only | PASS — flat alternative to any foldable |
| **D. Absent on construction day** | FAIL — returns to a missing insert; reconstruction requires supervised out-of-sequence assembly | PASS — photocopy the missed sheet | PASS — anchor insert is small and quickly reconstructed |
| **E. Benefits from diagrams/visual cues** | STRONG — tactile flap + shaded model boxes | GOOD — diagrams and drawing boxes (no tactile layer) | STRONG — keeps the tactile anchors where they matter |
| **F. Needs quick study/review before quiz** | GOOD — model physically persists in the notebook | GOOD — filed sheets form a packet | STRONG — anchor models + filed sheets both available |

## Read
- Guided Notes (and Hybrid) pass all six scenarios.
- INB fails the fine-motor and absent-student scenarios, and warns on large handwriting.
- The INB's tactile benefit (scenario E) is real but can be captured by Hybrid's few anchor inserts without inheriting the assembly burden.
