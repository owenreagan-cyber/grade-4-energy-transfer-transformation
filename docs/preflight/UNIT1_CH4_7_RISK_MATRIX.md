## Top shared risks to address before production

1. **Transfer vs transformation conflation** — the single highest cross-chapter
   risk; needs a canonical wording guard reused across Chapters 4, 6, 7.
2. **Vague-evidence prompts** — "use evidence" / "explain why" recur in every
   chapter; the STUDENT_PROMPT_CLARITY gate + Grade 4 prompt standard are the
   shared defense and must be applied to all four.
3. **Engineering criteria/constraints** — Chapters 6 and 7 both need an explicit
   design-process scaffold; build one reusable template.
4. **Assessment boundary** — no energy quantification in any chapter; the
   CURRICULUM_CORRECTNESS boundary families (fake m/s, ramp=height=speed, mass
   conclusion) should be generalized per chapter.
5. **Visual system** — Chapters 4–7 need their own themes and a shared
   diagram/icon family; the Chapter 3 Measurement/Data palette is chapter-scoped.
