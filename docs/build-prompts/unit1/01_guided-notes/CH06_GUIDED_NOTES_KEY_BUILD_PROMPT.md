## 7. Required content — the answer set

Mirror the student anchors exactly (BQ · V1 · V2 · N1–N3 · CHECK · BQ·NOW I THINK). Representative answers:

- **V1 — convert:** "to change from one form to another." **V1 — conversion:** "a change from one form to another." **V1 — energy transformation:** "the change of one form of energy to another form of energy."
- **V2 — engineering design:** "using a process to solve a problem (meeting criteria under constraints)." Acceptable: "designing and building to meet a need."
- **N1 three-way:** transfer = "energy moves (object to object / place to place)" · transformation = "energy changes form" · BOTH = "energy moves AND changes form in the same event."
- **N2 INPUT→CHANGE→OUTPUT:** worked device (flashlight): "stored energy in the battery → changes to light (and a little heat) → the bulb lights up; energy also moves through the wires (transfer)." Blank device accepts any source-faithful example.
- **N3 criteria:** "what a good design must do or have." **constraints:** "limits on what can be used or done." Cycle: DESIGN → BUILD → TEST → REFINE; failure note: "a failed test is useful information."
- **CHECK / BQ·NOW I THINK:** "Sample answer — student wording may vary."
