## 5. Instructional purpose

Scaffold students to hold the chapter's core idea — **a collision changes motion, energy of motion transfers to the target, and energy does not disappear** — using a before/after model. The fair-test connection is **same object(s), change speed only** (echoing Chapter 3), without ever requiring momentum/force math or a mass/weight comparison.

## 7. Required content (locked anchors — mirror verbatim)

- **Big Question (BQ):** "How is energy involved in collisions?" (Reader p.25). Supporting focus (TG L11): "What happens when objects collide? How are collisions predictable?"
- **Vocabulary (KEY WORD — V1):** `collide` (Reader p.25) · `collision` (Reader p.25) · `sound` (glossary p.28, taught L10).
- **Note sections (N-anchors):**
  - **N1 — Collisions Cause Changes:** before → collision → after. Students observe that motion may change, direction may change, and one object may begin moving / stop / slow / change direction.
  - **N2 — Collisions Can Transfer Energy:** a simple source/receiver or before/after model — the moving object transfers some energy of motion to the target.
  - **N3 — What Happened to the Energy?:** source-faithful language — some energy may be transferred to motion; sound may occur; a tiny amount of heat may occur; **energy does not disappear.** Do **not** require measuring collision heat.
  - **Fair-test connection (compact, no sublesson):** **same object(s), change speed only** (connects back to Chapter 3). State "change ONE thing, keep everything else the same."
- **Closing:** `CHECK YOUR THINKING` → `BQ · NOW I THINK`.

Section names must match the final-assessment traceability: `p.1 Key Words` and `p.1 Model It`.

## 8. Explicit exclusions (locked boundaries)

- **NO** mass/weight comparison as required content. **NO** "adding mass as a manipulated variable." **NO** formal "heavier object at the same speed has more energy of motion" claim. **NO** table requiring students to compare mass effects. **NO** mass-effects sublesson.
- **NO** momentum · impulse · force calculations · contact force · deformation (required) · kinetic/potential/mechanical/radiant/gravitational-potential · inertia · compression-wave/particle-level sound.
- **NO** measured collision heat (sound qualitative only; a tiny amount of heat may be *mentioned* as source-supported, never measured).
- **NO** engineering design cycle (Chapter 6).
- **NO** answer leakage; **NO** fabricated numeric speed/energy values.

> **Assessment flag:** Reader p.30 ("Weight Affects the Changes Caused by Collisions") and TG L10's weight demo **do** exist in the source. ## Cross-cutting rules (apply to this and every Unit 1 Guided Notes prompt)

### A. Obsolete INB slides are not authority
The existing Ch5 deck may contain obsolete INB anchors/directions. Use it only as **sequence evidence, existing-content evidence, and visual reference.** Derive the correct structure first from (1) Reader/TG, (2) accepted pacing, (3) current notes architecture, (4) chapter instructional sequence — then document required slide changes later. Do NOT force the notes to mimic INB-era slide structure.

### B. Necessary-writing audit
For each blank/written response, answer: **Why must the student write this?** Acceptable reasons: reinforces a core concept · records data · completes a meaningful model · supports retrieval · captures evidence · preserves synthesis · supports engineering thinking · prepares for a later required response. Weak: "because the Reader says it." Print a fact if it does not benefit from transcription. Maintain **~15–30% student-completed content**; avoid over-blanking.

### C. Core vs supporting vocabulary
Use **KEY WORD** for required vocabulary and (only where needed) **WORDS YOU MAY SEE** for supporting terms. Do not imply every term is test vocabulary. Status follows source/pacing/assessment alignment.

### D. 30-minute writing-load audit
Before approval, confirm all required writing fits the chapter's 30-minute block(s). Mark sections: `REQUIRED_WRITE` · `TEACHER_MODEL` · `DISCUSS_ONLY` · `OPTIONAL_WRITE` · `JOURNAL_OPTIONAL`. The packet must support pacing, not slow it down.
