## 7. Required content (locked anchors — mirror verbatim)

- **Big Question (BQ):** "What evidence shows that energy transfers from place to place?" (Reader p.17).
- **Vocabulary:**
  - **KEY WORD — V1:** `energy transfer` (Reader p.17) · `sound` (glossary p.28, taught L6).
  - No second vocabulary group is required. Do **not** add a `transfer vs transformation` vocabulary group here.
- **Note sections (N-anchors):**
  - **N1 — Energy Transfer:** energy can move from one object to another or from one place to another. Model: ENERGY → MOVES → TARGET CHANGES → EVIDENCE.
  - **N2 — Sound and Light Can Transfer Energy:** source → path → receiver → evidence (the source map and TG L6–L7 support this model: tapped cup → air → paper target moves; flashlight → beam → lit card).
  - **N3 — Heat and Electric Current Can Transfer Energy:** focus on the **observable evidence** that energy moved (warm water warms a cooler object; battery → wires → bulb/buzzer/motor turns on). **Do not teach electron mechanics.**
  - **N4 — Finding Evidence of Energy Transfer:** a compact organizer:
    | Source | Receiver | What changed? | Evidence energy transferred |
  - **Closing:** `CHECK YOUR THINKING` (2–3 retrieval/application items on transfer only) → `BQ · NOW I THINK` (return to the exact Ch4 BQ).

Section names must match the final-assessment traceability: `p.1 Key Words` · `p.2 Sound Transfers Energy` / `Light Transfers Energy` · `p.3 Heat Transfers Energy` · `p.4 Evidence of Energy Transfer`.

## 8. Explicit exclusions (locked boundaries)

- **Do NOT introduce formal transfer / transformation / BOTH classification in Chapter 4. Reserve the formal distinction for Chapter 6.** No `transformation` vocabulary group, no three-way classification table, no `BOTH` reasoning.
- **NO** compression-wave science, particle-level sound, electron flow, conduction/convection/radiation as required, current/voltage calculations.
- **NO** kinetic / potential / mechanical / radiant / gravitational-potential / inertia / momentum vocabulary.
- **NO** collisions content (Chapter 5) and **NO** engineering design cycle (Chapter 6) as required vocabulary.
- **NO** answer leakage; **NO** sample "correct" data.
- **NO** student wall outlets, hot plates, open flames, chalk dust, or coin-cell references in any diagram/direction.

> **Assessment flag:** the unit final contains transformation items (e.g., Q20 "Both transfer and transformation"). That content is taught in **Chapter 6**, before the unit final, so it does not justify teaching transformation in Chapter 4. If any *chapter-level* Ch4 quiz/check item required transformation wording, flag it for assessment review instead of teaching it early.

## Cross-cutting rules (apply to this and every Unit 1 Guided Notes prompt)

### A. Obsolete INB slides are not authority
The existing Ch4 deck may contain obsolete Interactive Science Notebook anchors/directions. Use it only as **sequence evidence, existing-content evidence, and visual reference.** Derive the correct Guided Notes structure first from (1) Reader/TG, (2) accepted pacing, (3) current notes architecture, (4) chapter instructional sequence — then document which slide changes are required later. Do NOT force the notes to mimic INB-era slide structure.

### B. Necessary-writing audit
Before finalizing each blank or written response, answer: **Why must the student write this?** Acceptable: reinforces a core concept · records data · completes a meaningful model · supports retrieval · captures evidence · preserves synthesis · supports engineering thinking · prepares for a later required response. Weak: "because the Reader says it." If a fact is important but does not benefit from transcription, print it instead. Maintain **~15–30% student-completed content**; avoid over-blanking.

### C. Core vs supporting vocabulary
Use **KEY WORD** for required instructional vocabulary and (only where needed) **WORDS YOU MAY SEE** for supporting terms. Do not imply every term is test vocabulary. Vocabulary status follows source/pacing/assessment alignment.

### D. 30-minute writing-load audit
Before approval, confirm all required writing fits the chapter's 30-minute block(s). Mark sections: `REQUIRED_WRITE` · `TEACHER_MODEL` · `DISCUSS_ONLY` · `OPTIONAL_WRITE` · `JOURNAL_OPTIONAL`. The packet must support pacing, not slow it down.
