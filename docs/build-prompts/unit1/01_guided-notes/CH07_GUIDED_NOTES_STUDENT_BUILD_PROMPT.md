### Source-verified history facts (anchor to these Reader pages — do not embellish)

The authoritative Reader already states the following; the builder must **preserve this wording and cite the pages**, not re-derive it:

- **Reader p.38:** before the light bulb people used open flames; "Edison did not invent the first light bulb — others had"; early bulbs were short-lived and expensive; **Edison improved** earlier designs to be safer and more affordable.
- **Reader p.39:** Edison's team tested **more than six thousand materials** for the filament; 1879 bamboo filament with carbon coating (electrical energy → light). Quote as the Reader phrases it ("When you have exhausted all the possibilities…").
- **Reader p.41:** "I haven't failed. I've just found ten thousand ways that won't work." — persistence defined. **Lewis Howard Latimer** improved the bulb (securing wire to filament; stronger/more durable filament); he worked for Edison's competitors and later Edison's company.
- **Reader p.42:** design improves over time (incandescent → CFL → LED).

> **History rule (locked):** all Edison/history wording must be derived directly from the authoritative Reader/TG. Preserve source wording. Avoid the lone-genius oversimplification *only if* the source itself avoids it (here the source explicitly says he did not invent the first bulb and worked with Latimer — so preserve that accurately). Do NOT invent dates, roles, collaborators, patents, or "first" claims beyond what the source states. Distinguish scientific/engineering content from historical enrichment. Keep supporting history vocabulary separate from required core vocabulary.
