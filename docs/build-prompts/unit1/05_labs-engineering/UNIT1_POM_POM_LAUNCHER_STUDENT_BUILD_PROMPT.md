# BUILD PROMPT — Unit 1 Pom-Pom Launcher (Student Design/Test Sheet)

> Standalone production prompt. Unit-level formative engineering build (Week 4 · Thursday 11/5, per `docs/playbook/UNIT1_PACING_CALENDAR.md`). Net-new ADAPTED resource — does not exist yet.

## 1. Mission

Create the **student design/test sheet** for the Unit 1 Pom-Pom Launcher — a compact (1–2 page) engineering record that walks a Grade 4 student through DESIGN → BUILD → TEST → IMPROVE using a cup + balloon + pom-pom launcher, in a **controlled testing lane**, without inventing measured speed or energy values.

## 2. Exact resource name

`unit1-pom-pom-launcher-student` — **Pom-Pom Launcher — Design & Test Sheet (Student)**

## 3. Audience

Grade 4 students at a desk (build happens at the desk; testing happens in the designated lane).

## 4. Required / optional status

**REQUIRED (formative build) · NOT a major grade.** Label the top corner `ENGINEERING · BUILD · TEST · IMPROVE` (a resource badge, not an "OPTIONAL" label — this is a required build but is not scored as a major grade).

## 5. Instructional purpose

Give the student a place to (a) record the challenge and their design idea, (b) run a fair test in the designated lane, (c) observe what changed, and (d) make ONE evidence-based improvement — consolidating stored energy → release → motion and the transfer/transformation language already taught (Ch1 stored energy, Ch4 transfer, Ch6 transformation). **No new essential science is introduced during this build.**

## 6. Source authority

- **Framework:** `docs/planning/ENGINEERING_CAPSTONE.md` and the Ch6 engineering cycle (DESIGN → BUILD → TEST → REFINE, already taught).
- **Pacing authority:** `docs/playbook/UNIT1_PACING_CALENDAR.md` (Week 4 Thu) and `docs/playbook/UNIT1_RESOURCE_MAP.md` (Pom-Pom Launcher = REQUIRED BUILD · net-new ADAPTED).
- **Lab reasoning standard:** `docs/standards/GUIDED_LAB_REASONING_STANDARD.md` (reason from own data, no fabricated measurements).
- **Reused taught vocabulary only:** stored energy (Ch1), motion / energy of motion (Ch2), speed (Ch3), transfer (Ch4), transformation (Ch6).

## 7. Required content

- **Challenge line (controlled — do NOT use "across the room" or "as far as possible"):**
  "Design a launcher that sends a pom-pom safely through the designated testing lane. Then improve your design so it works more consistently or reaches a farther target within the testing area."
- **Design box:** "Draw your launcher. Label the cup and the balloon." (drawing box ≥ 3.2 × 2.1 in).
- **Prediction:** "What will happen when you pull the balloon back and let go?" (one sentence, 2 ruled lines).
- **Fair-test note:** "Change ONE thing at a time. Keep everything else the same." with a small "one thing I changed" line.
- **Test record:** a 3-row × 3-column table — columns `Trial` · `What I did (pulled the balloon a little / a lot)` · `What happened to the pom-pom` (short-answer rows 0.60–0.70 in). No required cm; no speed number.
- **Observe/compare:** "Which trial sent the pom-pom farthest (or most consistently) into the lane? How do you know?" (compare two named trials).
- **Improve:** "Choose ONE change to make the launcher work better. Why will that change help?" (2 sentences / 3 ruled lines).
- **Retest line (optional):** "After your change, what happened?" (one line).
- **Connect:** one sentence-completion: "When I stretch the balloon, I store _____. When I let go, the stored energy changes to _____ (motion), and the energy _____ (moves/transfers) to the pom-pom." (use only taught words: stored energy · motion · transfer).

## 8. Explicit exclusions

- **NO** measured speed (no m/s), **NO** measured energy values, **NO** collision-heat measurement.
- **NO** kinetic/potential/mechanical/radiant/elastic-potential vocabulary — use "stored energy".
- **NO** required cm-distance unless the teacher chooses to add it; the sheet must not assume a measuring tape.
- **NO** answer leakage (no "correct" launch outcome shown).
- **NO** "across the room" / "as far as possible" framing (controlled lane only).

**1–2 pages.** Prefer 1 page front/back if it fits at 70–88% density; 2 pages only if the table + drawing box genuinely need the room.
