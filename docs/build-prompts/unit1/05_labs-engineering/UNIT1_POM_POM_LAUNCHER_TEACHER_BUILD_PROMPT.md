## 7. Required content (teacher-facing)

- **Objective:** students build a cup-and-balloon launcher, test it in the designated lane, and make one evidence-based improvement while using taught energy language.
- **Challenge (controlled — do NOT use "across the room" / "as far as possible"):**
  "Design a launcher that sends a pom-pom safely through the designated testing lane. Then improve your design so it works more consistently or reaches a farther target within the testing area."
- **Materials (per student/group):** 1 paper or plastic cup · 1 balloon · 1 pom-pom · tape · markers (optional decoration) · scissors (teacher-supervised). State quantities and the GROUP_COUNT multiplier.
  - **Allergy note:** **Follow school allergy procedures. Use latex-free balloons when required or appropriate.** (Do not assume "latex-free by default.")
- **Setup:** cut the bottom out of the cup (teacher pre-cuts if scissors are a safety concern); stretch the balloon over the cup opening; knot the balloon neck; pull the neck back, load pom-pom, release.
- **Testing-lane / safety setup (define all of these):**
  - **Launch direction:** one direction, away from all students.
  - **Testing lane:** a marked floor lane or cleared table corridor (tape lines or a long table runner).
  - **Target zone:** a marked zone at the end of the lane (e.g., a taped square or a target sheet) so "farther within the testing area" is concrete.
  - **Students behind launch line:** all students stay behind the launch line except the tester.
  - **No aiming at people:** never point the launcher at anyone; aim only down the lane.
  - **Controlled retrieval:** retrieve pom-poms only after the teacher says "clear"; no running into the lane.
  - **One group testing at a time (or another safe procedure).**
  - **Reset/cleanup:** return scissors, discard torn balloons, collect launchers or send home, clear the lane.
- **Sequence + timing (30 min):** challenge + criteria (2 min) → build (8 min) → initial test (3 trials) (8 min) → observe/compare + improve (7 min) → final test (2 min) → reflection + cleanup (3 min).
- **Required instructional arc:** challenge + criteria → build → initial test → observe/measure as appropriate → identify relevant energy ideas already taught → improve → final test → reflection. **No new essential science during this build.**
- **Look-fors:** a labeled design (cup + balloon) · a fair-test statement (one thing changed) · comparison of two named trials · one evidence-based improvement · the connect sentence uses "stored energy → motion → transfer".
- **Likely results:** pulling the balloon back **more** (farther) launches the pom-pom **farther** — more stored energy → more motion (do not require a number).
- **Misconceptions:** "the balloon makes energy" (energy is stored, then transferred); "pulling harder always works" (a torn/loose balloon changes the result — check the fair test); "distance = speed" (do not claim speed).
- **Safety:** scissors supervised; allergy-aware balloon choice (see allergy note); no launching at faces; clear launch lane; controlled retrieval.
- **Cleanup:** return scissors, discard torn balloons, collect launchers or send home, clear lane/target zone.
- **If short on time:** cut the final test or the retest step; keep challenge → build → 3 trials → one improvement.
- **What NOT to require:** measured cm distance, speed values, energy values, or the words kinetic/potential/elastic.
