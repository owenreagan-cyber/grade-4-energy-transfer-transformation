# ENGINEERING CAPSTONE

> Week 8 performance assessment (TG L13, 4 sessions; AP 13.1–13.4). This is the recommended **common challenge architecture** — a single, indoor-reliable, tabletop challenge.

## Recommended challenge
A **tabletop alert / signal / motion device** that transforms stored or motion energy into a clear **sound or light signal** — **not** an attachment to a real classroom door, and **not** weather/solar-dependent.

### Requirements (locked design direction)
- solves a real (model) problem
- uses an **energy transformation** (stored/motion → sound/light)
- **indoor reliable** (no solar dependence as the only pathway)
- inexpensive, safe, testable, repeatable, measurable
- supports **redesign**

### Energy model to use
`STORED ENERGY → MOTION ENERGY → SOUND / LIGHT SIGNAL` (with the unit's five-box model: source → form → transfer → transformation → evidence). Students label each energy form in **Grade 4 language** (stored energy, energy of motion, sound, light — **not** kinetic/potential).

## 4-day structure

| Day | Focus | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **Day 1** | Problem · criteria · constraints · energy model · blueprint | AP 13.1 Device Design Proposal |
| **Day 2** | Build · **Test 1** · collect evidence | AP 13.2 Device Test Results |
| **Day 3** | **Redesign** · **Test 2** · compare | AP 13.2 (second trial) |
| **Day 4** | Final test · presentation · reflection | AP 13.3 plan, AP 13.4 scoring guide |

## Criteria & constraints (teacher-set, fixed)
- **Criteria (success):** signal triggered by a clear input; sound audible at ~3 m OR light visible across the room; transformation chain correctly labeled.
- **Constraints (limits):** provided craft materials only; fits a defined footprint (e.g., 30 cm × 30 cm); no wall electricity; safe for indoor use.

## Scoring rubric (reward reasoning over spectacle)
1. **Problem understanding** — restates the problem accurately.
2. **Criteria/constraints** — design addresses both.
3. **Correct energy model** — labels source→form→transfer→transformation correctly.
4. **Testing** — runs fair, repeatable trials; records data.
5. **Evidence** — cites observations that support success/failure.
6. **Redesign** — makes a reasoned change and re-tests.
7. **Explanation** — explains how the device transforms energy.

> **Do not over-penalize a failed prototype if student reasoning is strong.** A device that doesn't fully work but is well-reasoned, tested, and redesigned scores higher than an unexamined one that happens to work.

## Guardrails
- "Pendulum Bell Alert" (from Phase 0 draft) is kept as a **teacher exemplar / post-task model** — **not** shown before students design independently.
- Use Grade 4 terminology throughout (stored energy, energy of motion, energy transfer, energy transformation).
