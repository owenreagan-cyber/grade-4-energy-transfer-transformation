# LAB ARCHITECTURE

> Eight-lab backbone. Labs 6 and 7 carry redesign rules; Lab 8 is the 4-day capstone. Each lab is specified by: question, target concept, variable changed, controlled factors, primary evidence, materials, safety, prep, cleanup, absent-student recovery, likely failure modes, and CORE/FLEX/EXTENSION tag.

## Lab 1 — Energy Detectives / Scavenger Hunt
- **Question:** *Where can we observe evidence of energy causing change?*
- **Target concept:** change is evidence of energy; stored energy exists in nonmoving objects.
- **Variable changed:** none (observation only).
- **Controlled factors:** none.
- **Primary evidence:** recorded observable changes (light/motion/sound) + battery powering a device.
- **Materials:** electric pencil sharpener + unsharpened pencil, classroom devices (light switch, fan, clock, bell/drum), flashlight + D-cell batteries, index cards (vocabulary deck), AP 1.1 log.
- **Safety:** standard; no heat claims.
- **Prep:** low; gather familiar classroom devices.
- **Cleanup:** return devices; store vocab cards.
- **Absent recovery:** complete AP 1.1 from home objects; battery demo video.
- **Failure modes:** students claim unobserved heat (e.g., "sharpener got hot") — redirect to observable evidence only; battery merely inspected instead of tested — **require** it to run a device.
- **Tag:** [CORE]

## Lab 2 — Moving Marbles
- **Question:** *How does a moving object cause change?*
- **Target concept:** a moving object has energy of motion and can cause change.
- **Variable changed:** **none** in core version (single gentle roll). **Identical marbles only.**
- **Controlled factors:** same marble size/type, same track.
- **Primary evidence:** target marble displacement (qualitative).
- **Materials:** identical glass marbles, grooved ruler/track, paper cups.
- **Safety:** standard; keep marbles off floor hazards.
- **Prep:** low.
- **Cleanup:** collect marbles.
- **Absent recovery:** short video of marble collision + before/after diagram.
- **Failure modes:** **mass + speed confound** (using steel/large marbles or varying force) — prohibited in core; keep identical marbles, single gentle roll.
- **Tag:** [CORE]

## Lab 3 — Ramp It Up! 2.0
- **Question:** *How are speed and energy of motion related?*
- **Target concept:** same object, greater ramp height → greater speed → greater energy (shown by larger cup displacement).
- **Variable changed:** ramp height (1, 2, 3 books).
- **Controlled factors:** **same ball**, same ramp, same cup, same release point.
- **Primary evidence:** cup displacement (cm); graph **Ramp Height vs Cup Displacement**.
- **Materials:** same ball, ramp, textbooks, plastic cup, meter stick/ruler, graph grid.
- **Safety:** clear launch lane; no projectiles toward people.
- **Prep:** moderate; pre-make ramps.
- **Cleanup:** stack books, return cup.
- **Absent recovery:** share class data set; complete graph from provided data.
- **Failure modes:** students claim they "measured energy/speed directly" — correct to "inferred"; inconsistent release — require repeated trials + average.
- **Tag:** [CORE]

## Lab 4 — Drop It!
- **Question:** *How does drop height affect impact evidence (crater)?*
- **Target concept:** higher drop → greater falling speed → greater energy of motion (same object).
- **Variable changed:** **fixed drop heights** (e.g., 30 cm and 60 cm — **not** knee/waist).
- **Controlled factors:** **same ball**, same sand container, same surface.
- **Primary evidence:** crater **diameter in cm** (depth only qualitative).
- **Materials:** same ball, shallow sand tray, ruler, metric tape.
- **Safety:** goggles (sand), keep sand off floor.
- **Prep:** moderate; pre-fill sand trays.
- **Cleanup:** sweep sand; level trays.
- **Absent recovery:** provided data + crater sketch.
- **Failure modes:** knee/waist heights (inconsistent) and mm depth probes (false precision) — use fixed cm heights and diameter; depth optional qualitative.
- **Tag:** [CORE]

## Lab 5 — Sound Across Space
- **Question:** *What evidence shows sound transfers energy?*
- **Target concept:** sound transfers energy through air to cause a change in a distant object.
- **Variable changed:** tap distance/intensity (optional).
- **Controlled factors:** same cup/drum, same target.
- **Primary evidence:** paper/cup target movement when drum is tapped.
- **Materials:** plastic cup/drum, paper strip or L-shaped paper target.
- **Safety:** standard; keep taps moderate.
- **Prep:** low.
- **Cleanup:** return cups.
- **Absent recovery:** demo video.
- **Failure modes:** turning this into a **compression-wave** lesson — keep focus on "sound causes a change at a distance" evidence.
- **Tag:** [CORE]

## Lab 6 — Energy Transfer Stations (REDESIGNED)
- **Question:** *What evidence shows light, heat, and electricity transfer energy?*
- **Target concept:** each form transfers energy from place to place; observable evidence marks the transfer.
- **Variable changed:** none (stations).
- **Controlled factors:** station setup fixed.
- **Primary evidence:** per-station observable change.
- **Station structure:**
  - **A. Light:** flashlight → target (e.g., spot on wall/solar cell/paper); observe the light reaching a distant target.
  - **B. Heat:** **warm water → cooler object** + thermometer (temperature rises).
  - **C. Electricity:** **low-voltage battery circuit → LED/bulb/buzzer/motor** (device runs).
- **Safety:** **no student wall-outlet work; no hot plate as default student station.** Warm water handled with cups/tongs; low-voltage battery only.
- **Prep:** moderate; pre-build circuits, pre-warm water (safe temperature).
- **Cleanup:** dispose water, store circuits.
- **Absent recovery:** station video or simplified at-home demo.
- **Failure modes:** hot-plate/surge-protector/tablet stations (superseded) — replaced by safe stations above.
- **Tag:** [CORE]

## Lab 7 — Crash Lab (variable isolation)
- **Question:** *What happens when objects collide, and how are collisions predictable?*
- **Target concept:** collisions transfer energy and change motion; outcomes follow the setup.
- **Variable changed:** **Part A** — ramp height (same car); **Part B** — car mass (same ramp height). **Never both at once.**
- **Controlled factors:** Part A: same car/cup; Part B: same ramp/cup.
- **Primary evidence:** **target displacement** (cm).
- **Materials:** toy car(s), ramp, textbooks, target (cup/block), meter stick.
- **Safety:** clear crash lane; goggles optional.
- **Prep:** moderate.
- **Cleanup:** reset ramp/cars.
- **Absent recovery:** provided data; Newton's cradle demo video.
- **Failure modes:** confounding mass + speed in one trial — isolate variables; using noisy clay-depth/loudness as primary data — use target displacement; Newton's cradle is **demo/phenomenon only**.
- **Tag:** [CORE]

## Lab 8 — Energy Engineer (4-day capstone)
- **Question:** *How can I design a device that transforms energy to solve a problem?*
- **Target concept:** engineering design — criteria, constraints, test → evidence → redesign → retest.
- **Variable changed:** device design (student-controlled).
- **Controlled factors:** criteria/constraints fixed by teacher.
- **Primary evidence:** test results, redesign comparison, final explanation.
- **Materials:** craft kit (string, bells, marbles, cups, tape, cardboard, rubber bands) — see `MASTER_SUPPLY_MATRIX.md`.
- **Safety:** craft tools only; no electricity required.
- **Prep:** high; pre-pack per-group kits.
- **Cleanup:** collect kits; keep prototypes for presentation.
- **Absent recovery:** design-on-paper + partner prototype.
- **Failure modes:** solar/weather-dependent sole pathway (prohibited) — use **indoor-reliable tabletop** challenge; rewarding spectacle over criteria (prohibited).
- **Tag:** [CORE — Week 8 performance assessment]
