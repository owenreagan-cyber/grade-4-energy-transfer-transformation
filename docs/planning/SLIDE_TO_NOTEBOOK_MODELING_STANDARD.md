# SLIDE-TO-NOTEBOOK MODELING STANDARD

> How slides support Interactive Science Notebook completion directly. Applies to all chapter slide decks (Ch.3+ from the start; Ch.1–2 retrofitted).

## Purpose
Slides explicitly model what students write, where they write it, and what they might draw — so notebook work is quick, consistent, and not transcription.

## For every notebook-writing moment, the slide shows
1. **Page title / matching icon** (so students find the right insert).
2. **Exact fill-in answer** (revealed, not front-loaded).
3. **One drawing idea / example** (labeled as an idea, not a required exact copy).
4. **Where to write/draw** (page section name).
5. **Teacher pause cue** (explicitly tells the teacher to stop for notebook time).

## Recommended slide labels
| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `NOTEBOOK` | open the notebook to this page |
| `WRITE` | fill in this wording |
| `DRAW` | draw this idea (ideas only, not exact copy) |
| `LABEL` | add labels/arrows |
| `CHECK` | teacher checks / partner check |

## Example cue slide
```
NOTEBOOK — VOCAB

WRITE:
Energy is the ability to cause ________.

(REVEAL →) CHANGE

DRAWING IDEAS:
- flashlight turning on
- rolling ball moving a cup
```

## Rules
- Do NOT create dense "copy this paragraph" slides.
- Keep cue slides to ONE notebook moment each.
- Prediction-before-reveal still applies: reveal the fill-in after students attempt.
- Student drawings are ideas — students may draw their own example.
- Cue slides reuse the deck's visual language (large type, high contrast, arrows).
