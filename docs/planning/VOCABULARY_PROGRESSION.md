# VOCABULARY PROGRESSION

> Authoritative terminology from the CKSci Teacher Guide and Student Reader. The TG's full glossary (TG pp. 143–144) distinguishes **Core Vocabulary** (blue; Student Reader page #) from **Language of Instruction** (bold; teacher-modeled, not required for independent student use).

## Core Vocabulary (14 terms — required for independent student use)

| Term | Reader page | Definition (source) |
|---|---|---|
| change | 1 | to become different |
| energy | 1 | the ability to cause change |
| stored energy | 4 | energy that has the ability to cause change at a later time |
| motion | 7 | the process of an object changing position |
| energy of motion | 9 | the energy an object possesses while it is moving |
| speed | 13 | a measurement of the distance an object travels over an amount of time |
| energy transfer | 17 | movement of energy from one object to another or from one place to another |
| collide | 25 | to come together with impact |
| collision | 25 | an instance of colliding |
| sound | 28 | a form of energy that comes from a vibrating object |
| convert | 32 | to change form |
| conversion | 32 | the process of being converted or changing form |
| energy transformation | 32 | the change of one form of energy to another form of energy |
| engineering design | 36 | a process used to develop a solution to a problem |

## Language of Instruction (teacher-modeled; NOT required for student use)
cause and effect · claim · contact · design problem · design solution · distance · electrical energy · electricity · energy change · evidence · forms of energy · heat · light · model · observation · position · prediction · reasoning · rotate · solution · transfer · transform · variable

## NOT core — do NOT make required (TG explicitly avoids / not in glossary)
- kinetic energy · potential energy · gravitational potential energy (TG verbatim: does **not** use kinetic or potential energy)
- mechanical energy · radiant energy · compression wave · inertia

## Engineering-only vocabulary (used in L13, not in glossary)
- **criteria** (desired features of a solution) · **constraints** (limitations/resources) — introduced informally in L13 "Know the Standards."

## Rules
1. Vocabulary is **never** drilled in isolation (TG: "not intended for use in isolated drill or memorization").
2. A cumulative **vocabulary card deck** is built lesson-by-lesson (index cards, alphabetical).
3. Quizzes assess vocabulary **in context**, not as isolated matching (see `QUIZ_STANDARD.md`).
4. Any enrichment use of kinetic/potential/radiant/compression-wave/inertia must be labeled **teacher language / EXTENSION** and never required.
