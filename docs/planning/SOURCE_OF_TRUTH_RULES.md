## Rules

1. **Authoritative sources control:**
   - chapter titles (7 chapters, exact titles)
   - core concepts (energy = ability to cause change; stored energy; energy of motion)
   - core vocabulary (14 Core Vocabulary terms)
   - NGSS standards (4-PS3-1, 4-PS3-2, 4-PS3-3, 4-PS3-4)
   - required scientific framing (transfer vs transformation distinction)
   - official lesson/activity-page numbering (AP 1.1 … AP UR.1)

2. **Supplemental materials may inspire:** examples, alternate practice, enrichment, Friday Flex ideas, visuals, teacher supports. They must be tagged `SOURCE`/`ADAPTED`/`CUSTOM`/`EXTENSION` and never be presented as core authority.

3. **Drafts preserve useful ideas but NEVER override authority.** The three AI-generated drafts carry confirmed terminology and science errors (kinetic/potential language, mass-speed confounds, compression-wave language, hot-plate station). Treat as idea banks only.

4. **When sources conflict:**
   1. document the conflict;
   2. do **not** silently reconcile;
   3. prefer the primary authoritative source (Level 1);
   4. preserve uncertainty in `OPEN_QUESTIONS.md`.

## Verified source facts (direct source inspection)

- **7 chapter titles** (from Reader TOC): Energy Causes Change · Moving Objects Have Energy · Energy and Speed Are Related · Energy Transfer · Collisions Transfer Energy · How Can Energy Transformations Solve Problems? · Solving Problems and Designing Solutions: Thomas A. Edison.
- **13 TG lessons** in 5 parts (A–E) + Unit Review (see `NINE_WEEK_SCOPE_SEQUENCE.md`).
- **NGSS PEs:** 4-PS3-1, 4-PS3-2, 4-PS3-3, 4-PS3-4.
- **Terminology rule (verbatim intent):** the unit "does not use the term kinetic energy. Neither does this unit use the term potential energy." Prefer **stored energy** and **energy of motion**.
- **Transfer vs transformation (verbatim):** "Transfer of energy is energy moving from place to place or from object to object. Transformation of energy is when energy changes from one form to another."
- **Collisions (verbatim):** "When objects collide, energy can transfer from one object to another and change the motion of the objects. In a collision, some energy is transferred from the objects to the air as sound or heat."
- **Pacing:** ≤20 teaching days recommended; each Core Lesson = 30–45 min.
- **Core Vocabulary (14 terms, Reader page #):** change(1), collide(25), collision(25), conversion(32), convert(32), energy(1), energy of motion(9), energy transfer(17), energy transformation(32), engineering design(36), motion(7), sound(28), speed(13), stored energy(4).
