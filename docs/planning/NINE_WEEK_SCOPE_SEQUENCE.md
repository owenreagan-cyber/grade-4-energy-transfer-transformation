# NINE-WEEK SCOPE & SEQUENCE

> Working course structure. Each week lists chapter, TG lessons, Reader pages (printed), Big Questions, core concepts, vocabulary, lab/activity focus, assessment checkpoint, and Friday Flex options. Markings: **[CORE]** = essential; **[FLEX]** = optional/nonessential; **[EXT]** = extension/enrichment.

## Week 1 — Chapter 1: Energy Causes Change
- **TG lessons:** L1 Energy Causes Change · L2 Forms of Energy
- **Reader pages:** 1–6 · **AP:** 1.1, 2.1, 2.2
- **Big Questions:** *Where can we observe evidence of energy causing change? What are some forms of energy?*
- **Core concepts:** energy = the ability to cause change; change as evidence of energy; forms of energy (motion, light, sound, heat, electrical); stored energy.
- **Vocabulary:** [CORE] energy, change, evidence, forms of energy, stored energy, light, heat, sound, electrical energy.
- **Lab/activity:** Lab 1 Energy Detectives/Scavenger Hunt (battery tested **in a device**, no unobserved heat claims) [CORE]; forms-of-energy sorting [FLEX].
- **Checkpoint:** exit tickets; notebook check; observation of AP 1.1.
- **Friday Flex:** catch-up · "Odd One Out" forms-of-energy · energy scavenger hunt extension.

## Week 2 — Chapter 2: Moving Objects Have Energy
- **TG lessons:** L3 Moving Objects Have Energy
- **Reader pages:** 7–12 · **AP:** 3.1
- **Big Question:** *How are energy, change, and movement of objects related?*
- **Core concepts:** all moving objects have energy of motion; a moving object can cause change.
- **Vocabulary:** [CORE] motion, energy of motion, position, distance.
- **Lab/activity:** Lab 2 Moving Marbles — **identical marbles only**; purpose = moving object can cause change; **do NOT vary mass + speed** [CORE].
- **Checkpoint:** AP 3.1 "On the Move" visual model; exit ticket.
- **Friday Flex:** "Energy Chain" card sort · collision demo preview · remediation.

## Week 3 — Chapter 3: Energy and Speed Are Related
- **TG lessons:** L4 Investigating Energy and Speed (2 sessions) · L5 Energy and Speed Are Related
- **Reader pages:** 13–16 · **AP:** 4.1, 4.2
- **Big Question:** *How are energy and speed related?*
- **Core concepts:** faster (same object) → greater energy of motion; ramp height vs cup displacement as evidence; students do **not** directly measure energy.
- **Vocabulary:** [CORE] speed, energy of motion, variable (Language of Instruction); prediction.
- **Lab/activity:** Lab 3 Ramp It Up! 2.0 (same ball, different heights, repeated trials, cup displacement, graph Ramp Height vs Cup Displacement) [CORE]; Lab 4 Drop It! (fixed heights, same ball, crater diameter in cm) [CORE].
- **Checkpoint:** graph interpretation; L4/L5 checks; observation.
- **Friday Flex:** graph/data practice · "Collision Predictor" warm-up · absent-student lab recovery.

## Week 4 — Chapter 4 Part 1: Sound + Light Energy Transfer
- **TG lessons:** L6 Sound Transfers Energy · L7 Energy Transfer
- **Reader pages:** 17–24 · **AP:** 6.1, 7.1
- **Big Questions:** *What evidence shows that sound transfers energy from one place to another? What evidence shows that energy transfers from place to place?*
- **Core concepts:** transfer = energy moving place-to-place/object-to-object; sound and light as transfer evidence.
- **Vocabulary:** [CORE] energy transfer, transfer; revisit sound, light.
- **Lab/activity:** Lab 5 Sound Across Space (cup/sound/paper phenomenon; evidence of sound transfer; **avoid** compression-wave detail) [CORE].
- **Checkpoint:** L6 Check (AP 6.1), L7 Check (AP 7.1).
- **Friday Flex:** "Transfer / Transformation / Both" sorting · sound phenomenon extension · catch-up.

## Week 5 — Chapter 4 Part 2: Heat + Electricity + Evidence/CER
- **TG lessons:** L8 Investigating Energy Transfer · L9 Evidence of Energy Transfer
- **Reader pages:** 17–24 · **AP:** 8.1, 9.1, 9.2
- **Big Questions:** *What evidence shows that light, heat, and electricity transfer energy from place to place? What evidence shows that energy is transferred from place to place?*
- **Core concepts:** heat and electric-current transfer; making claims supported by evidence; CER (Claims, Evidence, Reasoning) introduced.
- **Vocabulary:** [CORE] evidence, claim, reasoning (Language of Instruction); revisit heat, electricity, energy transfer.
- **Lab/activity:** Lab 6 Energy Transfer Stations (REDESIGNED: A light flashlight→target; B heat warm water→cooler object + thermometer; C electricity low-voltage battery circuit→LED/bulb/buzzer/motor; **no wall outlets, no hot plate as default station**) [CORE].
- **Checkpoint:** AP 8.1, AP 9.1, AP 9.2 CER.
- **Friday Flex:** CER completion · error-detective energy chains · remediation.

## Week 6 — Chapter 5: Collisions Transfer Energy
- **TG lessons:** L10 Collisions Transfer Energy · L11 Investigating Collisions (2 sessions)
- **Reader pages:** 25–30 · **AP:** 10.1, 11.1, 11.2
- **Big Questions:** *How is energy involved in collisions? What happens when objects collide? How are collisions predictable?*
- **Core concepts:** collisions transfer energy and change motion; some energy becomes sound/heat; collisions are predictable (same setup → predictable outcome).
- **Vocabulary:** [CORE] collide, collision; revisit energy transfer, energy of motion.
- **Lab/activity:** Lab 7 Crash Lab — isolate variables: Part A same car/different ramp heights; Part B same ramp/different car mass; primary outcome = target displacement; Newton's cradle as demo/phenomenon only [CORE].
- **Checkpoint:** AP 10.1, AP 11.1 (predictions), AP 11.2 (observations).
- **Friday Flex:** "Collision Predictor" · Newton's cradle demo discussion · catch-up.

## Week 7 — Chapter 6 Part 1: Energy Transformations + Engineering Concepts
- **TG lessons:** L12 How Can Energy Transformations Solve Problems?
- **Reader pages:** 31–36 · **AP:** 12.1
- **Big Question:** *How do energy transformations help people?*
- **Core concepts:** transformation = energy changing form; devices convert one form to another (toaster, solar panel); transformation + transfer often occur together.
- **Vocabulary:** [CORE] convert, conversion, energy transformation; revisit energy transfer; introduce engineering design, design problem, design solution (Language of Instruction).
- **Lab/activity:** energy-transformation device survey [CORE]; capstone pre-briefing [CORE].
- **Checkpoint:** AP 12.1; mid-unit checkpoint (Ch.4–5 spiral review).
- **Friday Flex:** "Design Repair Shop" · device-conversion matching · enrichment.

## Week 8 — Chapter 6 Part 2: Engineering Design / Build / Test / Refine
- **TG lessons:** L13 Designing Devices to Transform Energy (4 sessions)
- **Reader pages:** 31–36 · **AP:** 13.1–13.4
- **Big Question:** *How can I design a device that transforms energy to solve a problem?*
- **Core concepts:** engineering design process; criteria and constraints; test → evidence → redesign → retest.
- **Vocabulary:** [CORE] engineering design, criteria, constraints, model; revisit convert/transformation.
- **Lab/activity:** **Engineering Capstone** (4-day, tabletop alert/signal/motion device; indoor-reliable; not weather-dependent) — see `ENGINEERING_CAPSTONE.md` [CORE].
- **Checkpoint:** **Week 8 = performance assessment** (AP 13.1 proposal, 13.2 test results, 13.3 presentation plan, 13.4 scoring guide).
- **Friday Flex:** presentation polish · reflection · extension challenge.

## Week 9 — Chapter 7 + Synthesis + Written Assessment
- **TG lessons:** Unit Review (Ch.7 Thomas A. Edison) + Unit Assessment
- **Reader pages:** 37–42 (Ch.7) · **AP:** 13.5, 13.6, UR.1
- **Big Questions:** *How did Thomas Edison use his knowledge of energy transfer and transformation to solve problems? What have I learned about energy?*
- **Core concepts:** synthesis of transfer + transformation; engineering as iterative problem-solving; cumulative review.
- **Vocabulary:** full cumulative Core Vocabulary review (AP 13.5 crossword, 13.6 review).
- **Lab/activity:** cumulative review; no new labs [CORE].
- **Checkpoint:** **Week 9 = written summative unit assessment** (see `ASSESSMENT_BLUEPRINT.md`).
- **Friday Flex:** celebration/review games · assessment corrections · absent recovery.

## Notes
- **TG pacing:** CKSci recommends ≤20 days; this project expands to 9 weeks because Friday is non-core and labs are enriched. Each TG Core Lesson is 30–45 min.
- **Part E note:** CKSci maps Ch.7 (Edison) under "Unit Review," not as Lesson 13. This plan keeps Ch.7 as Week 9 synthesis + written assessment.
