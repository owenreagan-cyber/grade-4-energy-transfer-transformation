## Known review findings (do not rediscover / accidentally undo)

### CH1 — Energy Detective
- KEEP / LIGHT REVISE
- Replace `proof`/`proves` language with `evidence`.
- Avoid "Evidence — The Proof"; avoid "active energy".
- Use: `ENERGY → CHANGE → EVIDENCE`.

### CH2 — Moving Objects Have Energy
- STRONG KEEP / LIGHT REVISE
- Ensure separate title-only slide and separate Big-Question-only slide.
- Avoid premature transfer terminology where not needed.
- Use: `moving objects have energy of motion`.

### CH3 — Speed and Energy
- STRONG KEEP / LIGHT REVISE
- Emphasize **SAME OBJECT**.
- Measured: ramp height, cup displacement. Inferred: relative speed, relative energy of motion.
- No fake direct speed measurements. Avoid "push" wording if release is gravity-only.

### CH4 — Energy Transfer Pathways
- STRONG KEEP / LIGHT REVISE
- Replace `proof` with `evidence`; ensure transfer/transformation/BOTH are clear.
- Enlarge/remove tiny footer/disclaimer text. Correct grammar (`transfers and transforms`).

### CH5 — Collision Energy Transfer
- KEEP / MODERATE REVISE
- No clay dent depth as core evidence. Use target displacement / motion change / qualitative sound.
- Do not claim measurable collision heat. Keep same-object speed condition distinct from any mass comparison.

### CH6 — Energy Transformation Blueprints
- STRONG KEEP / LIGHT-MODERATE REVISE
- Avoid requiring chemical/mechanical terminology. Use `stored energy` where appropriate.
- Keep transfer / transformation / BOTH. Preserve criteria, constraints, engineering cycle.

### CH7 — Engineering Energy / Edison
- STRONG KEEP / LIGHT REVISE
- Use official title: `Solving Problems and Designing Solutions: Thomas A. Edison`.

### Unit 2 written assessment
- KEEP STRUCTURE / MODERATE REBUILD
- Remove Base-Load Power from required vocab. Replace scrubber-heavy item unless source-required.

### Unit 2 resource matrix / scenarios
- KEEP CONCEPT / MODERATE REVISE
- Simplify Grade 4 terminology. Avoid: base-load, peak demand, grid stabilization, high energy density, split atoms, zero emissions, storeable.
- Prefer: steady electricity · available when needed · no fuel burned during operation · depends on wind/sunlight · suitable location needed.
- Scenario wording: "identify a reasonable resource to investigate" — NOT "best energy solution".

### Fast & Curious
