2. Core model must remain **ENERGY → CHANGE → EVIDENCE**, not ENERGY → ACTION/CHANGE → EVIDENCE.
3. "No Active Energy" language should not be adopted.
4. Chapter 1 should not teach **transformation** formally (transformation is the Ch.6 focus).
5. "Motion energy" should not replace the project's preferred Chapter 2 term **"energy of motion."**
6. Pencil-sharpener heat should not be claimed unless actually observed (avoid invented/unverified heat claims).
7. Notebook pieces must follow the released Interactive Science Notebook system (3 inserts, dashed-line + "FOLD HERE" convention) rather than reverting to excessive cut/glue.
8. Supplemental slide content does not override released Chapter 1 curriculum.
