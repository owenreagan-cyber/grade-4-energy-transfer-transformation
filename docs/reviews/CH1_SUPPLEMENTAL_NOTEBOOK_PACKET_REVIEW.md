# Chapter 1 Supplemental Notebook Packet Review

> Review of `assets/supplemental/review/notebooks/ch1-interactive-notebook-packet.pdf` (7 pages: 6 inserts + pacing guide) against `docs/planning/INTERACTIVE_SCIENCE_NOTEBOOK_STANDARD.md` and the released Chapter 1 ISN.
> **Class:** SUPPLEMENTAL / REVIEW. Does NOT replace the released Chapter 1 notebook.

## Overall verdict
Contains genuinely useful ideas, but 6 inserts is too many for core Chapter 1 and several inserts leak later-chapter / out-of-scope language. Salvage specific ideas; do NOT adopt wholesale.

## High-value ideas to preserve
1. **ENERGY → CHANGE → EVIDENCE visual anchor** (Insert 2) — the cleanest single-page expression of the unit model. ✅
2. **Observation vs explanation rule** (Insert 2 "Energy Detective Thinking Rule") — "What did I actually see/hear/feel?" vs "What does it mean?" — excellent, directly supports the "students observe change, infer energy" guardrail. ✅
3. **Stored Energy lift-the-flap foldable** (Insert 4) — 3 flaps (battery / rubber band / raised object) with "future change" reveal; a strong, low-prep interactive element. ✅
4. **Drawing/memory clues** (Insert 1, Insert 3) — fill-in sentence frame + sketch; aligns with the notebook system's drawing-as-intentional-core feature. ✅
5. **Scavenger-hunt lab log** (Insert 5) — NOTICE → EVIDENCE → EXPLAIN → CONNECT structure; reusable scaffold. ✅

## Concerns (per the locked notebook standard)
| Concern | Detail | Disposition |
|---|---|---|
| 6 inserts too many | core Ch.1 uses 3; 6 would overload composition-book time | trim to 3 for core |
| "Evidence is proof" (Insert 1) | evidence ≠ proof (project standard) | reword to "evidence is an observation that supports an idea" |
| formal CER too early (Insert 6) | "prove energy was involved" + full CER — Ch.1 does NOT require formal CER | drop/simplify for Ch.1; defer CER to Ch.4+ |
| "Forms of Energy" page redundant (Insert 3) | overlaps core vocab; lists sound/heat/electrical which leak Ch.4 | keep only if it supports, not introduces, later content |
| motion-energy leak (Insert 3) | "MOTION ENERGY — energy in moving objects" leaks Ch.2 | defer to Ch.2 |
| sound/vibration leak (Insert 3) | "SOUND ENERGY … (vibrations)" leaks Ch.4 particle language | defer to Ch.4 |
| electrical plug imagery (Insert 3) | "Sketch an electrical plug, wire, or battery" — wall-outlet imagery conflicts with the low-voltage safety lock | replace with battery-only imagery |
| trim/glue standard | must match project dashed-line + "FOLD HERE" convention | conform to project standard |

## Decision
- **DO NOT replace the released Chapter 1 notebook.**
- Salvage: the ENERGY → CHANGE → EVIDENCE visual, observation-vs-explanation rule, Stored Energy foldable, and drawing/memory-clue frames — all already represented in the released Ch.1 ISN (which uses 3 inserts). The packet's extra inserts are **candidate FLEX/EXTENSION** material, not core.
