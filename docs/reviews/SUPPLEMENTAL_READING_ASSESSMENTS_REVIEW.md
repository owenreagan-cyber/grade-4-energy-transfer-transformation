# Supplemental Reading Assessments Review

> Review of `assets/supplemental/review/assessments/chapter-reading-worksheet-assessments.pdf` (15 pages: Chapters 1–7 reading comprehension/assessment worksheets + teacher answer key).
> **Class:** SUPPLEMENTAL / REVIEW. Content here does NOT replace released Chapter 1–4 quizzes or the Chapter 5 architecture.

## Overall verdict
Useful as an **idea bank for reading-comprehension questions**, but it repeatedly uses out-of-scope / advanced / contradicted language. Do NOT adopt wholesale. Salvage only source-safe question ideas.

## Salvageable (source-safe) ideas
- **Before/After evidence model** (Ch.1 Part C, Ch.2 Part C cause-effect table) — aligns with the released ENERGY → CHANGE → EVIDENCE model.
- **Stored-energy discrimination** ("sitting battery / stretched band / book on shelf = all stored energy") — good, source-aligned.
- **Multiple-choice structure with a deliberate distractor** — reusable format.
- **Real-world scenario (Ch.4 Part D: cold spoon in hot soup)** — matches the heat-transfer evidence idea (would need reword to "temperature change", avoid "flow").
- **Cause-effect table scaffold** (Ch.2 Part C) — reusable for Ch.5 collision predictions.

## What NOT to reuse
- Any item with m/s speed numbers, mm crater depth, force, deformation, contact force, "air particles," "vibrates as waves," "proof."
- Ch.6/Ch.7 content until those chapters are built (and only after historical verification).

## Decision
- **DO NOT replace released Ch.1–4 quizzes.** Salvage only the listed source-safe ideas later, with `SOURCE`/`ADAPTED`/`CUSTOM` tags, after removing the MAJOR-flag language.
