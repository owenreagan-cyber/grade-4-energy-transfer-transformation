## Science wording rules (locked)
- "Energy is the ability to cause change."
- "A change can provide evidence that energy is involved."
- "Stored energy can cause change later."
- NOT used: evidence=proof, mechanical/radiant energy, electromagnetic spectrum, electron movement, potential/kinetic energy.
