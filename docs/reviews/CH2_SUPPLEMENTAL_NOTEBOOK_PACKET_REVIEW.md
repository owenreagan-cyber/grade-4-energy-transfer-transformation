> Review of `assets/supplemental/review/notebooks/ch2-interactive-notebook-packet.pdf` (7 pages, 15,861 bytes). Source: `~/Downloads/ch2-interactive-notebook-packet.pdf`. **DO NOT REPLACE the released Chapter 2 ISN.**

## What is useful (salvageable ideas)
- **Visual vocabulary layout** — one anchor page per term with definition + "memory clue sketch frame" (motion; energy of motion).
- **Motion / energy-of-motion definitions** — sentence frames "Motion is the process of an object changing ____ over time"; "energy of motion is the energy an object possesses while it is ____."
- **Before-and-after diagrams** — two-box cause/effect drawing frames (shooter marble before → target marble after).
- **Cause/effect visual scaffolds** — MOTION → CHANGE → EVIDENCE model with phenomenon practice boxes (raindrop→puddle, basketball→net, hammer→nail).
- **Student drawing frames** — bounded sketch boxes ("draw a soccer ball changing position A→B").
- **Lab-evidence visual structure** — data-table scaffold for target displacement + sound/motion observation columns.

## What is superseded / rejected (do NOT adopt)
- **Six core inserts is too many** — released Ch.2 ISN is 3 inserts; 6 over-loads the notebook.
- **Packet explicitly follows the superseded ISN standard** — "80/20 Assembly Rule" + 3–5 min per-entry caps are from the old standard.
- **Active Motion Energy vs Stored Energy sorting pocket** — blurs the Ch.1/Ch.2 boundary (stored energy is Ch.1 content; Ch.2 is motion only).
- **Heavy emphasis on collision terminology** — "Marble Collisions" lab + collision framing belongs to Ch.5, not Ch.2.
- **Trial design that varies too much** — slow/fast **and** glass/steel shooter **and** mass and speed in one table (multi-variable confound; violates one-variable-at-a-time).
- **"proof/proves" language** — "quantitative measurement **proves** energy was transferred", "**proves** that all moving objects carry energy of motion". Project standard is evidence **supports/shows**, never proves.
- **Formal CER too early** — full Claim-Evidence-Reasoning + "Mastery Badge" is premature for Ch.2.
- **Steel ball bearing / heavy-shooter structure** — heavy-shooter material is not in the released Ch.2 lab (identical-marble control).
- **Direct conflict with released Ch.2 architecture** — released Ch.2 = moving object causes change only (no collisions, no stored-energy sort, no CER).

## Verdict
- **DO NOT REPLACE** released Chapter 2 ISN (`production/student-notebook/chapter-02/02-ch2-interactive-notebook.docx`).
- Released Ch.2 ISN remains **authoritative** for project production.
- Salvageable visual-scaffold ideas may inspire future notebook work, but no packet page is adopted as-is.
