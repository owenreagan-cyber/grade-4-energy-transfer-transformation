**How are energy, change, and movement of objects related?**

## 3. Key concept(s)

1. **A moving object can cause a change** (it bumps, knocks, pushes, or turns something).
2. **All moving objects have energy of motion** — energy is the ability to cause change, and a moving object causes change.
3. **The change is the evidence**: we know a moving object has energy because we observe the change it causes (we do not see the energy itself).
4. **People use the energy of motion** to do work — a swinging hammer drives a nail, a swinging pendulum turns clock gears, and moving air (wind) spins turbine blades.

## 5. Application scenario

- **Marble collision** — "A moving marble rolls into a still marble. The still marble starts to move." The observed change (still → moving) is evidence that the moving marble had energy of motion. Reinforces observation-as-evidence (we see the change, not the energy), with no speed/distance measurement (Ch3) and no transfer/transformation language (Ch4–6).
