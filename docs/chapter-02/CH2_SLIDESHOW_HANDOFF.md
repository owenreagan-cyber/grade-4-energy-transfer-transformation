## 7. Content intentionally NOT on the permanent notes

- No second BQ banner (TG L3 has only the one BQ).
- No kinetic / potential / mechanical / radiant / gravitational potential / momentum / inertia / compression wave.
- No speed–energy comparison (Chapter 3 scope) — "speed"/"fast" is **not** a required term here.
- No transfer/transformation classification language (Chapters 4–6 scope).
- Use **"evidence"**, never "proof/proves/prove".
- The Marble Collision demo is observed for **change = evidence** only — do NOT drift into
  Chapter 5 collision-transfer or Chapter 3 speed/mass variables.
- Lesson activities (marble collision demo, AP 3.1 "On the Move" before/after diagram) are
  **oral/demo/application**, not permanent notes content beyond the N2 OBSERVE box.
