## Design rules

- **30-minute block.** Every session plans **≤ 25 required minutes** so about 5 minutes remain for transitions, materials, questions, and cleanup.
- **Two sessions only:** Wed 10/14 · Thu 10/15. Monday, Tuesday, and Friday of Week 1 belong to Chapter 1. Chapter 2 does not borrow Friday.
- **Friday policy:** Friday may hold catch-up, review, synthesis, a quiz, makeup, or optional enrichment. It is never the first exposure to essential new core content. Chapter 2 vocabulary and models are taught Wednesday and Thursday. Friday 10/16 is the Chapter 1 quiz, not a Chapter 2 lesson.
- **No session starts on a reveal slide.** Session 1 opens on slide 1. Session 2 opens on slide 2 (Big Question re-entry).
- **Notes pages:** p. 1 (BQ, V1, N1) and the V2 line at the top of p. 2 → Session 1. The rest of p. 2 (N2) and p. 3 (N3, CHECK, BQ return) → Session 2.
- **Quiz slot:** Thu 10/15 · Session 2 · quiz in students' hands at minute 12 · 8 items · 12 pts · the full 12 minutes are protected · TEACHER CHOICE: OPEN NOTE OR CLOSED NOTE.
- Practice page: ungraded, NOT on any session's critical path. Assign after Session 1 as take-home. Recommended before the Thursday quiz.
- Moving Marbles and the Chapter 2 Friday FLEX bank are optional enrichment after the quiz. They are not Week 1 Friday. That Friday is Chapter 1.
- **Optional review slides 28–37** stay outside both required blocks.

**If short on time:**

- Skip slide 26 (already optional). It is not part of the required 24 minutes.
- If the opening runs long, shorten the N3 read-aloud (slide 21) to 30 seconds and read the CHECK sample (slide 25) in 20 seconds. Saves about 1 minute.
- Do not skip N2, N3, CHECK, or the Big Question return. Keep that writing in class. Do not shorten the 12-minute quiz.
- A 30-second recall of yesterday's marble is inside slide 19. One reminder roll is enough. It is not a second investigation.

### Thursday fallback — protects the quiz start

Thursday plans 24 required minutes. A normal transition loss leaves the quiz start protected by yielding synthesis, not new content.

| Priority | Task | Slides | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | N2 model, write, draw, reveal | 17, 18, 19, 20 | required in class, including a 30-second marble recall |
| 2 | N3 three useful changes | 21, 22, 23 | required in class |
| 3 | CHECK YOUR THINKING | 24, 25 | required in class, before the quiz |
| 4 | BQ NOW I THINK | 27 | required in class, before the quiz |

- Planned handout: minute 12, after 12 minutes of instruction. The quiz then uses minutes 12-24.
- The 12 quiz minutes include handing the quiz out, student work, and collection. Do not shorten them.
- If the opening runs long, shorten only the N3 read-aloud and the CHECK sample. N2, N3 writing, CHECK writing, and the Big Question stay in class.
- A full 5-minute late start is absorbed by the 6-minute buffer only if those two read-alouds are shortened. Do not move the quiz to Friday.

## What is not in either block

- Review slides 28–37 (five question/answer pairs): enrichment only, after the quiz.
- Slide 26 (which change shows the energy of motion): optional. Skip it when the quiz needs the time.
- Moving Marbles: optional enrichment after the quiz. It is not Thursday's lesson and not Week 1 Friday.
- The Marble Collision on Wednesday is a mini demonstration. It is not a second investigation and it does not measure speed.
