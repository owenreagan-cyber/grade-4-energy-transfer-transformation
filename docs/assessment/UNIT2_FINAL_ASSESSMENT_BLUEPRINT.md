### FINAL DEFINITION ORDER

A. A gas fossil fuel, mostly methane, found underground. *(EXTRA — Natural Gas)*
B. A machine that changes motion into electricity. *(Generator)*
C. A resource that can be restored more quickly than it is used up. *(Renewable Resource)*
D. A fuel formed from the remains of organisms that lived long ago. *(Fossil Fuel)*
E. Energy produced by heat that is transferred from inside Earth. *(Geothermal Energy)*
F. A nonrenewable material mined from rock, used for nuclear energy. *(EXTRA — Uranium)*
G. A machine with blades that spin when wind, water, or steam pushes them. *(Turbine)*
H. A solid fossil fuel formed from plants in wet, swampy places. *(EXTRA — Coal)*
I. A resource that cannot be restored as quickly as it is used. *(Nonrenewable Resource)*
J. A device that converts sunlight to electricity. *(Solar Cell)*
K. Electricity generated by the energy in moving water, usually through a dam. *(Hydroelectric Power)*
