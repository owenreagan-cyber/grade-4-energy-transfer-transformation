## Non-negotiables applied
- **Friday policy:** no first exposure of essential new core content on Friday. Ch1 quiz Fri (10/16) and the Unit-1
  summative Fri (11/6) are scored assessments, not new content — permitted.
- **30-minute inclusive block:** every lesson includes classroom transition, Reader handling, lab prep/cleanup, and
  departure to specials (see `production/final-8.5-week/TEACHER_PREP_GUIDE.md` for minute-level detail).
- **Guided Notes = primary student artifact; Science Journal = secondary.**
- **Transfer (moves) vs transformation (changes form): allow BOTH, never conflate.** Same object at different
  speeds for the speed–energy relationship.
