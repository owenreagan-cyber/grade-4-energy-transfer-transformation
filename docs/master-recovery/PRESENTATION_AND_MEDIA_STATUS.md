## Media standard (expanded)
- Allowed: project-created, live demos, educational YouTube, PBS/NASA/Smithsonian and other credible publishers,
  licensed/permission-authorized external media.
- Duration: 15–60 s quick phenomena · 1–2 min focused explanation · 3–5 min substantial · longer only with
  instructional justification.
- A media slide must: introduce its video, include a **Find Out / Follow Along** question, state a
  discussion/observation purpose.
- Track: publisher, URL, transcript, timestamps, science accuracy, rights, embed permissions, teaching position,
  fallback, approval status. **Do not download external copyrighted media without authorization.**
