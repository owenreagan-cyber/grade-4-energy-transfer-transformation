# UNIT 1 PRODUCT STANDARD

> Unit-wide product standard. Consolidated from established decisions only (no new curriculum decisions).

## Scope
- Grade 4, CKSci-aligned, "Energy Transfer and Transformation," ~9 weeks.
- **30-minute science blocks**, M–Th core + Friday (FLEX).
- **7 chapters** — exact Student Reader titles; Reader chapter ≠ TG lesson. Never invent/substitute chapters.

## Authorities
- **Canonical Reader:** `assets/authoritative/student-reader/complete/Science 4 - Energy Transfer - Student Book.pdf`.
- **Canonical Teacher Guide:** `assets/authoritative/teacher-guide/`.
- Authoritative files are never edited in place or renamed.

## Terminology (locked)
- **Core Vocabulary = 14 terms** (exact defs + Reader pages in `docs/planning/VOCABULARY_PROGRESSION.md`).
- **NOT required:** kinetic energy, potential energy, gravitational potential energy, mechanical energy, radiant energy, compression wave, inertia.
- Vocabulary is never drilled in isolation; assessed in context.

## Conceptual locks
- Transfer = energy moves (place/object); transformation = energy changes form; **BOTH allowed** — no forced either/or.
- Speed–energy = **same object** at different speeds (never mass + speed confound).
- Evidence ≠ proof ("supports/shows," not "proves").
- Observation vs inference always distinguished.

## Artifact hierarchy
- **Guided Notes** = primary student content/reference artifact.
- **Science Journal** = secondary (reflection, drawing, evidence, extension).
- Retired: Interactive Science Notebook (INB) and legacy `student-notes/` are archived/superseded by premium guided notes.

## Tagging (required on every resource)
- **Priority:** `CORE` / `FLEX` / `EXTENSION`.
- **Provenance:** `SOURCE` / `ADAPTED` / `CUSTOM` / `EXTENSION`.

## Production
- Editable-first: PPTX/DOCX/Markdown masters; PDF = release export only.

- Release status vocabulary: `DRAFT` → `QA` → `ACCEPTED` → `RELEASED`.

- No "complete" claim without passing gates in `docs/planning/QA_GATES.md` (plus phase-added `data-quality` and `misconception` audits).
