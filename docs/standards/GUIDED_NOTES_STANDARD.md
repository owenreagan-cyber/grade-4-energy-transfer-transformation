## Purpose
- Notes are a **scaffold for thinking**, not a copy of the Reader.
- They capture student thinking, evidence, and models.

## Page structure (established)
- Default page includes: Big Question (top), 3–5 essential ideas (fill-in), current-chapter vocabulary, one visual model, one diagram/energy-flow chain, example/non-example, conclusion/evidence box, and a cumulative retrieval question.
- Scaffold labels (not Reader headings): KEY WORDS / MODEL IT / DRAW-LABEL / CHECK YOUR THINKING / CONNECT.
- **Per-section Reader page refs** are added as labels; scaffolds are kept (RD-6 / §F).

## Density (RD-6 — locked)
- Keep current note-cue density; do **not** add cues for symmetry. Cue count reflects content, not neglect.

## Qualities
- Concise · writable · visually spacious (Grade 4 handwriting space) · useful during review.
- Aligned to slides, quiz, and final (same Big Question, vocabulary, model).

## Non-negotiables
- Grade 4 terminology only.
- Distinguish observation from inference.
- One model/diagram minimum per page.
- Generous writing space; grayscale-safe; no answer leakage.
